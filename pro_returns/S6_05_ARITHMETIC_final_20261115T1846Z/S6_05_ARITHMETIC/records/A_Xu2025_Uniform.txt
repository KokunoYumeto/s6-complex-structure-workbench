                                                 case of dihedral Maass newforms.




                                                                                    1. Introduction
                                             One of the important problems in the theory of L-functions is to ask for good upper
                                          bounds on the critical line which are stronger than the ones obtained by the Phragmen–
                                          Lindelöf principle. Starting with the work from Weyl [51], many considerable results have
                                          been achieved (see [7], [47], [39], [26], [13], [40], [35], [41], [42], [44], [2], [16], etc). In
