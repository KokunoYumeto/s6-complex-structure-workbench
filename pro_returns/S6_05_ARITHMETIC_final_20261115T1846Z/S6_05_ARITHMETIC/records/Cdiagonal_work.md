# C.7.9: diagonal analytic-subgroup and functional-transcendence audit

The fixed semiabelian extension attached to a nontorsion elliptic class q has endomorphism ring Z, even for a CM elliptic quotient. Functoriality requires alpha^*q=nq; in principal-polarization coordinates this is (alpha^dagger−n)P=0. Every nonzero elliptic endomorphism is an isogeny. The remaining difference of two lifts factors through Hom(E,Gm)=0. All conventions and this uniqueness step are displayed in the manuscript.

For E361, y²+y=x³−38x+90, P=(0,9), doubling gives Q=(4,−2). The exact division identity gives N(Q)=19^(1/4)N(P)^4. Thus the reduction to a real identity-component point preserves algebraicity. In the algebraic differential dx/(2y+1), wp′(u)=−3. The branch 0<u<omega1/2 makes f_u(u)>0. Under the hypothetical norm-algebraicity assumption the compact lift uses positive r=3*N(Q)²/sqrt(19), so v=log(r/f_u(u)) is real and exactly v=c*u for c=zeta(u)−eta1*u/omega1.

The slope is unconditionally transcendental. The line already contains the nonzero period (omega1,c*omega1). Dividing that period by an integer yields a nonzero algebraic torsion point. If c were algebraic, the analytic subgroup theorem would give an elliptic subgroup projecting isogenously to E, forcing the extension class to be torsion. The exact period-division step was independently checked in the attached Huber–Wüstholz primary source, Chapter 6, immediately following Theorem 6.1 and in the proof of its annihilator refinement. This is stronger than merely saying that c is not known algebraic. The actual compact line fails the required algebraic-Lie-subspace hypothesis.

The analytic Ax–Schanuel route was checked using Junjiro Noguchi, *Analytic Ax–Schanuel for semi-abelian varieties and Nevanlinna theory*, Journal of the Mathematical Society of Japan 76(1) (2024), 1–22, DOI 10.2969/jmsj/89588958. The full author manuscript is https://www.ms.u-tokyo.ac.jp/~noguchi/An-axsch.pdf; source and extracted text are preserved in C_Noguchi2024_author.pdf/txt, with fetching code and output C7_noguchi_fetch.py/out. Definition 1.2, Theorem 1.3, and the complete Section 3 proof were opened. Journal metadata was independently opened at https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jmath/76/1/76_1/_article/-char/en .

For f(z)=(z,c*z), nondegeneracy holds: a proper connected algebraic subgroup projecting onto E would split the extension after an isogeny, and the vertical torus cannot contain the nonconstant elliptic projection. The theorem gives graph dimension at least 3, while containment in G_Q times the complex line gives at most 3. Equality is therefore exact. A single algebraic value at z=u, or its integer multiples [n]R, causes no functional dimension defect. The remaining missing implication is an arithmetic specialization result or an additional functional identity derived from one metric value. Neither follows from the checked theorem. The endomorphism computation prevents adding noninteger CM directions inside this fixed extension.

This does not prove or disprove algebraicity of the real norm. The C.7.9 statement and the functional audit are additions to the exact obstruction record, not a claimed solution of C.7(c).
