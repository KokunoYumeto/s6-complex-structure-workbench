# Complexified Gaussian norm: C.7.11

Date: 15 November 2026. This is the direct two-variable attack on the antiholomorphic factor requested by root at 17:14 UTC.

## Exact results

On the conductor-361 model, with Q=(4,-2), beta=i sqrt(19), s2=-2, k=pi/A and real u, define F(z,w)=sqrt(19) theta(z) theta(w) exp(-kzw), H=F^2. The manuscript proves global paired-lattice periodicity, F(z,bar z)=N(z)^2, and the rank-four additive subgroup Gamma=Z[beta]*(u,bar u)+{(omega,bar omega):omega in Lambda}. Gamma lies in a real two-dimensional plane.

Under norm algebraicity, H, rather than just F, has all its orbit values in a single number field. The exact identity is H(alpha u,bar alpha u)=19^(1-Norm(alpha))*F_alpha(Q)*bar(F_alpha(Q))*H(u,u)^Norm(alpha). Squaring removes an otherwise unjustified assumption about all positive square roots lying in a fixed number field.

The algebraic direction V_alpha=alpha*d/dz-bar(alpha)*d/dw cancels the Gaussian and the unknown D(u) in the first logarithmic derivative. Its second logarithmic derivative equals an algebraic number plus 2*Norm(alpha)*k. We prove k transcendental by applying the analytic subgroup theorem to a period of the nontrivial universal vector extension if eta1/omega1=s2+k were algebraic. Therefore no algebraic-valued differential-stable family containing H exists at these points. At alpha=1 the exact ratio is V^2 H/H=4*k-8. This is the decisive failure of the attempted algebraic-jet bootstrap.

## Primary finite-order criteria checked

1. Waldschmidt (2009), Auxiliary functions in transcendental number theory, Ramanujan Journal 20(3), 341–373, DOI 10.1007/s11139-009-9204-y. Author manuscript https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~michel.waldschmidt/articles/pdf/SASTRAaftp.pdf. Theorems 3.6 and 3.7 require stability under all partial derivatives over the polynomial ring generated by the algebraic-valued family over one number field. The exact second jet violates this hypothesis under the norm-algebraicity assumption.

2. Waldschmidt (1975), Dimension algébrique de sous-groupes analytiques de variétés de groupe, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 25(1), 23–33, DOI 10.5802/aif.543. Full author scan https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~michel.waldschmidt/articles/pdf/Fourier.pdf. Theorem 1 on page 24 was read in text and visually; its complete proof on pages 25–27 was also read. It requires (d-1)*ell > sum(rho_j)+2*d*(n-1)*(lambda+1), in addition to common-field values, meromorphic denominator bounds, arithmetic-size bounds and separated points. For the independent triple wp(z), wp(w), H(z,w), n=2,d=3,ell=4,rho_j=2, the inequality would be 8>12+6*lambda. It fails even if all other conditions were granted with lambda=0. The actual paired subgroup is in a real plane, so elementary packing forces lambda>=1. No claim is made that this is the sharpest possible criterion or that all conceivable multivariable approaches fail.

## Main-question status

C.7(c) remains undecided. Complexification gives a valid algebraic-value set and a genuine rank increase, but it does not give the algebraic derivative data needed by the differential criteria, and it does not satisfy the displayed derivative-free criterion. The proof does not replace an algebraic-group map by a complex-linear Lie projector, and it does not silently identify a number field with a field containing the transcendental metric constant k.
