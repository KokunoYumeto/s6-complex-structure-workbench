chains of identifications. These observations give disjoint saturated neighbourhoods of any

core. Compactness of S supplies the uniform bound used above. This proves properness of
the joint map to (t, u).

t, and then impose xy = uϕ on the two line coordinates over the one-dimensional fibre of S.
Consequently t−t0, u−u0 is a regular sequence in every total local ring. The regular-sequence

smoothness of S. At u = 0 the node equation is ab = 0, and the two collar maps recover
precisely the specified pinching of the two sections of R.
