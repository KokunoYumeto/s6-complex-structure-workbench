(c) Finally the period-refined rank comparison (Waldschmidt, 1988a, Theorem 4.1, pp. 385–386,
and Corollary 4.3, p. 386) (Roy and Waldschmidt, 1993, main theorem,p.363) has coefficient
α(G) = 2 dim G −2d0(G) −d1(G), where the last terms count additive and multiplicative
quotients. Both vanish for GQ. An additive homomorphism vanishes on its torus and then
on the proper quotient E; a nonzero torus character extending to GQ would kill a nonzero
integer multiple of its nontorsion extension class. Thus α(GQ) = 4.
(d) The line ℓc has period rank one. A general period is (ω, ζ(u)ω −η(ω)u + 2πij). Writing
ω = aω1 + dω2, membership in ℓc gives du/ω1 ∈Z by Legendre’s relation. Since u/ω1 is
irrational, d = j = 0. The numerical rank bound in the cited theorem is (4−1) dim ℓc = 3. The
period and the logarithm (u, cu) are independent over Q and span rank two; the algebraicity
of the latter’s exponential remains exactly the point-algebraicity question in 26.14. These
explicit ranks leave that question open.
