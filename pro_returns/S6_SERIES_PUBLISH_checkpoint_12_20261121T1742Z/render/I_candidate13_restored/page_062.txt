Theorem 10.6 (The maximal domain of proper discontinuity). [tag 01RC]
The set O is the largest Γ-invariant open subset of D on which the action is properly
discontinuous. It is also the largest such open subset for PZ. Each sign region is a complex
manifold in the analytic topology, and its quotient by either discrete group is a Hausdorff complex
analytic orbifold.
