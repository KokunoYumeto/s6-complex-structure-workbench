4. Retract U to its real two-torus. The cellular cochain complex with coefficients in the local
system is the two-generator Koszul complex. Its last differential has image NqΛ + NrΛ, and
hence
H2(U, Λ) = Λ/(NqΛ + NrΛ) = Λ/(Z bw + Zbδ) ≃Zbγ ⊕Zbu.
The relation lattice is primitive: Nq already has image Z bw + Zbδ.
5. The analytic exponential sequence is
0 −→Λ −→O(V) −→T −→0.
The product of two punctured discs is Stein, and O(V) is coherent. Cartan’s theorem B
therefore kills its positive cohomology (Cartan, 1952, exposé 19, §5, pp. 14–15). The long
exact sequence gives the stated isomorphism H1(U, T ) ≃H2(U, Λ). The representatives of
H1(U, T ) give holomorphic torsors on U by translation gluing.

of a Cartier line bundle to its multiple of the flat cycle c in H2(U, Λ). Both common invariant
cycles lie in NqΛ, so this multiple is zero.
