Proof. 1. We use the semistable model 21.2. Remove disjoint open tubular neighbourhoods of
the two conductor sections from R+, and denote the remaining compact manifold by Z. Its
two boundary circle bundles are identified by the plumbing; let E denote the first. The Euler
class of E →S is n = m −F. It is primitive. The Thom sequence for (R+, Z) gives
H5(Z) = Z,
H4(Z) = 0,
H3(Z) = L/⟨F, m⟩≃Z8,
H2(Z) ≃Z10,
H1(Z) = 0.
If H is the projective-bundle class restricting to zero on s0 and to −m on s∞, and A is the
exceptional divisor class, the two restriction maps on H2(R+) are
(α, h, a) 7−→α + aF,
(α, h, a) 7−→α −hm.
Their combined image is primitive of rank twelve, and its cokernel is L/⟨F, m⟩. In degree two,
intersection with the two sections is onto Z2: a generic projective fibre meets both once, and
an exceptional projective fibre meets just the section containing the centre once. In degree
six the map is the diagonal Z →Z2. These are precisely the Thom maps giving the asserted
groups.
2. The Gysin sequence of the primitive circle bundle gives
H2(E) = n⊥⊂L,
H3(E) = L/Zn,
H1(E) = H4(E) = 0,
H5(E) = Z.
The smooth fibre is obtained from Z by the boundary mapping cylinder. In its Mayer–Vietoris
sequence the degree-two difference map is the normalization difference d+
2 restricted to n⊥.
In the coefficient coordinates (s, t, h) = (aF, av, am) the constraint is h = s, so this map
has primitive rank two and kernel K. The degree-three difference map is, modulo F, m,
(T∗−I)a = (aF)v, hence has primitive image Zv. These statements use the surjectivity of
the three intersection coordinates proved in 21.11. The degree-five difference is zero: the
plumbing reverses the normal circle orientation, and the two boundary fundamental classes
sum to zero in Z. The degree-zero difference is zero as well. Exactness now gives the claimed
groups and the displayed degree-three extension. Poincaré duality, or the same integral exact
sequence, confirms the absence of torsion in complementary degrees.
3. We also compute monodromy directly from this decomposition. In the neck xy = u, a positive
circuit of u rotates one boundary normal coordinate once relative to the other. A cycle in Z
is unchanged. A three-cycle whose cut-open boundary represents a ∈K acquires the tube
obtained by sweeping a once around that normal circle. The Gysin description sends this tube
to the class of a in H3(E) = L/Zn, and then to its class in L/⟨F, m, v⟩. This is exactly the
map in the statement. The same description in the other degrees is zero, using the groups and
difference maps just computed. A tube has no cut-open boundary, which proves (Tu −I)2 = 0.
4. Finally K ∩⟨F, m, v⟩= ZF, since the Gram matrix of m−F, v has determinant eleven. Hence
the rank is six. In the unimodular E8 summand, the primitive rank-two lattice spanned by
m −F, v has discriminant eleven; its orthogonal complement maps into the dual quotient with
cokernel of order eleven. Thus the nonzero Smith factors are five ones and eleven.
