Proposition 22.49 (Every origin component is a threefold). [tag 030V]
Suppose the partial family of 22.41 has a proper analytic completion f : Z →∆q × ∆r with
smooth pure four-dimensional total space, with r ◦f flat, and with its prescribed identification
over the punctured bidisc. The map to this fixed bidisc is assumed holomorphic. Then every
irreducible component of f−1(0, 0) has dimension three. In particular, there is no equidimensional
completion, and no completion having even one two-dimensional component of the origin fibre.
