Let α(q) = qmκ(q), where m ≥1 and κ is a holomorphic unit on a disc, and let β be
holomorphic with 0 < |β(0)| < 1. Shrink the disc so β is a unit and 0 < |α(q)| < |β(q)| < 1 for

Their holomorphic family over the punctured disc has no proper complex-analytic completion over
the full disc, even allowing singular or nonreduced central fibres and a singular total space. This
remains true after every finite base change ramified at zero.
In particular one cannot replace the blown-up Hopf fibres in 22.43 by the unblown family with
one multiplier κ(q)q →0 and the other tending to a nonzero contraction.
