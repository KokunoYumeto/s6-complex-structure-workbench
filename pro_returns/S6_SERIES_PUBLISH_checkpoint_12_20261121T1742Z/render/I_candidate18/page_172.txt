Proposition 21.13 (The pointwise nearby Euler function). [tag 02ZT]
For X ♭→∆u the nearby-fibre Euler function on the double locus of Q is zero off S∞and one
at every point of S∞, including its node. Its integral is one. The smooth part of S∞has Euler
number zero, so its integrated contribution is zero. For either semistable family X ± the nearby
Euler function is one off the double locus and zero on it, giving χ(X(u)) = 26 −2 · 12 = 2.
Proof. 1. Off the zero divisor of ϕ, the transverse nearby fibre of xy = uϕ is an annulus and
has Euler number zero. At a smooth zero, the local equation is xy = uz; for fixed u ̸= 0 one
solves for z, and the small local nearby space is contractible. Its Euler number is one. At
the node it is the quadratic cone xy = uzv, also contractible, with Euler number one. The
smooth part of the nodal elliptic curve is C∗and has Euler number zero; the node contributes
one. The core Q \ S is a C∗-bundle and contributes zero. This proves the first count.
2. In the resolved family the local map on the double locus is simply ab = u times two transverse
coordinates, including above the old node.
Its nearby annulus has Euler number zero
everywhere on that locus. Away from it, the family is a submersion and the local value is
one. Integrating gives the second count. If the nodal threefold in the first family is smoothed
rather than small-resolved, its ordinary-double-point Milnor fibre is homotopy equivalent to
S3 and has Euler number zero; the replacement changes 1 to 0, whereas the small resolution
changes 1 to 2. These agree with the intrinsic virtual number in 21.8.
Proposition 21.14 (Non-Kähler models and the monodromy weight filtration). [tag 02ZU]
The smooth fibres of these untwisted models have b1 = 1 and admit no Kähler metric. Moreover
the exceptional small-resolution curve in R± is null-homologous, so that normalization is not
Kähler either. The rational monodromy weight filtration, defined algebraically from N = Tu −I
and centred at degree three, has graded ranks 6, 2, 6 in weights 2, 3, 4.
Proof. 1. The odd first Betti number excludes a Kähler metric. In the generization matrix of
21.11 a global section of R2h∗Z has equal branch values, hence degree zero on the small
exceptional curve. All classes pulled back from R also have degree zero on it. The Leray
calculation exhausts H2(R±), so the curve has zero homology class; it cannot have positive
area for a Kähler form. Finally N2 = 0, rank N = 6, and dim H3 = 14, so the centred
filtration is W2 = im N, W3 = ker N, W4 = H3. Its graded dimensions are the asserted ones.
Proposition 21.15 (An infinite explicit subclass). [tag 02ZW]
Let Dm be the central fibre after the m maximal-cone star subdivisions in 21.1, starting with
Q+, where m ≥0. Then
π1(Dm) = Z,
H∗(Dm; Z) is free,
(b0, . . . , b6) = (1, 1, 9 + m, 7, 11 + m, 0, 1 + m).
and χ(Dm) = 14+3m. For m ≥1 its normalization is the disjoint union of Blp R+, m−1 copies
of Blpi P3, and a final P3. The original two sections are still glued by T. The new components

The nearby Euler function is one off the double locus and zero on it; its integral remains two.
The nearby integral monodromy remains exactly that of 21.12.
