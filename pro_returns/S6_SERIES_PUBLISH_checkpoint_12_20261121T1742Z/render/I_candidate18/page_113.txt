The integral circle quotient and its cusp replacement
Definition 16.1 (Circle-quotient conventions). [tag IP06] We write g, u, w, d for the ordered
homology basis (bγ, bu, bw, bδ) in this section, and γ for its first dual covector. The cusp coordinate
is q = 1/t. Positive target orientations are the complex orientation on the base followed by the
positive γ-circle orientation. The complex orientation on X fixes the source orientation.

Writing a = e2πiθ and choosing r with rκ ≡1 (mod m), the invariant
coordinates sar and am identify its quotient with D2 × S1. The base coordinate becomes
(sar)m(am)−r, and hence has multiplicity m at its central circle. Before the finite quotient
the map is a trivial TL-bundle. The free actions on source and target are equivariant for that
map, so it descends locally to a smooth TL-bundle. Properness follows from compactness of
TL. The logarithmic translation used to glue the root chart to J changes the character by its
γ-component. Applying γ to the gluing translation therefore gives the target gluing, proving
agreement on every overlap.
