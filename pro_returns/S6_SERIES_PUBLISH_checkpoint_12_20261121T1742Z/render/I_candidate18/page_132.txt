6. Each of the three labelled sections on the punctured disc belongs to a degree-one branch of

The normalization of a degree-d branch over a punctured disc has cyclic monodromy of

holomorphically to a map from the full disc to P1. Let these three maps be c1(s), c2(s), c0(s),

clearing powers of the base coordinate in its meromorphic coefficients. Its projection to Z is the
asserted proper projective modification, and its second projection is the required holomorphic
marked map. Resolve this reduced graph isomorphically over the smooth punctured family
by the same analytic resolution theorem. Composing its holomorphic marked map with the
resolution gives the asserted smooth proper modification.

Theorem 18.5 (Vanishing of horizontal meridians). [tag 02RG]
In a smooth resolved proper filling carrying the marked base map, every parameter-central
component dominating P1 has zero normal-meridian lattice class modulo Zbδ, relative to the
ordinary parameter origin.
