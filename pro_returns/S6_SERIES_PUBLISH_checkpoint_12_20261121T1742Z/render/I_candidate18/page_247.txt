14. The dual complex. All normalization pairs are now rational toric pairs. Every vertex link is one
circle and every edge has two incident faces, because each rational double curve has two triple
points. Thus the finite dual complex K is a connected closed topological surface. It is oriented:
the iterated residues of a trivialization of ωD are nonzero at every triple point, and the residue
theorem on each rational double curve makes its two coefficients opposite, with the gluing
sign for the two branches. These coefficients form a nowhere-zero two-cycle on K. Constancy
of coherent Euler characteristic in the proper flat analytic family (Bănică and Stănăşilă, 1976,
Chapter III, Theorem 4.12(iii), pp. 134–135), and the alternating normalization resolution,
