Proposition 19.18 (The supercritical level classification). [tag 032Q]
For every H > H∗, the degeneracy locus ZH is exactly one embedded C1 Jordan curve in
the finite t-plane. It is real analytic unless H = H0 = 3 log 48/(2π); at that height it is real
analytic away from t = 0 and has the regular crossing described in 19.15. There are no singular
coarse levels at any supercritical height. The bounded complementary disc contains t = 1 and has
Dc > 0; the complementary disc in P1, containing the cusp, has Dc < 0. The point t = 0 is in
the latter disc for H < H0, on the curve for H = H0, and in the former disc for H > H0.
The bounded discs are strictly nested as H increases, shrink to {1} as H ↓H∗, and exhaust
the finite plane as H →∞. Their small-height boundary shape is the expansion in 19.11. The
maximal open base locus of the complex manifold construction consists of these two complementary
discs. Approaching their common boundary, the covolume of the marked root torus lattice is
covol(ΠcZ4) = Im τ |Dc| −→0.
At the boundary the period map has rank three and the exact quotient and Hausdorff reflection
are those of 19.13.
