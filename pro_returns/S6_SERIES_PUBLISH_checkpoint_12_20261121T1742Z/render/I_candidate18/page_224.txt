Remark 22.40 (The two axial models and their overlap). [tag PT18] Proposition 22.39 uses the
rotating model R on q ̸= 0 and the reduced Mumford family on r ̸= 0. On their overlap the
exact isomorphism is ζ 7→ζ −σ + F, with F given by (22.11). The larger rotating model has
local equation q = z12 at its q-boundary for r ̸= 0, so every q-vertical divisorial valuation there
has order in 12Z. The reduced Mumford boundary has a divisor of q-order one. A common
proper modification over q would identify the function q and the associated divisorial valuations;
Proposition 22.11 gives the resulting contradiction. Thus the overlap isomorphism specifies
precisely the two-open gluing on B×. Its noncompletion at the missing origin is Proposition 22.54,
and the obstruction for the full global family is Theorem 18.6.
