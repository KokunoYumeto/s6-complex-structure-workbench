Proposition 22.45 (Exceptional-curve nonclosure and persistence in a semistable cap). [tag 030S]
Let K∗→∆s be the modified degree-one Kato family of 22.43, with q = s12, and let E →∆s
be its unchanged persistent elliptic axis. For s ̸= 0 let Cs ≃P1 be the exceptional curve of the
single Hopf blow-up.
The union of the Cs has no proper analytic divisor closure in K∗. Further finite proper
modifications cannot give these curves a proper analytic divisor closure: any such closure would
push forward to a forbidden one. In particular, the generic Hopf blowdown does not extend as a
relative proper holomorphic modification, or as a relative meromorphic contraction represented
by a proper analytic graph, across s = 0.

functions (Q, r), its divisor r = 0 is reduced normal crossings, and one component K contains a
neighbourhood of some Cs0, s0 ̸= 0, agreeing with the complementary Kato region and its ordinary
base coordinate Q. Suppose this curve is disjoint from every conductor branch of r = 0. Then
every sufficiently nearby fibre (Q, r) = (Q0, r0) contains an embedded rational curve. Consequently
such a cap cannot have the prescribed ordinary two-tori as its r ̸= 0 surface fibres.
