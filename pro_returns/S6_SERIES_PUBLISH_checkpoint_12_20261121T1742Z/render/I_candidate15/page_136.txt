Evaluation and graph resolution give a proper pluricanonical image. Over the field K of
meromorphic parameter germs, its image is a smooth rational normal curve. More explicitly,
the kernels of multiplication on the direct-image modules, tensored with K, give its quadratic
equations; on the punctured disc these are the Veronese equations and generate its ideal. The
three labelled critical-value curves give distinct K-rational points. The coprime multiplicities
are unequal, and the third point is the cusp.
5. Over an algebraic closure of K there is a unique rational parameter taking those points
to 0, 1, ∞. Uniqueness makes it invariant under every field automorphism over K, so its
coefficients belong to K. They are meromorphic germs with finite principal parts. Composing
and resolving the graph yields the marked base map required in 18.3. This explicitly recovers
a degree-one base parameter.
