Proposition 22.12 (Hodge theory of analytic unramified torsors). [tag 030C]
Let V →C be any compact smooth analytic genus-one surface locally isomorphic, as a torsor
over every base disc, to the Jacobian surface E →C of 22.8. Then b1(V ) = 6, and V is Kähler.
For every holomorphic line bundle M on V , the projective bundle PV (O ⊕M) is Kähler. Every
smooth compact threefold bimeromorphic to such a bundle satisfies the ∂¯∂-lemma.
Consequently none of these projective bundles or smooth compact bimeromorphic models can
be the non-∂¯∂normalization component required in 22.16.
Proof. 1. Write p : E →C for the projective Jacobian. The calculation in 22.8 gives R1p∗OE =
OC(−∞), and g(C) = 3.
The coherent Leray sequence therefore gives h1(E , OE ) = 3.
Projectivity and Hodge decomposition imply b1(E ) = 6. The topological Leray sequence with
rational coefficients is
0 −→H1(C; Q) −→H1(E ; Q) −→H0(C, R1p∗Q) −→H2(C; Q) −→H2(E ; Q).
The last arrow is injective because p has a section. The first arrow is an isomorphism of
six-dimensional vector spaces. Exactness thus implies H0(C, R1p∗Q) = 0.
2. The local torsor identifications differ by translations. On a smooth elliptic fibre translations
act trivially on H1. On the I12 fibre they preserve the orientation of the component polygon
and therefore act trivially on its one-dimensional H1 as well. These identifications commute
with the local specialization maps. Hence, for π : V →C, they glue to R1π∗Q ≃R1p∗Q
on the whole curve, including infinity.
The same Leray edge sequence for V now gives
H1(V ; Q) ≃H1(C; Q).
Thus b1(V ) = 6 is even.
Buchdahl’s theorem (Buchdahl, 1999,
Theorem 11, p.297) proves that V is Kähler.
3. Choose a Hermitian metric on O ⊕M. The induced metric on the tautological relatively
ample line of its projectivization has a closed real (1, 1) curvature form θ positive in the
vertical direction. If ω is a Kähler form on V , compactness implies that θ + Aπ∗ω is positive
in every direction for a sufficiently large constant A. It is closed, so it is a Kähler form on the
projective bundle. A smooth compact bimeromorphic model is therefore in Fujiki class C and
satisfies the ∂¯∂-lemma; the domination statement is recalled, with its precise reference to the
∂¯∂theorem, in (Chen, 2026, Section 2.1, p.8). Apply 22.16 for the final obstruction.
