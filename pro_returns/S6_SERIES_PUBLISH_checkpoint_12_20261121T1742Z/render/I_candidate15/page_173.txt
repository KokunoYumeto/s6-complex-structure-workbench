Theorem 21.16 (Euler number and fundamental group for every toroidal output). [tag 02ZX]
Let Σ be any output of 21.5 for u = rn, and let DΣ denote the underlying reduced central
space. Let k(Σ) be the number of selected pure top ray orbits, and let t4(Σ) be the number of
maximal four-cones in one fundamental prism. Then
χ(DΣ) = 11k(Σ) + t4(Σ),
π1(DΣ) = Z,
H1(DΣ; Z) = Z.
These assertions include nonreduced central divisors. At a point with precisely one local central
branch of multiplicity ℓ, the nearby Euler function is ℓ; at a point with at least two local branches
it is zero. Its integral is always two. For every reduced output the pure top count is k(Σ) = n, so
χ(DΣ) = 11n + t4(Σ). The full integral homology is given by Theorem 21.17.
