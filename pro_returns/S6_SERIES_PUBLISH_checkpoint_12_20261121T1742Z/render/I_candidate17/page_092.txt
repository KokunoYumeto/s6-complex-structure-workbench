Proof. 1. By 13.2, the Dolbeault image of A is d1g ̸= 0. The class B = A is nonzero, whereas its

subspace consists precisely of (z, ¯z).

H1(X, OX) = Cg. On a smooth trivial line with unit frame s, put ¯∂zs = −2πizG ⊗s.

Multiplication by i/(2π) proves (13.4). The two independent basis vectors prove injectivity,
additivity as real Lie groups and the assertion about the image.
3. The closed real form θ is exact since H2(X; R) = 0. It has zero harmonic projection. De
Rham Hodge decomposition, with dGd = Gdd, therefore gives dd∗Gdθ = θ. The Green operator
and d∗preserve reality, so α is real and dα = θ. The (0, 2) component yields ¯∂G0 = 0. To
prove nonvanishing, an equation G0 = ¯∂u would give
θ = ∂¯∂(u −¯u) = 2i∂¯∂Im u.
Semipositivity of θ would make Im u plurisubharmonic. The maximum principle on compact
connected X would make it constant, contradicting the nonzero pullback of ωFS on the regular
locus of f.
4. Since α = G0 + ¯G0, the Chern formula already proved sends exp([G0]) to [θ]BC, which is also
c1,BC(f∗O(1)). Its injectivity proves the stated line-bundle equality, with exactly the declared
2πi exponential and curvature conventions.
