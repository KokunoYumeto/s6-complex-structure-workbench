near Z. Locally this follows by lifting an embedding and its regular sequence of equations;

total space. The relative cotangent complex is consequently perfect of amplitude [−1, 0]. Its
dual defines a topological complex K-class V on that total space; local finite vector-bundle
complexes define the class by descent, or by stabilization and partitions of unity; the analytic-
to-topological comparison is given in (Levy, 2008, Section 3.1, Lemmas 3.1–3.2, pp. 588–590).
Its restriction to a smooth fibre is its tangent bundle, and its restriction to Z is its intrinsic
virtual tangent class.
