An embedded Tate interface at the missing corner
Proposition 22.42 (The other Kato axis). [tag 030P]
In the proper smooth Kato family K →∆s of 22.27 there is a second relative effective Cartier
divisor D ⊂K, disjoint from the persistent elliptic divisor E. The surface D is smooth and proper
over ∆s. Its general fibre and central fibre are
Ds = C∗/(κ(s)sn)Z
(s ̸= 0),
D0 = the reduced rational n-cycle.
At its nodes the relative local equation is uv = s. Its normal line in K is the multidegree-zero
Tate line whose factor on the closing collar is a(s). In particular it has degree zero on every
component of the central cycle. Scaling the first blowdown coordinate gives a holomorphic C∗
action on the pair (K, D) over the base.
For n = 12 this constructs an embedded proper copy of the cusp’s elliptic root interface, with
its degree-zero normal line, after choosing the units κ, a to be the Tate and normal multipliers.
In the additive period convention u = (τ, µ), W = e−2πiζ2, these multipliers are κ(s)s12 = e2πiτ
and a(s) = e−2πiµ. The elliptic translation by 6P is realized by first-coordinate scaling a(s)−6.
The comparison identifies the embedded Cartier divisor and its normal line. At each central
node the ambient base map has the local form s = uv −h computed below.

unmodified other Hopf axis, with the stated Tate multiplier and normal multiplier a(s).
