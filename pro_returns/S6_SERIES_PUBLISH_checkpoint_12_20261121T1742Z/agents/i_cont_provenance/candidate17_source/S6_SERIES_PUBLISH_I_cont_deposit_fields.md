## Paper I

### Title

The $(3,4,\infty)$ family of complex six-spheres. I. Periods, moduli and the two ends

### Abstract

We determine the periods, moduli and two limiting regimes of the $(3,4,\infty)$ family of complex six-spheres. A theta expression for its elliptic period gives the exact parameter disc $0<|w|<216$. Integral affine and cusp comparison maps classify the discrete data, parameter identifications and real structures, including the fixed three-spheres. We compute the integral monodromy group, its maximal domain of proper discontinuity and the algebraic dimension of every smooth fibre. Logarithmic direct images and explicit relative differential modules determine the Dolbeault, Bott–Chern, Aeppli and tangent cohomology, all Picard twists and the deformation parameter. Global Dolbeault representatives extend through the multiple fibres and cusp. At the puncture, the corner monodromy obstructs every proper Hausdorff analytic filling after any finite parameter cover; the analogous obstruction holds for the coprime higher-multiplicity sphere families. At the outer boundary, the intrinsic Albanese invariant obstructs continuation, while the dual period lattices determine the local Hausdorff reflection and all additive limits. The arithmetic norm satisfies exact division and height identities; we describe its torsion values, critical points and level curves.
