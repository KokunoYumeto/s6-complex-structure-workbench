# Primary-source audit: unrestricted reducible torus fillings

All sources below were opened in full on 15 November 2026. This is a scope audit, not an invocation of their conjectures and not a claim that the narrower torus question is known to be open. The general-type component question was unresolved when the first version of this audit was written. It is now settled in this manuscript by C.2.UN.15, using translation fields and single-valued meromorphic flows. C.2.UN.16 supplies two commuting fields on the actual root-cover component, and C.2.UN.19 proves that every original class VII normalization component has Hopf minimal model. An exhaustive description of all actual reducible analytic fillings remains a further task; the literature restrictions in the table remain exactly as stated.

## Exact consequences for this manuscript

UN4 proves that every fixed filling has zero-Euler vertical refinements. The strict transforms of its essential non-uniruled components retain their birational types. Therefore restricting to zero-Euler supports cannot itself remove an essential component type from the unrestricted filling problem.

UN3 proves that one punctured torus family has infinitely many pairwise non-bimeromorphic fillings, even after every finite base change. In particular its explicit Kodaira/Hopf filling cannot be compared meromorphically with the smooth product filling: inverse-map resolution would force its non-uniruled divisors to survive birationally, contrary to their calculated first homology. This disproves that particular missing comparison step. It does not claim that no other classification method can exist.

UN6 makes no priority claim for the existence of Kodaira-dimension-one components. The Rao–Tsai source gives the earlier original references precisely: Nishiguchi, MathAnn263(1983),p383; *Degeneration of surfaces with trivial canonical bundles*, ProcJapanAcad59(1983),304–307, Example5,Section6; Ueno1983; Fujimoto1988,Example3.4(1). The original four-page Nishiguchi1983 scan was not successfully retrieved in this audit, so its construction is not represented as independently checked.

UN13 was independently audited by the local-sheaves agent. Finite-quotient averaging gives its displayed rational and coherent invariants; the explicit exceptional Gysin sections split the two added rational degrees; formal functions on successive O_P2(2r) proves higher coherent direct-image vanishing; forty vertical balancing blocks give the stated final Betti numbers. Its Kummer K3 components are verified members of the unrestricted essential-component list.

The monodromy agent completed the general-type exclusion in C.2.UN.15. Its actual finite base change makes a chosen component reduced, and rescaled translation fields give semicomplete meromorphic vector fields there. Guillot's classification excludes general type. C.2.UN.16 strengthens this to a rank-two lattice with exact restriction kernel sL, yielding either a commuting meromorphic frame or an actual rational/elliptic first-integral fibration. In algebraic dimension zero, its covering minimal model is a torus or Hopf surface.

C.2.UN.19 then uses the specific cyclic stabilizer, rather than replacing a generically finite map by an asserted isomorphism. The quotient E/G is properly bimeromorphic to the original normalization. After equivariant contraction, a torus quotient has even first Betti number, while a cyclic Hopf quotient is smooth and Hopf by its determinant character, Lefschetz fixed-locus calculation, and the explicit local reflection quotient. This excludes every other class VII minimal type in the original filling. Root independently audited UN16 and UN19 in full. These statements still do not assert a generative classification of all reducible fillings.

## Rank-one elliptic-cover source audit

Fujimoto, *Logarithmic transformations on elliptic fiber spaces*, J. Math. Kyoto Univ. 28(1) (1988), 91–110, was read in full at <https://scispace.com/pdf/logarithmic-transformations-on-elliptic-fiber-spaces-1uinrvp8jj.pdf>. Example 3.4(1), printed p.99, applies the construction of Example 3.1 to a twisted principal bundle with one fixed elliptic fibre. Example 3.1 blows up the base and performs logarithmic transforms along the exceptional curves; on the principal central component these introduce multiple smooth elliptic fibres. Thus the resulting elliptic component has Euler characteristic zero and holomorphic Euler characteristic zero, by the actual elliptic fibration and its smooth multiple fibres. Its Kodaira dimension one does not furnish a positive-holomorphic-Euler-characteristic example. Example 3.4(3)'s K3/Enriques examples have K3/Enriques general fibre. The false necessity assertion in Theorem 2.8 is not used; its separate counterexample and exact quotient map are recorded by the deformations agent.

Fujimoto's *Logarithmic Transformations on Elliptic Fiber Spaces II—elliptic bundle case*, printed pp.81–102 in <https://eprints.lib.hokudai.ac.jp/repo/huscap/all/5450/16.pdf>, also starts with principal fixed-elliptic bundles. Its bibliography gives Ueno, *On non-Kähler degenerations of complex tori*, in *Recent Trends in Mathematics, Reinhardsbrunn 1982*, Teubner-Texte zur Mathematik 50 (1983), as a separate source. That contribution has not yet been retrieved in full here.
