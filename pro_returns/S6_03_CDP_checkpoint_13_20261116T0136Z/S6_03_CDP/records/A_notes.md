### Unequal multiplicities settled

The earlier transfer obstruction for reducible unequal-multiplicity fibres is now resolved by A.3.5, and is retained above only as a record of the earlier stage. Every component Si of a connected reducible support meets another Sj. The restricted canonical section of OY(Sj) cuts a nonzero Cartier divisor D on Si. For any fixed locally free E, the spaces H0(E(-nD)) form descending subspaces of H0(E) with zero intersection by Krull intersection and analytic continuation, so they vanish for large n. A torsion-killing power of OY(Sj) lies in Pic0(Y), because b2(Y)=0. Hence every Picard component has a twist giving zero ambient sections on Si. The component-addition Cartier filtration of the full fibre, including repetitions and unequal multiplicities, then gives a finite intersection of translated dense-open vanishings. Thus a full fibre with all-twist ambient persistence necessarily has irreducible support. On its uniform multiple mS, the graded twists transfer persistence to the reduced S, and A.5.4 applies.

A.5.6 records the more general vector-bundle version of the Gauduchon argument that led to this reduction. A line metric is adjusted to constant contracted Chern curvature by the Gauduchon scalar elliptic equation. The fixed bundle's curvature is bounded; Bochner at a positive maximum excludes a holomorphic section when the line degree is sufficiently negative. This gives a bound independent of the line's particular holomorphic structure. The elementary Cartier argument in A.3.5 is sufficient for reducible fibres, so that result does not rely on A.5.6.

A.5.6 has been strengthened to persistence on one Picard component under rational b2(Y)=0: absorb its representative in E, use all powers in Pic0 to get degree zero, then use a torsion-killing power of every ambient line to obtain degree zero for every pullback. Real c1=0 suffices for the curvature-flatness argument. Thus K_Z+D=h^*(KY+S), D>0, excludes κ(Z)≥0 on a persistent normalization component under rational hypotheses too. The whole Picard map is proved zero on class VII only with H2(Y,Z)=0; with rational vanishing alone its Pic0 map is zero and finite torsion images can remain.

### Audit of the local-complete-intersection extension C4e

The root's C4e reduction was checked independently, including the ruling contractions. For a blowdown σ:Z→Z′ with exceptional E, D′=σ_*D and coefficient a of E in D, the actual divisor comparison is K_Z+D=σ*(K_Z′+D′)+(1+a−mult_p(D′))E. Its intersection with E and c1(K_Z+D)=0 force a=mult_p(D′)−1. No factorization of h through the contraction is needed. This exact relation justifies both reverse-blowup arguments. Reduced horizontal conductor stays smooth and disjoint; horizontal conductor 2C, once C²=0, forces all contractions away from C and leaves no vertical part on the minimal model.

The extra generic-type hypothesis is redundant in that proof. For D_Z=2C+V and positive-genus normalization of its image curve D, all generic branches over D come from C, because the vertical curves are rational. Their conductor exponents are all two. For a reduced transverse plane curve f=∏f_i, the functions supported on branch i are (∏_{j≠i}f_j)|_{f_i}R_i, since the corresponding principal ideals intersect in their product. Therefore c_i=c_i(intrinsic)+Σ_{j≠i}I_ij. A singular branch has intrinsic conductor at least two, and a further branch would contribute at least two; hence the sole-branch case has conductor two and ring C+t²C{t}=C{t²,t³}. If all branches are smooth, exponent two permits only two branches with intersection multiplicity two, or three pairwise transverse branches. These are exactly A2, A3 and the ordinary triple type. A.5.5 applies once the image curve is a local complete intersection. The remaining global restriction in C4e is therefore the local-complete-intersection condition itself.

### Non-lci symbolic-square cusp: A.2.6

A.2.6 gives an exact local obstruction to importing the conormal quadratic jet into the remaining case. The prime ideal I=(y²−xz,yz²−x⁴,z³−x³y) is the kernel of x↦t⁵,y↦t⁶,z↦t⁷. For F=4x⁷−15x³yz²+10x²y³z−3xy⁵+4z⁵, the critical locus is exactly that curve. The gradient identities are written in (A.NL.3), and Hess(F)|D=2t²¹(7t²,0,−5)ᵀ(7t²,0,−5). The radical cubic is −20t¹⁷. ### Reduced conductor-image scheme: A.5.9

A.5.9 completes the remaining ruled obstruction when the conductor image scheme Γ is reduced, with no original lci condition on its image curves. Connectedness is proved using the exact blowdown formula A.RC.5: the minimal ruled divisor 2C0+V0 is connected and every reverse blowup compatible with effective D_Z preserves connected support. Reducedness also settles the generic branch comparison without assuming the image curve already has positive genus. At its generic point, O_Γ and Q each have length one. Thus (η_*O_T)/c has length two. The horizontal component C contributes twice the degree C→D, so that degree is one and no other branch lies over D. ### Topological genus bound for every horizontal image: A.5.11

A.5.11 proves g(D^ν)≤1 in the remaining ruled normalization situation for a torus fibration, without any lci or conductor-image reducedness assumption. All normalization exceptional curves are vertical, and the unique horizontal strict-conductor curve C is a ruling section. Its map gives φ:B=C→Σ=D^ν. When Σ has positive genus, every other conductor component is vertical and has rational image. The actual map ψ=φ∘p~ on the normal surface has nonconstant values on a normalization fibre only within a finite set A⊂Σ. The finite set consists of constant values on vertical conductor components and points above finitely many singularities/intersections on D.

Collapse an embedded finite tree J⊂Σ containing A. The quotient q:Σ→Σ/J is a homotopy equivalence, since J is a contractible CW subcomplex. Proper finite normalization is a topological quotient, so qψ descends to a continuous a:S→Σ/J. The curve normalization map δ:Σ→D⊂S satisfies aδ=q; thus a is surjective on fundamental groups.
