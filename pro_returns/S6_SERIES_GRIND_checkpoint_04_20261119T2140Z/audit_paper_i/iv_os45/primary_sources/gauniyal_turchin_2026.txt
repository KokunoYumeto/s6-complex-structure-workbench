               Abstract We derive combinatorial formulas for invariants of smooth embeddings of (2` − 1)-spheres
               into R3` for ` ≥ 2. In particular, we obtain such a formula for the Haefliger invariant, which classifies
               smooth knots S 4k−1 ,→ R6k up to isotopy. Our approach is similar in spirit to the work of Goussarov,
               Polyak, and Viro, who expressed finite-type invariants of classical knots in terms of Gauss diagrams.
               Analogously, we project higher-dimensional knots and links onto a hyperplane and study the preimages
               of the loci of double and singular points in the embedded spheres. As an auxiliary result, we show that the
               space of n-dimensional braids with m strands in Rn+q is a homotopy retract of the space of string links
               t Rn ,→ Rn+q for q ≥ 3, thereby providing a short proof to a conjecture of Komendarczyk, Koytcheff,
               m
               and Volić.

               0-submanifold of S 1 . For example, the Gauss diagram from Figure 1 can alterna-
