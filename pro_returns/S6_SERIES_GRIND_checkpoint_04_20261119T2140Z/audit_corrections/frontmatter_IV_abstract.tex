\begin{abstract}
We compute the smooth returns of the marked torus-fibred six-sphere
families in $\Theta_7=\mathbb Z/28$, keeping the physical line,
positive parameter loop, finite caps and ordered cusp fan in every
formula. An explicit inverse-line and quaternionic cap comparison
fixes the physical sign. The original reference return is zero;
the primitive OS45 return is three, so its positive covers realize
every class. Complete integral fan algorithms compute the scalar
and marked source returns and give explicit counterexamples to the
former norm-polynomial rule.
For the E6 point lines $M_6$ and $M_2$, the order-three source
loops have physical pairs $(-27,-12)$ and $(-3,-12)$ and exactly
sixteen fan values. The full selected-infinity loops have pair
$(-4,8)$ and all fourteen odd fan values. The selected-I3 $M_6$
loop has pair $(47,-4)$ and explicit words of at most seven native
flops realizing all twenty-eight classes. Proper relative cap
products preserve both characteristic integers separately throughout
the stated reduced and good cap progressions, with both primitive
norm signs and their full prescribed reference maps. Each conclusion
retains its actual scalar range and endpoint. The final part records
the remaining attachment statements and their precise proof gaps.
\end{abstract}
