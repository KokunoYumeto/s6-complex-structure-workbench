In the displayed quotient defining $\mathcal H$, let $\Delta$
denote the triangle deck group of
$\mathfrak h_z^\circ\longrightarrow B^\circ$.
It is the image of $\pi_1(B^\circ)$ under the deck representation;
it is not identified with that fundamental group. Thus
\[
 \mathcal H=\bigl(\mathfrak h_z^\circ\times
       (L_\Lambda\otimes\mathbb R)/L_\Lambda\bigr)/\Delta
\]
uses the actual deck action and its restricted integral monodromy.
Equivalently one may replace $\mathfrak h_z^\circ$ by the universal
cover of $B^\circ$ and quotient by $\pi_1(B^\circ)$.
The invariant-lattice, fibre-dimension, minimal-copy and finite-cover
arguments are unchanged: they use the image of the monodromy
representation, while the finite-index argument correctly applies
to subgroups of $\pi_1(B^\circ)$.
