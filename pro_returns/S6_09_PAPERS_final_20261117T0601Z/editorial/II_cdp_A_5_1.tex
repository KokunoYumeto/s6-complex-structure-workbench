
\begin{proposition}[What trivial normalization pullback forces]\label{cdp:st:A.5.1}
Let $S$ be a connected reduced full fibre of a proper map from a complex manifold to a smooth curve. For every line bundle $A$ on $S$ with $\eta^*A\simeq\OO_{\widetilde S}$, the ambient group contains the canonical image of $H^0(\widetilde S,\OO_{\widetilde S})$. In particular it is nonzero. This applies to the subcase of Proposition~7.3 in which all $\eta^*(L_t|_S)$ are trivial. It is compatible with the vanishing of $H^0(S,Q\otimes A)$ through the exact maps of \ref{cdp:st:A.1.1}, as \ref{cdp:st:A.4.1} demonstrates.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A local coordinate of the base trivializes $N^*$. Tensoring the injection in (A.3) by $A$ and applying the projection formula gives
$\eta_*\eta^*A\hookrightarrow K\otimes A\hookrightarrow V\otimes A$.
Taking global sections preserves injectivity. Every connected component of the compact normalization has the nonzero constant function $1$, proving nonvanishing. No normal-crossings assumption is needed for this inclusion.
\end{proof}

The line in the printed proof asserting $H^2(\widetilde S,\omega_{\widetilde S})=0$ also cannot hold for a connected compact normal Cohen--Macaulay surface with its dualizing sheaf: Serre duality identifies its dual with $H^0(\widetilde S,\OO)=\C$. Replacing $\omega_{\widetilde S}$ there by $\OO_{\widetilde S}$ matches the vanishing stated at the beginning of that proof, but does not settle its omitted genus-one case.
