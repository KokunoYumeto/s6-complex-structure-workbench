
\begin{proposition}[An unavoidable failure on a rational homology six-sphere]\label{cdp:st:C4b}
Let $f:Y\to\PP^1$ be a connected-fibre holomorphic map from a compact complex threefold with $b_1=b_2=b_3=0$. For every $L\in\Pic(Y)$ there is a scheme-theoretic fibre $F$ with
\[
 H^2(F,(T_Y\otimes L)|_F)\ne0,\qquad
 H^0(F,\Omega_Y^1|_F\otimes(K_Y\otimes L^{-1})|_F)\ne0. \tag{C.16}\label{cdp:eq:C.16}
\]
For torus general fibre the reduced fibre Euler numbers sum to two. Within the fibre class of \ref{cdp:st:C4a}, exactly one fibre has rational normalization; it is an irreducible reduced normal-crossing fibre of Euler number two. For that normalization,
\[
H^2(\widetilde S,K_{\widetilde S}\otimes
             \eta^*(K_Y^{-1}\otimes L))=\C
\quad\hbox{for every }L\in\Pic(Y).                    \tag{C.17}\label{cdp:eq:C.17}
\]
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The divisor of any fibre is nonzero and effective. The index argument C.1 therefore gives $H^2(Y,T_Y\otimes M)\ne0$ for every line bundle $M$. Suppose $R^2f_*(T_Y\otimes L)=0$. Twisting by a sufficiently large positive pullback from the base kills $H^1(R^1f_*)$, so Leray would give a line bundle $M=L\otimes f^*\OO(n)$ with $H^2(Y,T_Y\otimes M)=0$, a contradiction. Thus $R^2f_*(T_Y\otimes L)$ is nonzero. Its stalk has nonzero residue at some point, and top-degree base change gives the first nonvanishing in \eqref{cdp:eq:C.16}. Cartier-fibre adjunction and Serre duality give the second, with the exact line bundle displayed there.

For torus general fibre \ref{cdp:st:C2a} gives $\sum e(F_{\rm red})=e(Y)=2$. In the class of \ref{cdp:st:C4a}, \ref{cdp:st:A.4.6} makes every singular reduced contribution either zero or two, with two exactly for the rational hexagonal normalization; smooth finite torus quotients have zero Euler number. There is consequently exactly one positive fibre. Its smooth rational normalization has $\Pic^0=0$ and torsion-free integral second cohomology. Every ambient line bundle has torsion first Chern class, whose pullback is therefore zero. In particular both $\eta^*K_Y$ and $\eta^*L$ are trivial. Serre duality on the connected rational normalization gives \eqref{cdp:eq:C.17}.
\end{proof}
