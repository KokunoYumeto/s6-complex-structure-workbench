A TWO-PARAMETER FAMILY OF COMPLEX STRUCTURES ON S 6

arXiv:2609.33785v1 [math.AG] 27 Sep 2026

JEFF VIACLOVSKY
Abstract. We construct a two-parameter family of complex structures on the standard
six-sphere, starting from a rational elliptic surface with singular fibers III ∗ , I1 , I1 , I1 . The
resulting torus fibration over P1 has one multiple fiber of multiplicity four, one hexagonal
fiber, and two rank-one degenerations. These complex structures are not biholomorphic to
the original Alpöge–Claude examples.

1. Introduction
The purpose of this paper is to construct complex structures on S 6 which are not biholomorphic to the original examples of Alpöge–Claude [3]. We follow Engel’s construction from
an elliptic surface, a line bundle, and a lifted translation [4], but start with the minimal
regular model of
(1.1)

y 2 = x3 + tx + 1.

This rational elliptic surface π : S → P1 has a III ∗ fiber at infinity and three nodal fibers at
the roots p1 , p2 , p3 of 4t3 + 27. The section P (t) = (0, 1) generates its Mordell–Weil group.
For M = OS (P − O), a suitable lift of translation by −2P gives a family of complex two-tori
over the complement of these four values.
The threefold examples are obtained by taking a free Z-quotient of M × |SU , where U =
1
P \ {p1 , p2 , p3 , ∞} and SU = π −1 (U ). This is a smooth torus fibration. We then complete
this family to a holomorphic map f : X → P1 with four exceptional fibers. Over p1 and p2 ,
the special fibers have normalizations which are ruled over elliptic curves; each singular fiber
is obtained by identifying its zero and infinity sections. These are rank-one degenerations.
The special fiber over p3 has the same hexagonal local model as in Alpöge–Claude: the
normalization is a degree-6 del Pezzo surface, and the fiber is obtained by identifying a cycle
of P1 s in pairs; the resulting singular fiber has three double curves and two triple points.
This degeneration is rank-two. The above three fibers are all reduced.
