The minimal cyclic smooth-recognition gap is now closed.
D.2.44--45 compute the actual relative three-frame invariant
for every residual-four, eight and twenty pattern, including
all simply connected finite-II logarithmic orders and twists.
Together with D.2.35--39 and D.2.42, and the sphere cases,
this identifies all $162$ minimal simply connected patterns.
The remaining scope of D.2 concerns general higher translation
weights and additional regular logarithmic transformations.
Their full fundamental groups and integral homology are
computed by D.2.41 and D.2.46, but those groups alone do not
identify their smooth types. Even in weight one the extra-log
group can be
$\mathbb Z/g\oplus\mathbb Z/R\oplus\mathbb Z/(cR/g)$,
with the actual extension parameters retained in $g$.
The corresponding stable tangent and complete classifying
invariants, or an explicit framed-handle recognition, have
not yet been supplied uniformly for that broader range.
