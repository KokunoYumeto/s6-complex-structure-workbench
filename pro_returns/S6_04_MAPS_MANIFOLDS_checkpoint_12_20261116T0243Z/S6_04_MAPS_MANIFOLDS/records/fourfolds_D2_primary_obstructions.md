Its order divides9: on CP1 x CP2 take the integral degree-two class 3x+y. Its mod3 reduction factors through CP2 and hence sends the six-dimensional fundamental class to zero. The ordinary mod3 cube is reduction Z9→Z3. Our manifolds therefore have mu in{0,3,6}; the automorphisms ±1 of H2 identify3 and6 and leave0 distinct. The oriented rank-four normal bundle is trivial, since Y_k is spin, and its framing is unique since pi2SO4=0. The result has H2=H3=Z3 and b3=0. The mod3 class extends across the surgery trace. For k0 it has (P,mu)=(0,0); for k3 it has (0,3). Hence these two explicit models are the exact alternatives for the remaining c3 cases. No choice between them is asserted.

The needed primary invariant is the Pontryagin cube P3(alpha) in H6(X;Z9), evaluated on [X]. There is stronger localization here than merely mod2. For c4 or8, every scalar stabilizer for C_c is either trivial or the whole group. The fixed locus is C plus one I_c elliptic curve E. Thus the complement is a free C_c-space and localization by the generator t in H2(BC_c;Z/c) is valid over Z/c itself. Unlike the C9/C3 attempt, there is no intermediate isotropy to discard.

The unsquared symmetric AH line of D.2.35 has character c/2 on C and zero on E. The common-piece torsion-free fundamental group and exact normal columns identify its reduction with the global degree-two generator. Normalize that generator to evaluate1 on the complex C. The equivariant restrictions are then x+(c/2)t on C and alpha_E[E]^*+eta*zeta on E. Here eta has degree1 on BC_c, and zeta comes from H1(E;Z/c). Although eta can have nonzero square at an even cyclic group, zeta lifts integrally on the oriented elliptic curve and has square zero. Thus the mixed class squares to zero.

The normal Euler classes have leading terms -t² and -w²t², and degree-one localization gives alpha_E=−w². The cube on C is3(c/2)² x t², which is zero modulo c for both4 and8. The elliptic contribution is zero. Consequently the full mod-c cube vanishes. Zhubr Section3.12 gives H6(K(Z_(2^m),2);Z)=Z_(2^m), detected by this cube, so mu=0 integrally in its complete target.

The natural spin class lambda=p1/2 also vanishes. The equivariant complex determinant is trivial: H²(X;Z)=0 and its base equivariant weight is zero at C. Thus lambda=−c2 equivariantly. On either fixed curve the tangent-line correction has square zero, and lambda divided by the normal Euler class is exactly−1. Therefore <alpha lambda,[X]>=−(1−w²). The actual weights are1 or3 for c4 and5 for c8; in both cases w²=1 mod c. This gives lambda0. Naturality and the surjection from free coefficient groups in Zhubr3.11--3.12 show2P=PD(lambda) for all coefficient groups (the equality holds on both free generators epsilon,zeta and hence descends). Hence the only remaining refined class is P=0 or c/2. Each is fixed by every automorphism of Z/c, so there are exactly two candidate oriented smooth types.

The nonzero candidate is the image of the conic-surgery class under Z2→Zc,1↦c/2: its refined P is c/2 and its target cube is zero. A concrete simply connected representative is obtained from M_conic #(S2 x S4) by surgery on an embedded sphere representing (torsion_generator,−c/2) in Z2⊕Z. Its quotient H2 is Zc, and the surgery trace extends the indicated K(Zc,2)-map. The zero candidate is surgery on c times the fibre sphere in S2 x S4. These descriptions use the full Zhubr invariant and do not infer equality from homology.

The remaining order-two refinement is not determined by merely halving the congruence for lambda: division by two modulo an even integer is precisely the missing information. A possible spin quadratic/Arf invariant or an explicit spin-bordism surgery calculation is needed. No such calculation is claimed in this note.
