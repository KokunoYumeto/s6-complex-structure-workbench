OS45 P2G,Q8G, weight2 at I8: refined-framing research after checkpoint11.

Established independently: the ordinary regular-fibre three-tori detect
only the even subgroup of H3(X)=Z8. The positive cap's two free H3
classes also have even linking coordinates (-126,192) against the chosen
bounding chain for eight times the H2 torsion generator. Consequently a
new regular torus or a free local H3 class does not by itself decide the
remaining refined bit.

The exact bridge in root_OS45_bridge.py resolves the homology problem.
The positive cusp boundary's angular area e1 wedge delta has order2.
It is zero in the cap by an actual angular solid torus and zero in the
exterior by the single monodromy column15, coming from the first I1
whose monodromy is T1 and whose section cocycles vanish. The two free
normal-torus images in exterior H2 are independent, so the full MV kernel
is exactly this Z2. A glued bridge is therefore an odd H3 class.

Geometric route being examined: the exterior monodromy annulus has
commutator [e2,theta]=e1. Closing it with the cusp e1-disc and taking its
delta circle should produce a delta-invariant T3 spanning the two pieces.
This is not yet asserted as an embedded framed representative. The
angular cap disc meets a literal critical double curve at its core;
an actual push-off and its Bott/framing correction must be tracked.

A discarded early suggestion was that an odd free H3 class already lay
inside the positive cusp. The exact normal-column pairing is even, so
the MV torsion bridge is the relevant additional source. A proposed
matching RP3 from slopes e1 and e1-2delta was also premature: those two
slopes do not occur with the same marked elliptic direction in this
example. The actual first I1 has vanishing e1 with no delta correction.

All coefficients of the retained critical-cycle bar bounding chain are
even. This controls its core and normal-disc contributions, but does not
control the separate cap specialization chains which identify the
literal critical curves with their regular-fibre preimages. Treating
that observation alone as even critical linking would be a gap.

Potential reference-framing argument: on the complement of the critical
cycle the abstract differential kernel and target admit invariant
complex frames. If the bridge is realized as a free delta-circle bundle
over a torus with trivial circle clutch, its actual tangent/normal frame
can be chosen over the quotient torus, and its comparison with the
invariant reference factors through that two-dimensional quotient.
This would give even reference parity. The actual geometric realization
and the specialization-chain intersection remain to be proved.

Other ideas considered but not used as proofs: an equivariant spin
localization formula for the refined class; a degree-two line-power map
to a smaller cusp; and a disk-bundle filling of a hypothetical RP3.
The first lacks the necessary integral two-primary refinement, the
second is not an unramified covering of the filled simply connected
spaces, and the third has an unresolved relative spin-structure issue.
