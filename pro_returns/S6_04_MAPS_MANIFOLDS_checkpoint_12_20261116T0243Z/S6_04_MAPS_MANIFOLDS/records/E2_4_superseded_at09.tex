\paragraph{Problem E.2.4 (Open in this incomplete return).}
The identification of the fivefold (E.16) by a smooth
classification theorem for ten-manifolds with its infinite
fundamental group remains.  Its exact group, all integral
homology and constancy under displacement are established.
The earlier integral Leray extension gap is resolved by the
direct complement Mayer--Vietoris calculation in E.2.6 below;
its middle torsion includes a genuine factor of order 24.
That proof also gives the exact smooth gluing model
$(E\times T^4)\cup(T^4\times E)$ with its marked boundary
identification.  We distinguish this explicit diffeomorphism
model from an application of a classification theorem: the
computed homology and fundamental group alone do not determine
a smooth ten-manifold.
The subsequent E.2.9 proves that the natural framing bounds
over the aspherical group model.  E.2.10 excludes an ordinary
closed-spin model for the actual regular-fibre embedding, and
E.2.11 computes the second-homotopy coinvariants as
$\mathbb Z^2\oplus(\mathbb Z/12)^6$.  In particular the full
second-homotopy module is not projective; a connected-sum model
with an aspherical product and sphere products is impossible.
The full $\mathbb ZG$-module, its higher attaching data and the
resulting smooth surgery classification remain to be determined.
