### Postrelease base-domain clarification

The statement says a compact curve, while Kawai's construction has a nonsingular base curve. The exact transport to the normalization was checked after release. Because Y is reduced, f factors through C_red. The normalization ν:C^ν→C_red is finite. Over the regular locus the lift exists uniquely. ### Postrelease clarification of the frozen local Albanese construction

The following expands the local-family paragraph of the frozen alternative proof; its frozen bytes remain unchanged. Its fibre integrals depend holomorphically on the parameter.

The fundamental group of a primary Kodaira surface is a central extension of Z² by Z² with nonzero degree. Its centre is the fibre subgroup; quotienting by that centre gives the rank-two Albanese lattice. A differentiable marking over the disc fixes this quotient. The one-form annihilates the central fibre cycles on every fibre, so its two periods on the quotient supply exactly the fixed rank-two period lattice, not an unspecified sublattice.
