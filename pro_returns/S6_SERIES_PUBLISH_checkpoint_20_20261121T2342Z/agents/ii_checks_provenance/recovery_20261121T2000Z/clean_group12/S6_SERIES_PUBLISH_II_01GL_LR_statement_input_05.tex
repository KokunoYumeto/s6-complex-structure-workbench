\begin{thm}\label{thm: main} Let $Y(u)$ be the family of complex manifolds constructed in the previous sections. 
	Then the differentiable manifold underlying the constructed complex manifolds $Y(u)$ is diffeomorphic to $S^6$.

\end{thm}
