\begin{prop}\label{prop: compactification X}
The fibration $\eta^\circ\colon X^\circ\to\IC$ extends to a proper
map $\eta\colon X\to\IP^1$ from a smooth compact complex threefold.
The fibre $X_\infty$ is obtained from a del Pezzo surface of degree six
by identifying opposite sides of its anticanonical hexagon.  The
section $\sigma^\circ$ extends to a holomorphic section $\sigma$ of
$\eta$.
\end{prop}
