$$ \eta: \tilde S \to S$$ be the normalization of $S$; denote by $N \subset S$ the non-normal locus, equipped with the
complex structure given by the conductor ideal. Let $\tilde N \subset \tilde S$ be the complex-analytic preimage. 
Let
$$ \pi: \hat S \to \tilde S$$
be a minimal desingularization and 
$$ \sigma: \hat S \to S_0$$
be a minimal model.

\begin{lemma} \label{lem:Mori} In the notations of (\ref{not}), we have
$$ \omega_{\tilde S} \simeq \eta^*(\omega_S) \otimes \sI_{\tilde N}$$
and 
$$ \omega_{\hat S} \simeq \pi^*\eta^*(\omega_S) \otimes \pi^*(\sI_{\tilde N}) \otimes \sO_{\hat S}(E) =  \pi^*\eta^*(\omega_S) \otimes \sO_{\hat S}(-\hat N) \otimes \sO_{\hat S}(- \hat E)$$
with an effective divisor $E$ supported on the exceptional locus of $\pi$ and $\hat N$ the strict transform of $\tilde N$ in $\hat S$.
Moreover, there are exact sequences
$$ 0 \to \sO_S \to \eta_*(\sO_{\tilde S}) \to \omega^{-1}_S \otimes \omega_N \to 0$$
and
$$ 0 \to \sO_N \to \eta_*(\sO_{\tilde N}) \to \omega^{-1}_S \otimes \omega_N \to 0$$
\end{lemma}
