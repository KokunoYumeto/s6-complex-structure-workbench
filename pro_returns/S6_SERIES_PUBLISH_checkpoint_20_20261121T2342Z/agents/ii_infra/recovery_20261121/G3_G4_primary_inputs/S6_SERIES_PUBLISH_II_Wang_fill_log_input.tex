\begin{proposition}\label{fill:log}
Twist the deck action by this translation, as in
\cite[Construction~3.5]{Engel}:
\[
 \widetilde\rho(x)=\rho(x)+\ell/3.
\]
This action is free. The quotient $X'/\langle\widetilde\rho\rangle$
is a smooth proper filling of the original punctured family, with
central fiber $3B$. Its reduction $B=X'_0/\langle\widetilde\rho\rangle$
is bielliptic. Under parallel transport in $X'$, specialization from
$B$ is the pullback of the covering $p:X'_0\to B$.
\end{proposition}
