Proposition 16.4 (Pointwise cusp limits and the fixed double curve). [tag 02PB]
The literal character has no continuous extension at either triple point or at generic points
of two of the three double curves. It has the constant limit 0 on the smooth locus of W and on
the third double curve away from the triple points. In the chart q = z0z1z2, x1 = z1, x2 = z2,
the extendible double curve is z0 = z1 = 0, z2 ̸= 0. The fixed curve of the C∗-action in direction
d = e2 is instead z0 = z2 = 0, z1 ̸= 0, and is one of the nonextendible curves. Consequently the
extendible double curve and the fixed double curve are distinct.
