Proposition 22.31 (The obstruction to a marked period lift). [tag 030H]
Fix 0 < |α|, |a| < 1, and put
H = (C2 \ {0})/⟨(x, v) 7→(αx, av)⟩,
E = {x = 0} ≃C∗/aZ.

h.
