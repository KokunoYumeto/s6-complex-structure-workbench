Proposition 21.1 (The two minimal toroidal refinements). [tag 02ZJ]
Among subdivisions with no new rays, the fan in 20.5 has exactly two smooth period-invariant
subdivisions restricting to the chosen A2 triangulation at hu = 0. These are also all subdivisions
which are flat over the original (q, u) bidisc, have reduced u-central divisor, and retain that
boundary triangulation. Without the joint-flatness restriction there are infinitely many smooth
invariant subdivisions retaining that triangulation and having reduced u-central divisor.

2. For the stronger assertion, a new ray with hq, hu > 0 would give a divisor mapping to the
