Parameter equivalence and deformations
Remark A.1 (Period and deformation comparison). [tag 02YS] For the fixed standard (3, 4, ∞)
data, the tangent cohomology (1, 1, 1, 0), smooth one-dimensional Kuranishi germ, automorphism
group C∗, and equivalence Xc ≃Xc′ ⇐⇒c′ −c ∈2Z agree with the corresponding assertions
of Du et al. (2026a). Version 2, entitled Oka Geometry of Complex Six-Spheres, omits those
tangent-cohomology, miniversality, full-automorphism and exact-period assertions. Version 1,
submitted on 23 September 2026, states the exact 2Z equivalence as its Theorem 1.4.
Proof.
1. Use the same oriented triangle, ordered integral basis (bγ, bu, bw, bδ), finite twists, and
untranslated unit-square cusp fan as version 1.
Its chosen function β0 and our βpart
obey the same affine transformation laws, and both β0 + τ and βpart + τ extend over the
cusp. Their difference descends to a holomorphic function on P1, hence is the constant
δ = βpart−β0. The exact parameter map and inverse are a = c+δ and c = a−δ: substitution
gives Ωc+δ = Πc in the stated ordered basis. Writing M0 = sup(ℑβ0 −6(ℑµ)2/ℑτ), our
corresponding bound is Mpart = M0 + ℑδ, so this map also takes our admissible half-plane
bijectively to version 1’s. The ordinary quotients, finite logarithmic maps, and cusp charts
then identify by their identical marked periods and specified gluing data. In particular
a′ −a = c′ −c. Its Theorem 1.4 therefore gives the displayed exact-period equivalence and
the full automorphism group in this standard-data scope.
2. Theorem 6.12 of version 1 gives the tangent-cohomology tuple, and Proposition 6.16 gives
the smooth reduced Kuranishi germ and the locally miniversal parameter family. These are
the four assertions compared with theorems B.2, C.4 and C.5 under that same parameter
and marking.
3. The second version retains the period construction and the Oka conclusions. Its displayed
section list and its mathematical statements omit the first version’s logarithmic-deformation
and classification sections and the four assertions just listed.
