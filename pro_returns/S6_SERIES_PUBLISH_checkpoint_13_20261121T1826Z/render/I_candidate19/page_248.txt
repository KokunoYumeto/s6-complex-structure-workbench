Proposition 22.53 (Full-rank periods do not bound an affine comparison). [tag 030Y]
There is a proper semistable family of two-dimensional complex tori over a disc, with smooth
total threefold and rank-two unipotent monodromy on H1, and a fibre-preserving automorphism
over the punctured disc with the following properties. It acts identically on all relative invariant
one- and two-forms and on the Gauss–Manin variation. Their coherent extensions supplied by the
proper family therefore give the same lattices under this identification. An absolute holomorphic
canonical form extending across the centre is also unchanged. Nevertheless, the automorphism
has no meromorphic extension to the total family.
Thus these invariant-form and connection data do not alone control an affine origin or a
marked total-space comparison, even in the presence of a proper smooth semistable cap.
