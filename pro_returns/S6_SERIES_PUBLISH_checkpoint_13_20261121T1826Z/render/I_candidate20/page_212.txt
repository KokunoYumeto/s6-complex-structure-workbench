4. Now contract E in the compact surface S as in 22.28. The regular global-to-local deformation
map in that proposition lifts the specified analytic germ Z →A1 to a proper deformation
Y →∆of Y = S/E. The lifted deformation agrees analytically with this cone smoothing in a
neighbourhood of its singular point. Away from that neighbourhood the central surface is
smooth; openness of smoothness and properness allow a common shrinking of ∆for which
the family there is a submersion. Blow up the single cone vertex in Y. The resulting map
V →∆remains proper. Near the exceptional divisor its local model is the smooth space just
computed; elsewhere it is unchanged and smooth. Its central strict transform is exactly S,
because the maximal-ideal blow-up of the degree-eight elliptic cone is its given resolution
Tot(N) near E. The other component is Q, and their intersection is the original marked
elliptic curve. The local equation t = ξz proves flatness and reducedness near the intersection;
at a smooth point of either component t is a local coordinate. The noncentral fibres are the
smooth compact fibres of Y.
