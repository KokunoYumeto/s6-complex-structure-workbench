Proof. 1. The map of cocharacter lattices for the base change is
(y1, y2, hq, hr) 7−→(y1, y2, hq, nhr).
The old top ray indexed by l pulls back to (0, nl, 0, 1). Smoothness on the two-dimensional
top face forces insertion of every primitive ray ρnl+i, 0 < i < n: a skipped interval of length
d > 1 would retain lattice index d on that face. Joint flatness excludes any new ray with both
base heights positive, and the fixed bottom excludes new rays of hr = 0. Reducedness permits
only top rays of hr = 1. These are consequently all the rays.
2. On the left face ACρ0ρn, a triangulation with these collinear top vertices has a unique triangle
containing the bottom edge AC. Write its third vertex as ρa. The triangles on either side of
that bridge are then forced. The analogous right face has bridge BDρb. The first period takes
A, C to B, D and fixes every top ray, so periodicity requires a = b = k. The fixed bottom
triangles ABC and BCD must then cone to ρk. The remaining successive top intervals force
exactly the displayed cones, leaving no choice. Conversely those cones have determinant ±1
and common-face intersections. Their normalized volumes sum to n + 2, the volume of the
prism, so they cover it. For n = 2 and n = 12, the determinant, intersection and volume
calculations use exact rational arithmetic.
3. The period actions are
G1 : (y1, y2) 7→(y1 + hq, y2),
G2 : (y1, y2) 7→(y1, y2 + hq + nhr).
Thus the choices propagate consistently from the fundamental prism.
The proper toric
subdivisions descend by the covering construction of 20.6. Every ray has pure q-height or
pure r-height, so in a regular affine chart q and r are monomials in disjoint sets of coordinates.
This proves joint flatness. All nonzero r-heights equal one, and there are at most two top
rays in any maximal cone; this proves reduced normal crossings and absence of triple points.
Properness follows by descent from the proper toric modification of the base-changed proper
cusp model.
4. The central components and all their intersection maps are specified by the stars of the ρi.
Modulo the periods their indices are i mod n; adjacent indices meet in the conductor elliptic
surface. Away from the cusp their normalizations are the ruled bundles of the expanded
plumbing. At the unique bridge class the star has both bottom triangles, and gives the
nodal-curve blow-up and small resolution described in 21.2, on the corresponding ruled bundle.
This star description also fixes the gluing when the bridge is on an inserted component. The
endpoint choices k = 0, n are the canonical resolutions of the base changes of the two original
models. Intermediate values are related to them by the successive side flops.
5. Finally, G1 fixes each index and G2 changes it by n. The units in the cusp periods are
single-valued holomorphic functions of q and do not change a ray label. The toric charts
themselves are defined over q, not a root of q. Hence these labels remain global on the
punctured q-disc, proving the monodromy assertion. Further smooth periodic subdivisions
with mixed rays give infinitely many models flat over r alone, as in 21.1; they are excluded by
the stated joint-flatness condition.
