6. For an integral shear the Jacobian charts themselves identify: the period lattices differ by Sb,
and the bounded cusp matrix changes by an integer in its β entry. Every toric unit exp(2πiCλ)
is therefore unchanged. The fan, lattice, and toric gluings are the same, and on root discs
the basis change identifies the smooth Jacobian and its cyclic action. Equality of the torsor
classes now gives a holomorphic isomorphism for the fixed sheaf A, which consists precisely of
translations extending over the cusp. It extends on all three special charts. Conversely every
isomorphism preserves the class, so forces b even.
