Proof. 1. The presentation g1g2g0 = 1 sends the third peripheral generator to −4 + 3 = −1. The
first two images generate Z/12, so the resulting action on the twelve sheets is transitive. This
constructs the connected cover by its monodromy representation.
2. The rational function t4/(t −1)3 has orders 4, −3, −1 at the three punctures. Its twelfth-root
equation has the same marked deck representation. The covering-space identification is the
map which sends a chosen twelfth root at one base point to its chosen sheet and continues
both along the same paths. Its equality of monodromy makes that map single-valued; its local
analytic root coordinates make it holomorphic. Normalization extends this identification to
the compact covers.
3. Locally y12 = zau(z) has gcd(12, a) branches of ramification index 12/ gcd(12, a), after
absorbing the holomorphic unit in a local twelfth root. Thus the three contributions to
ramification are 4(3 −1), 3(4 −1), and 12 −1. Their sum is 28. Riemann–Hurwitz gives
2 −2g = 2 · 12 −28 = −4, so g = 3. Reversing the deck convention is realized by y 7→y−1,
with the reciprocal defining equation; the compact curve and the ramification calculation are
unchanged.
