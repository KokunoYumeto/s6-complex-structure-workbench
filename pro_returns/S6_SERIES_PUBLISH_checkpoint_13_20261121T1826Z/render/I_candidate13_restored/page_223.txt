Remark 22.38 (The invariant-cycle equality in the Hopf degeneration). [tag PT17] The compact
fibration of Proposition 22.37 has identity local monodromy T on H1(F; R), but b1(Y ) = 0 and
b1(F) = 1. It therefore refutes the converse equality clause in Honda and Viaclovsky (2025,
Theorem 1.1, p. 553), also stated as Theorems 1.2 and 3.2 in arXiv version 1, pp. 1 and 8. The
relative-homology and circle-bundle sequences in that proof give
b1(Y ) ≤dim H1(F; R)T ≤b1(F)
(Honda and Viaclovsky, 2025, arXiv version 1, equation (3.9), p. 9). Identity of T is equivalent
to equality in the second inequality. In the present example the first inequality is 0 < 1. Equality
of the endpoint Betti numbers still forces T to be the identity by this same chain of inequalities.
For an irreducible central fibre, the top-dimensional homology in the displayed relative sequence
has rank one, giving b1(Y ) = dim H1(F; R)T , as proved in Honda and Viaclovsky (2025, arXiv
version 1, Corollary 3.7, p. 11). The example here has two central components, with the inclusion
maps on H1(E) computed in Proposition 22.37.
