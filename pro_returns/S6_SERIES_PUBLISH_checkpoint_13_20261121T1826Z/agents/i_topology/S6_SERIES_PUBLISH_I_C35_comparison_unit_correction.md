# C.3.5 — exact unit correction to the preread derivation

The preread note chose special local coordinates in two intermediate equalities. The retained markings permit original coordinates with nonconstant units. The printed target proof correctly retains these units. This is a correction of the checker note, not a source refutation.

Let t=s_1^3 a_1(s_1), t−1=s_2^4 a_2(s_2), with a_j holomorphic units. Then, with the actual coordinates,

K s_1^−12=a_1^4/(t−1)^3,
K s_2^12=t^4/a_2^3.

These exact expressions prove the required extension and agree with the target statement. The earlier special-coordinate equalities are recovered only after the explicit changes s'_1=s_1 a_1^(1/3), s'_2=s_2 a_2^(1/4) on sufficiently small discs, with chosen holomorphic roots. Those coordinate changes were not part of the retained marking and are not needed here.

Likewise keep q=t_c and put A(q)=q t(q), a holomorphic unit. Then

q K(t(q))=A(q)^4/(A(q)−q)^3=:H(q),

so H is holomorphic and nonvanishing. The explicit change to q'=1/t(q) is q'=q/A(q); its derivative at the cusp is 1/A(0)≠0. The earlier formula H=(1−q)^−3 applied to that special q' coordinate; the corrected comparison keeps H(q) in the original cusp chart. The full unit U_(12,c) and gamma-hat deck multiplier multiply to (1,q^−1H(q)) exactly, for this general H as well.

For finite extension write kappa_1=(2pi i)^−1 log(F/(s_1G)) and kappa_2=(2pi i)^−1 log(Fs_2/G). Both arguments are holomorphic units on the retained discs. Then ell=w_1+kappa_1 and ell=−w_2+kappa_2 modulo integral constants, which is precisely the actual collar comparison used in the printed proof. All composition, integer defect and loop-sign calculations in the preread note were independent of the special-coordinate equalities and remain unchanged.
