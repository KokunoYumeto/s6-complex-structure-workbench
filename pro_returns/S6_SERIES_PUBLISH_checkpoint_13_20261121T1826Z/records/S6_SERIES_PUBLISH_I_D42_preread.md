## Marked cusp and the twist

Write q for the holomorphic base coordinate. The periodic smooth toric model has cocharacter lattice N'=Z^2⊕Z, with q the last character, and deck lattice barLambda generated by bar(gamma-hat),bar(u-hat). Its exact translation map is B0=[[0,1],[-1,0]], so B0 bar(gamma-hat)=(0,-1) and B0 bar(u-hat)=(1,0). These are the maps in primary AC26 Notation4.1 and Lemma4.3. The action on the dense torus is (x,q) -> (c_lambda(q) q^(B0 lambda) x,q), where c_lambda is a holomorphic unit to q=0.

The coherent class g_gamma restricts to the constant cocycle gamma in this cusp covering: gamma kills the toric lattice generated by w-hat,delta-hat and has values 1,0 on the two displayed deck generators. This also follows from its defining fibre image and the local Leray isomorphism over the disc; equality over the ordinary fibres extends in the locally free R1O bundle to q=0. Exponentiation gives the character (a,1), a=exp(2 pi i z), of M_z. Thus a is unequal to 1 exactly for z outside Z. On every normalization stratum this flat character line has its fixed trivialization; no reparameterization of z occurs.

## Logarithmic tangent sheaf on the central fibre

Let L=T_N(-log W) on the cusp neighbourhood N. Pullback to the toric cover gives O⊗N'_C, with its invariant logarithmic frame. Differentiating the deck formula gives, on W, the constant matrices U_lambda=[[I2,B0 lambda],[0,1]], since q times the logarithmic derivative of c_lambda(q) vanishes at q=0. Explicitly, U_gamma has its sole off-diagonal entry -1 in position (2,3), and U_u has entry +1 in position (1,3). They commute and their nilpotent parts square to zero.

The semisimplicial normalization resolution of W has a rational complete toric surface, rational curves and points as strata. L and M_z pull back to trivial bundles on each stratum. Every positive stratum-cohomology group therefore vanishes. Its global normalization complex computes the cellular cohomology of the real two-torus with coefficient representation (a U_gamma,U_u). Subdivision by the diagonal does not change that cellular complex's cohomology: elimination of the diagonal edge/one triangle gives the square CW complex. The latter is the Koszul complex V -> V⊕V -> V with maps d0(v)=(Av,Bv), d1(u,v)=Bu-Av, where A=aU_gamma-I, B=U_u-I.

Here det A=(a-1)^3. With a unequal to 1, define h1(u,v)=A^(-1)u and h2(w)=(0,-A^(-1)w). Commutation of A and B gives h1d0=I, d0h1+h2d1=I, d1h2=I. The new symbolic program verifies these exact identities. Hence all H^j(W,(L⊗M_z)|W) vanish. A finite free complex for the proper flat map over the disc computes these fibre cohomologies. A complex of finite free modules over the local ring C{q} that is contractible modulo q is contractible near q=0, by lifting the splittings and inverting their determinant units. Equivalently, successive top-degree Nakayama and analytic base change give the same conclusion. Therefore every (R^j f_*(L⊗M_z))_0 is zero.

## The exact normal quotient

At q=x, xy, or xyz, the quotient T_N/L is respectively the direct sum of the normal line modules of the one, two, or three local branches. For example, at xyz the local map sends (a,b,c) to (a mod x,b mod y,c mod z), with kernel generated by x partial_x,y partial_y,z partial_z. These maps glue on the normalization nu:S->W and give T_N/L=nu_*N_S. Since W is the full reduced principal toric boundary, restricting its divisor to a normalization component gives N_S=O_S(-D_S), where D_S is its toric boundary. The invariant logarithmic two-form identifies K_S(D_S)=O_S, hence N_S=K_S. This identification uses the base coordinate q and the invariant logarithmic volume; the determinant-one deck shears preserve that volume. Thus the global exact sequence is

0 -> L -> T_N -> nu_*K_S -> 0.

There is one normalization component S, the complete smooth rational toric surface with hexagonal fan. The map to the disc is constant q=0. The pulled-back M_z is trivial on S, directly from its flat character construction (the deck group acts freely on distinct normalization components of the cover). Serre duality and rationality give H^j(S,K_S)=(0,0,C) in degrees 0,1,2. Tensoring the exact sequence by M_z and taking direct images therefore gives R0=R1=0 and R2=C{q}/(q). This proves the entire module structure and the exact length one, rather than only its one-dimensional fibre.
