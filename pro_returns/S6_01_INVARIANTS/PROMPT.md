## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

- **Lead 1 (the diamond).** With E = Ω¹_X(log D)/f*Ω¹_B(log Σ), D = W + S₁ + S₂, Σ = {p₀, p₁, p₂}: f_*E = O ⊕ O(−1), R¹f_*E = O² ⊕ O(−1)², R²f_*E = O ⊕ O(−1); the connecting maps κ₀: f_*E → Ω¹_B(log Σ) ⊗ R¹f_*O_X and κ₁: R¹f_*E → Ω¹_B(log Σ) ⊗ R²f_*O_X satisfy div(det κ₀) = p₁ + p₂, κ₁ is surjective and H⁰(κ₁): C² → C has rank one; hence the direct images of Ω¹_X above and the rows (q = 3,…,0) of h^{p,q}: (0,0,0,1), (0,1,2,1), (1,2,1,0), (1,0,0,0).
- **Lead 2 (the scalar).** h^{1,2} = 1 if and only if aτ′ + μ′ is not identically zero, where h = a dζ₁ + dζ₂ is the unique section of f_*E with ⟨h, ξ⟩ = 1; this holds as soon as μ′(z₂) ≠ 0 in the uniformizing coordinate at the order-four point, where τ′ = 0. If instead the rank of H⁰(κ₁) were 0, one would get h^{1,2} = 2 and h^{1,1} = 3.
- **Lead 3 (μ in closed form).** μ is the elliptic logarithm of a generator P of the Mordell-Weil group of the rational elliptic surface y² = x³ − 3t³(t−1)x + 2t⁴(t−1)², P = ±(0, √2 t²(t−1)), and ℘_τ(μ) = 0 on every regular fibre (`course/cusp-and-compact-threefold.md` §9, Theorem 9.1, Theorem 11.2, (11.14), (13.9)). So μ is, up to sign and periods, the function of Eichler and Zagier (*On the zeros of the Weierstrass ℘-function*, Math. Ann. 258 (1982)): the zeros of ℘(·, τ) are m + 1/2 + nτ ± [ log(5 + 2√6)/(2πi) + 144πi√6 ∫_τ^{i∞} (σ − τ) Δ(σ) E₆(σ)^{−3/2} dσ ]; Duke and Imamoglu (attached) give a hypergeometric form. The special values are μ(z₁) = (2 − ρ)/3 with ρ = e^{πi/3} and μ(z₂) = (1 − i)/2. Check the formula against the original before you use it.
- **Lead 4 (Frölicher and Bott-Chern).** E₂ = E_∞ follows from the diamond and b_k(S^6) for any value of h^{1,2}; the Bott-Chern and Aeppli numbers follow from McHugh's Theorem 6.3, Proposition 6.4 and §7.1; H^{1,1}_BC is spanned by A = [∂G] and B = [∂̄Ḡ] for a ∂̄-closed representative G of the generator of H^{0,1}; every product of two classes of positive degree in H_BC vanishes; the map Pic(X) = C → H^{1,1}_BC is z ↦ zA + z̄B, and exp(12𝔤) = f*O(1).
- **Lead 5 (tangent cohomology).** χ(T_X) = c₃/2 = 1, h⁰(T_X) = 1, h³(T_X) = h⁰(Ω¹_X ⊗ K_X) = h⁰(Ω¹_X(−2S₂)) = 0, so h²(T_X) = h¹(T_X) ≥ 1: the obstruction space is not zero. For general M ∈ Pic(X): f_*(T_X ⊗ M) = 0 and R¹, R² have finite support with length(R²) − length(R¹) = 1; is (h⁰, h¹, h², h³)(T_X ⊗ M) = (0, 0, 1, 0)? The stalk of R²f_*(T_X ⊗ L) at p₀ is C{t} or C{t}/(t^r), r undetermined.
