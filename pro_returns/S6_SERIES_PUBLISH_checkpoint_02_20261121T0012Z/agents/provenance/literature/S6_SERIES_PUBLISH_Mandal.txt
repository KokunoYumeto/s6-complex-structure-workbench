                                                 Abstract. Let Mℓ,m = ℓ HP2 #m HP2 be the connected sum of ℓ copies of HP2 with m
                                                 copies of HP2 endowed with the opposite orientation. We characterise, in terms of Pontrya-
                                                 gin classes, the real vector bundles over Mℓ,m admitting a stable complex structure, and
                                                 deduce that Mℓ,m is stably almost complex if and only if ℓ − m is even. We then determine
                                                 when an oriented real vector bundle of even rank over Mℓ,m admits a complex structure.
                                                 Consequently, Mℓ,m admits an almost complex structure if and only if ℓ = 2m + 1 and m is
                                                 odd. This corrects an assertion made by Sato and Suzuki in 1974, according to which Mℓ,m
                                                 is never almost complex. We also identify the unjustified step in their argument.

                                                                                      1. Introduction
                                             The existence of complex and stable complex structures on real vector bundles is a funda-
                                          mental problem in topology, investigated extensively by Massey [Mas61], Thomas [Tho67],
                                          and Albanese and Milivojević [AM26], among others, in terms of obstruction theory, charac-
                                          teristic classes and secondary cohomology operations.
                                             For the quaternionic projective spaces HPn , the non-existence of almost complex structures
                                          was established in the decade following 1949. The case n = 1, in which HP1 ∼       = S4 , was
                                          settled by Ehresmann [Ehr49] and by Hopf [Hop49]. For n ≥ 4, Hirzebruch [Hir53] ruled out
                                          the existence of such structures using characteristic classes. The remaining cases, n = 2 and
                                          n = 3, were subsequently resolved by Milnor, a result announced during Hirzebruch’s address
                                          at the 1958 International Congress of Mathematicians [Hir60]. Milnor’s proof has remained
                                          unpublished, and a self-contained proof for all n ≥ 2 was later provided by Massey [Mas62],
                                                                           n
                                          who computed the ring K(HP e       ) and the Chern character of the tangent bundle of HPn .
                                          Gauduchon, Moroianu and Semmelmann [GMS11, Theorem 1.1] have since shown that HPn
                                          is not even stably almost complex for n ≥ 2; the case n = 1 is exceptional, the sphere S4
                                          being stably parallelisable and hence stably almost complex.
                                             The existence of almost complex structures on connected sums has received considerable
                                          attention, in particular for connected sums of complex projective spaces, studied by Go-
                                          ertsches and Konstantis [GK19] and by Yang [Yan19]; further results in this direction are

   A complex structure on an oriented real vector bundle ξ of rank 2n over a topological space
X is given by a bundle endomorphism J of ξ with J 2 = −id inducing the orientation of ξ.
Equivalently, ξ admits a complex structure if there exists a complex vector bundle of rank n
over X whose underlying real vector bundle is isomorphic to ξ as an oriented bundle. More
generally, a stable complex structure on a real vector bundle ξ over X is defined by a complex
vector bundle over X whose underlying real vector bundle is stably isomorphic to ξ. For X
a finite connected CW complex, an oriented real vector bundle ξ of rank 2n over X admits a
complex structure precisely when the homotopy class of its classifying map lies in the image
of the map [X, BU (n)] → [X, BSO(2n)] induced by the canonical inclusion of the unitary
group U (n) in the special orthogonal group SO(2n), where BG denotes the classifying space
of a topological group G. Similarly, writing K(X)
