# Prereading derivation08: fixed-marking anti-biholomorphism obstruction

Target: night:P1.17. Written before opening its printed proof. Inputs are the target statement, the marked matrices/cusp basis of P1.1, and P1A.4's algebraic-reduction statement. The following matrices were solved freshly from their equations before this derivation was written; no printed reflection or fan matrix was read.

The algebraic reduction to P1 is intrinsic. An anti-biholomorphism induces an antiholomorphic automorphism of that base. The order5 point, order4 point and nonnormal cusp are individually distinguished, and all three are real in the chosen lambda coordinate. Therefore the induced base map is lambda -> conjugate(lambda): composing it with ordinary conjugation gives a holomorphic Mobius transformation fixing three points, hence the identity.

On a smooth torus, lift the map to universal covers. Its antiholomorphic derivative is periodic, hence bounded on a lattice fundamental domain and constant by Liouville. The fibre map is therefore an antilinear affine map with one integral lattice map, locally constant over the connected smooth base. Based real peripheral paths to infinity and zero are fixed by conjugation and their loops are reversed. Thus its rational cohomology map R must satisfy R A=A^-1 R and R B=B^-1 R, with A=M1^t,B=M2^t.

Solving these homogeneous rational equations gives a one-dimensional space, generated by the following primitive integral matrix, normalized to fix e0:

R = [[1,2,-1,-1,2,-1],
     [0,0,0,0,-1,1],
     [0,0,-1,0,0,1],
     [0,0,0,-1,0,1],
     [0,-1,0,0,0,1],
     [0,0,0,0,0,1]].

It has R^2=I and determinant-1. The homology map is Rh=R^(-t). Integrality and unimodularity force the scalar multiplying this primitive reflection to be +1 or-1: a rational scalar preserving the primitive entries is integral and its sixth power must be1. Affine translations do not change this lattice action.

The cusp M0=(M1 M2)^-1 transforms under Rh to M2 M1. To compare with the same cusp peripheral path, the based transport correction gives Rc=M1 Rh (equivalently M2^-1 Rh, up to the cusp monodromy). Direct multiplication gives Rc M0 Rc^-1=M0^-1. In the specified cusp basis S this has block form [[P,Q],[0,-P]], with

-P = K = [[1,0,0],[0,1,0],[1,-1,-1]].

The two alternative based corrections differ by M0^-1, whose induced map on the quotient by the vanishing lattice is identity; consequently K is independent of that path representative.

In multiplicative cusp coordinates, complex conjugation reverses each angular circle. Thus an anti-holomorphic affine map with angular matrix P has valuation-lattice linear map -P=K: explicitly x -> u(q) conjugate(x)^K sends the angular cycles by -K=P, and the valuation vector by K followed by the translation given by the orders of u(q). A map extending biholomorphically across the fixed normal-crossing compactification must preserve its divisorial valuations and their incidence, hence the corresponding affine map of the prescribed height-one fan. The translation part changes vertices but not edge differences. This follows directly from the pullback of the monomial divisors in each smooth toric chart: pullback orders are the exponent matrix, and intersecting coordinate divisors give cones carried to cones. The periodic deck quotient preserves this local incidence calculation.

Every Kuhn edge difference is a vector with nonzero entries all equal to1 or all equal to-1. But K e2=(0,1,-1), which is not such a vector; -K e2 fails by the same mixed-sign check. Neither possible unimodular reflection preserves even the edge set, hence neither can extend over this fixed marked fan. This excludes an anti-biholomorphism between any two members, independently of the parameter, and therefore excludes antiholomorphic automorphisms and real structures.

The matrix identities, the one-dimensional solution space, both cusp transport identities and all edge-image tests are to be rerun in newly authored reflection_and_fan.py before comparing the printed proof. This argument does not claim a general obstruction for a different cusp marking or fan.
