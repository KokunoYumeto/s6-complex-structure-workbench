## UN.11: nearby cycles, exact signatures and occurrence

Resolve only the central divisor, using an embedded resolution which is an isomorphism over its already smooth disjoint branches. At an SNC point the equation is a unit times z₁^(N₁)…z_r^(N_r). Absorb the unit locally. The nearby fibre has d=gcd(N₁,…,N_r) components, each homotopy equivalent to (S¹)^(r−1). A positive base loop cyclically permutes the d components; its d-th power is a torus translation and acts trivially on cohomology. Thus for K=lcm_j N_j, the K-th power acts as the identity on every stalk of every R^qψQ, hence on those constructible sheaves themselves. The proper nearby-cycle spectral sequence

H^p(S,R^qψQ) ⇒ H^(p+q)(F,Q)

is equivariant for the actual geometric monodromy. Its total degree one has only (p,q)=(1,0),(0,1). The induced filtration therefore has two quotients killed by T^K−1, and (T^K−1)²=0 on H¹. Eigenvalues are roots of unity. If M is their common order, then T^M−1 and T^K−1 have the same nilpotent Jordan-block sizes in characteristic zero: on a generalized λ-block their respective first derivatives at λ are Mλ^(M−1) and Kλ^(K−1), both nonzero. Hence (T^M−1)²=0 and its rank is at most two. Orientation of the real four-torus gives det T=1.

The same local model gives stalk Lefschetz trace N_j at a smooth branch precisely when N_j divides k. At a point on at least two branches, each invariant connected torus has alternating trace (1−1)^(r−1)=0; nonfixed components give trace zero. A finite triangulation compatible with the central divisor and constructible nearby cycles makes the global alternating trace the sum of cell Euler contributions times these stalk traces. Properness identifies the nearby-cycle hypercohomology with the actual torus fibre. Therefore

det(1−T^k)=Σ_{n|k} n a_n.

Möbius inversion gives the exact a_n. The determinant depends only on eigenvalues, and for each cyclotomic block it depends only on gcd(k,M), since automorphisms of the cyclotomic field permute the primitive roots. Define the a_d on divisors d of M by inversion. The corresponding divisor sum has the same value at every k because it depends on gcd(k,M). Uniqueness of inversion then gives a_n=0 for n not dividing M. Every determinant is nonnegative: nonreal eigenvalues pair into |1−λ^k|², while the real factors are 0 or 2.

The degree-four cyclotomic possibilities use degrees 1,2,4. The multiplicity of −1 is even because det T=1, and the remaining dimension makes the multiplicity of +1 even. The degree-four irreducible possibilities are Φ₅,Φ₈,Φ₁₀,Φ₁₂; the degree-two ones are Φ₃,Φ₄,Φ₆. Enumerating their degree-four products gives exactly nineteen patterns. The fresh determinant/inversion computation in `cover_lattice_return_checks.py`, written before this complete proof exposure, has already checked all nineteen listed sequences and the averages. If a₁=0, then det(1−T)=0 and 1 is an eigenvalue, forcing every L_k and every a_n to vanish. The converse is immediate from a₁.

For the minimum-multiplicity assertion, a first exceptional divisor over a singular branch or branch intersection has multiplicity at least 2m₀. Every subsequent centre requiring embedded resolution lies on such an exceptional divisor or on at least two relevant branches, and again introduces multiplicity greater than m₀. No resolution centre is needed on the smooth isolated locus of a minimum-multiplicity original branch. Thus the multiplicity-m₀ open strata on the resolution are exactly the original smooth open branches of multiplicity m₀. Applying the trace formula at k=m₀ gives the stated equality and nonnegativity. If every original multiplicity exceeds one, then there is no multiplicity-one exceptional component either. Hence a₁=0 and the whole signature vanishes. In the irreducible case the multiplicity-m open stratum is exactly S_reg. For m>1 it has Euler zero; for m=1 its Euler number is det(1−T), whose eleven values are the table's first coefficients.

To realize every row, choose a finite-order automorphism g of an abelian surface with that characteristic polynomial. Products of elliptic curves with unit automorphisms give every product of degree-one and degree-two blocks. The Φ₅ and Φ₁₀ blocks are supplied by the Jacobian of y²=x⁵−1 and the order-five automorphism, with or without the hyperelliptic involution. For Φ₈ one can use E_i² with g(v,w)=(iw,v); for Φ₁₂ use E_ζ³² with g(v,w)=(−ζ₃w,v). Their real eigenvalues are exactly the four primitive roots of the asserted order. (Here E_ζ³ means the elliptic curve with ζ₃ multiplication; this descriptive notation has no exponent operation.) Each g preserves some ample class after summing its finite orbit.

Form the actual projective quotient (A×P¹)/〈(g,z↦ζ_nz)〉 and project to t=z^n. Over t≠0,∞ one orbit meets any chosen sheet once, giving an actual isomorphism of the fibre with A and connected general fibre. Only 0 and ∞ can be singular values. Resolve the quotient and its full special divisors projectively. Both supports have even Euler number by UN.1. A point blowup adds two. A point blowup followed by a smooth plane-quartic blowup in the new P² adds 2−4=−2; choose the quartic transverse to every boundary line. The SNC local coordinate calculation for a smooth centre transverse to the other components keeps SNC. These operations can bring each even Euler number to zero independently. If a marked support began smooth with Euler zero, use point, quartic, point to preserve zero while making its support reducible. All centres are vertical, so the actual monodromy and a_n sequence remain unchanged. This realizes all rows with exactly the two marked singular values and reducible SNC supports.

## UN.24: necessity, four quotient configurations and realization

Take the actual normalized root component E over the given normalization component, with cyclic stabilizer G. The quotient is birational to the original component. Pullback of the K3 holomorphic two-form through the resolved quotient graph is a nonzero holomorphic two-form on the smooth minimal model of E. If the K3 has algebraic dimension zero, so does E: the meromorphic function field of the finite quotient is the invariant field, and every meromorphic function upstairs satisfies its orbit polynomial over that field. UN.16 gives a torus or Hopf minimal model; UN.19 excludes the Hopf quotient because the actual quotient has the stated Hopf invariants. Thus E is a torus. UN.23 then makes the cyclic quotient symplectic with no eigenvalue one. The affine generator has a fixed point because 1−L is an isogeny; translation by a solution of (1−L)a=b makes it linear.

A finite symplectic linear map in complex dimension two has eigenvalues λ,λ^(−1). Its rank-four rational characteristic polynomial is (x²−2cosθ x+1)². Its x³ coefficient says 4cosθ is an integer p; its x² coefficient says p²/4+2 is an integer, so p is even. Therefore 2cosθ is an integer strictly between −2 and 2, or equals −2, giving orders 2,3,4,6 (order one is excluded). This is an independent exact integer argument for the order list.

The fixed-point count is |det(1−g)|. For order two there are sixteen fixed points and sixteen A₁ singularities. For order three there are nine fixed points and nine A₂ singularities. For order four, g fixes four points, giving four A₃ chains. The involution fixes sixteen; the other twelve have orbit size two, giving six A₁ chains. For order six, g fixes one point, giving A₅. Its square fixes nine, giving (9−1)/2=4 A₂ chains; its cube fixes sixteen, giving (16−1)/3=5 A₁ chains. The cyclic local action is (ζ^r,ζ^(−r)); its invariant ring and regular toric subdivision resolve it by the A-chain of length stabilizer-order minus one. The number of exceptional curves is thus 16,18,18,18. Burnside Euler averaging gives 8,6,6,6 for the singular quotient. These numbers will be rerun by new exact code.

For positive algebraic dimension, the non-torus root cases carry the actual equivariant elliptic fibration from UN.20/22; after quotient and resolution its connected general fibre remains elliptic because a rational general fibre would kill the K3 two-form. The map descends to the minimal K3: on a common resolution, K-nefness and the fibre intersection identity make every exceptional curve vertical. Its base is P¹ because H¹ of the base injects into the K3 H¹.

For a projective cyclic torus quotient K3, its exceptional configurations contain four pairwise disjoint (−2)-curves C₁,…,C₄. Choose an integral positive class H orthogonal to all exceptional curves by pulling back an ample quotient divisor. On a K3, H²=2d for a positive integer d. Four squares give d=Σb_i², so D=H−Σb_iC_i is a nonzero integral isotropic divisor class. Its intersection with a suitable positive ample class is positive after choosing its sign. Riemann–Roch makes D effective. Repeated reflection in a (−2)-curve with negative D-intersection preserves D²=0 and the positive cone while decreasing intersection with a fixed ample class by a positive integer; the process terminates at a nef effective isotropic class. Divide by its divisibility to make it primitive. A moving system exists by Riemann–Roch. Its fixed and moving parts are orthogonal to this nef isotropic class, so the Hodge index theorem makes their classes proportional to it. Primitivity forces no fixed part; the moving part has square zero, hence no base points. Its Stein factor is an elliptic fibration, since adjunction gives arithmetic genus one. This supplies the actual map in the remaining projective case. For algebraic dimension one, the algebraic reduction of a K3 is an elliptic map; this is the precise cited surface-classification input.

Occurrence uses exactly the quotient (A×P¹)/〈(g,ζ_n)〉 and its smooth resolution. The strict transform of A/g over zero is an actual normalization component whose minimal resolution is the required K3. The Euler-balancing operations from UN.4 act in vertical exceptional P² neighbourhoods and preserve that component's birational class. Apply them separately over zero and infinity. Properness and compactness are inherited from the finite quotient and proper modifications, and projectivity is retained when A is projective. This proves the entire occurrence assertion without changing the given torus or replacing its K3 quotient class.

## UN.32: removing the elliptic-cover alternative by an actual quotient

Continue with the actual root component E and its cyclic stabilizer G. Canonical pullback gives p_g(E)≥1, while UN.28 gives p_g(E)≤1. If its minimal model is a torus, UN.23 gives the conclusion. Otherwise UN.22/29 give an isotrivial elliptic fibration q:E→B with identity integral monodromy, only smooth multiple fibres, and χ(O_E)=0. The nonzero two-form and the coherent calculation give p_g(E)=g(B)=1 and q(E)=2, so B is elliptic. Its fibration is intrinsic in this branch, as the algebraic reduction or Iitaka map, and is G-equivariant.

The following local quotient computations are proved here directly. They use no consequence of UN.32 and no full invocation of UN.33, whose statement cites UN.32.

Let H be the kernel of G's action on B. On a regular fibre every h∈H acts affinely. The nonzero two-form pulled back from the K3 is H-invariant, and the base derivative is one, so its fibre linear part is one. Thus H acts by translations. At a smooth multiple fibre, use its actual local model

(F×Δ_z)/〈δ〉,   δ(v,z)=(v+τ,ζ_m z),   ord(τ)=m,

with base t=z^m. An H-action can be lifted so that it fixes z: multiply a lift by a suitable power of δ. Such a lift is a finite torsion translation on F, and these lifts form a finite translation group T. Quotient F by T. If τ̄ has order m′, the kernel of the residual δ-action on F/T rotates the disc with order m/m′. Quotient this kernel by w=z^(m/m′). The remaining free action has order m′ and base t=w^(m′). Therefore W=E/H is smooth and has an isotrivial elliptic fibration over B, still with identity monodromy and only smooth multiples. This also proves the precise extension of the quotient through every old multiple fibre.

The residual cyclic group Q=G/H acts faithfully on B, and W/Q is birational to the K3. Its quotient base is P¹. A generator of a cyclic automorphism group of an elliptic curve with rational quotient has nontrivial linear part α; 1−α is surjective, so translating a fixed point makes it linear. Its order is 2,3,4 or6, and every nonidentity stabilizer has nontrivial linear part.

On the smooth part of W the invariant two-form is dq∧dv in local translation coordinates, with the fixed nowhere-zero base differential understood. Identity monodromy makes the relative linear changes equal to one; translation changes dv by a base one-form, which disappears in the wedge. Thus the induced constant fibre character β of Q satisfies αβ=1. This equality holds for every stabilizer.

At a multiple fibre over a branch point, let a stabilizer generator have order s and nontrivial fibre character β of that same order. Lift it to the local root chart. The disc coordinate forces commutation with δ, hence βτ=τ. Since 1+β+…+β^(s−1)=0, one gets sτ=0 and m divides s. The affine map on the central elliptic curve has a fixed point because β−1 is an isogeny. Choose such a lift. Its s-th power is a deck transformation fixing that point; the free translation deck action forces that power to be the identity. If ρ is the lift's disc eigenvalue, then ρ^s=1 and ρ^m is a primitive s-th root, so gcd(m,s)=1. Consequently m=1. This rules out a multiple fibre over every branch point by exact local maps.

Away from those branch points the quotient base map is unramified, and an existing multiple fibre keeps its multiplicity in W/Q. The descended fibration on the minimal K3 has no multiple fibre: its canonical-bundle formula has K_B⊗L of degree −2+χ(O_K3)=0, and any multiple fibres would give a nonzero effective canonical divisor, contradicting K_K3=O. Birational modifications of fibres preserve their gcd multiplicity. Hence W has no multiple fibres anywhere.

W→B is now a principal elliptic bundle with fixed fibre F. Its integral characteristic class is c∈H²(B,Λ_F)=Λ_F. The actual Q-action identifies the pullback bundle by a degree-one base automorphism with the bundle whose fibre lattice is changed by β. On Čech translation cocycles this says βc=c. Since β≠1 has no fixed nonzero vector in Λ_F, c=0. Classification of principal real two-torus bundles therefore gives a topologically trivial bundle and the integral cohomology ring of T⁴. The surface has b₁=4, hence is Kähler. The Albanese construction used in UN.5 has degree one by that cohomology ring; it contracts no curve, since a rational curve would map constantly to B and cannot lie in an elliptic fibre. Thus W is a complex two-torus. Equivalently, the Kähler surface classification with κ=0 and b₁=4 gives the same conclusion.

The K3 two-form pulls back to a nonzero constant torus two-form on W, and Q preserves it. Thus Q acts symplectically. Its nontrivial generator has no eigenvalue one, because its two eigenvalues have product one and finite order; eigenvalue one would make the generator a translation, contrary to the fixed-point base action. Translating a fixed point makes it linear, and the order calculation above gives 2,3,4,6. The actual quotient W/Q is birational to the given K3, so uniqueness of a minimal K3 identifies the required minimal resolution. The occurrence direction is already furnished by the explicit quotient in UN.24. This completes the prereading derivation in every algebraic dimension.
