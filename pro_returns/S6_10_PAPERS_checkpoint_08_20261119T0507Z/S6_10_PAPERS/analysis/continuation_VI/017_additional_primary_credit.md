## The algebraic-dimension-zero foliation antecedent and its actual map

Brunella, *Foliations on complex projective surfaces*, arXiv:math/0212082v1, 5 December 2002, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0212082, §10 pp.28–30, was reopened. On pp.29–30 the algebraic-dimension-zero K3 case contracts the negative part of the foliation canonical class and takes a cyclic cover trivializing that class. The covering surface is a torus, and the foliation is the quotient of a constant torus foliation. This is a direct earlier source for the algebraic-dimension-zero K3 foliation conclusion.

Here is the exact connection to the limiting-field construction, recorded to avoid an unsupported comparison of scopes. In UN.16 the cyclic stabilizer G of the multiplicity-one root component E acts on the actual saturated lattice L of ambient vertical fields. Pullback sends s to a root-of-unity multiple of s, preserves the order along E, and therefore preserves L and sL. It induces a complex-linear action on the two-dimensional image L/sL in meromorphic tangent fields on E. A generator has finite order, so its minimal polynomial divides x^{|G|}−1, which has distinct roots; it has an eigenline. That eigenline gives a nonzero G-invariant rank-one meromorphic tangent direction on E.

On the open set where E→E/G is étale, the derivative identifies this invariant direction with a tangent line on E/G. The line descends because choosing a different sheet changes a chosen field by its nonzero eigencharacter and therefore leaves its one-dimensional span unchanged. On the common smooth open set of E/G and its K3 minimal resolution M, transport this line by the actual birational isomorphism. Extend it meromorphically across the complement and take its saturation in T_M. Clearing divisorial poles locally and removing common divisorial zero factors produces a rank-one coherent saturated subsheaf; its reflexive hull is a line bundle on the smooth surface, with only isolated singularities in the map to T_M. Every rank-one tangent distribution is integrable on its regular locus, so this is a holomorphic singular foliation on M. Thus when a(M)=0 the precise hypothesis of Brunella's §10 is fulfilled. This gives the promised morphism from the limit-field construction to the earlier foliation theorem. The present all-algebraic-dimensions result additionally proves, through the explicit H quotient and characteristic-class calculation in UN.32, that its elliptic-cover branch also supplies a torus quotient.
