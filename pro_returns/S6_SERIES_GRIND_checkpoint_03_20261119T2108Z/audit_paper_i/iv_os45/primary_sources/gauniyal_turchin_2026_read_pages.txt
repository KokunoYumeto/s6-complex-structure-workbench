               4.1. Invariants of immersions S 4k−1 # R6k−1
                  According to Smale [24], there is a bijection between the set (group) of regu-

               π4k−1 V6k−1,4k−1 , where V6k−1,4k−1 denotes the Stiefel manifold of (4k − 1)-frames in
               (6k − 1)-space. Given an immersion f : S 4k−1 # R6k−1 , let Ω(f ) ∈ π4k−1 V6k−1,4k−1
               denote its Smale invariant. In these dimensions, it is easier to understand Ω(f ) ratio-
               nally. Consider the fibre sequence S 2k → V6k−1,4k−1 → V6k−1,4k−2 , which induces
