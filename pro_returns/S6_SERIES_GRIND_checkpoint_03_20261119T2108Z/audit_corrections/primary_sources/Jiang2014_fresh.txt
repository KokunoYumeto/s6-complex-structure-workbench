1    Introduction
In this paper all manifolds under consideration are closed, oriented and
topological, unless otherwise stated. Moreover, all homeomorphisms and
di¤eomorphisms are to be orientation preserving. Given a positive integer n
let S n (resp. Dn+1 ) be the di¤eomorphism type of the unit n–sphere (resp.
(n + 1)–disk) in (n + 1)–dimensional Euclidean space Rn+1 .

De…nition 1.1. A circle action S 1 M ! M on a manifold M is called
regular if this action is free and the orbit space N := M=S 1 (with quotient
topology) is a manifold.
    Similarly, a smooth circle action S 1 M ! M on a smooth manifold M
is called regular if this action is free (see [26, p.38, Proposition 5.2]).

   For a given manifold M one can ask
Problem 1.2. Does M admit a regular circle action?
