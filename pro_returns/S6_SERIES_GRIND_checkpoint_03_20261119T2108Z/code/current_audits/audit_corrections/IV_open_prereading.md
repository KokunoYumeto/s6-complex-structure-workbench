## E6 base map: a direct rational invariant calculation

Δ=−16t²[27t²+(4−18r)t+r²(4r−1)],

j_r(t)=−256(r²−3t)³/{t²[27t²+(4−18r)t+r²(4r−1)]}.

At t=r²/3 the bracket is r²(3r−1)²/3. Thus on r∉{0,1/4,1/3}, the divisor of j has a unique zero of order three at r²/3 and a unique zero of order one at infinity; its unique double pole is at zero, while the selected quadratic has two distinct nonzero roots. A Möbius symmetry of this actual j-function fixes infinity, zero and r²/3, hence is the identity. This proves the required base-rigidity conclusion once the actual induced map on the primitive elliptic quotient has been constructed. At r=0 the cancelled rational function is 6912/(27t+4), of degree one, so its symmetry group is again trivial. This route includes the extra P_r=0 and Q_r=0 points excluded from K109, and explains the broader domain in K113/K114 without a genericity assumption on those auxiliary polynomials.

The missing geometric bridge at this point is the intrinsic lift of an arbitrary isomorphism to the genuine cyclic cover and elliptic quotient. No source/base conclusion is credited until that bridge is supplied.

## Fibre linear parts and endpoint obstructions

A holomorphic torus isomorphism is a translation followed by a complex-linear map of its lattice. After base rigidity, the linear lattice map commutes with the monodromies. The expected centralizer consists of scalar maps and an invariant-vector/covector rank-one shear. A nonzero real rank-one shear cannot be complex linear, since a complex-linear map has even real rank. Integral invertibility then leaves the two signs. This is a proof strategy, not a completed centralizer calculation: the actual matrices and integral kernel must be computed.

For the negative sign, preservation of a holomorphic root meridian of order m sends its invariant norm n to −n up to an m-multiple. Hence m divides 2n. With n=±1, all m≥3 are excluded. The reduced M6 cap of order two remains: its exclusion requires the complete ordinary/annular reference, including the arbitrary added period, rather than the finite norm equation alone. The full obstruction must retain n+mν; its nonzero odd value for m=2,n=±1 is the intended last obstruction. The reference's actual cochain and the source of that equality have not yet been derived here.

For a positive linear part, a fibrewise lift descends to translation by a section of the original elliptic surface. Its Mordell–Weil rank is one in the stated E6+A1 fibre configuration. The height of G is 2−4/3−1/2=1/6; the root discriminant is six, so the height/discriminant formula and primitivity must identify all section translations as integers times G. The existence and scalar ambiguity of the genuine line lift must then be calculated by Miller divisors, through both root caps and the full annular reference. An arbitrary physical coefficient extending on the compact completed object is constant. This yields C* only after every possible residual section translation has been tested.
