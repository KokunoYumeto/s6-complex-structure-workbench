# Superseded P4.9 status passage

The following passage described the state before P4.21–26 and P4R.1–9. It is retained as a record of the work. Its numerical-sign gap is closed by P4.26; equality/classification remains under investigation.

```latex
\begin{statement}{P4.9}{Unresolved for arbitrary nonnormal irreducible supports}
The preceding proof establishes the full assertion of Part 4.2 for
normal-crossings supports. P4.12 also proves that every normal
irreducible support is smooth. The unproved case is a nonnormal
irreducible support, without a normal-crossings hypothesis. Its
normalization may be singular, and its normalization map may fail
to be an immersion along a curve. The vector-bundle sequence and
nodal conductor count in P4.6 then require additional local terms.
\end{statement}
We have checked CDP 2020, Lemma 4.6, Propositions 4.7, 4.11 and
7.3, separating their local conductor identities from their disputed
global restriction argument. We have not used that final restriction
argument. Kamenova's Theorem 3.1 requires a semistable Kähler total
space and trivial canonical bundle. Schröer--Siebert's Propositions
2.4--2.6 classify admissible normal-crossings surfaces with trivial
canonical bundle. Their precise hypotheses do not provide the missing
comparison map in the arbitrary nonnormal case. The canonical-torsion
and resolution argument P4.12 reduces the minimal resolved
normalization to rational, irrational ruled, or class VII. It does
not yet bound the ruled genus, the singular conductor contribution,
or the class-VII normalization invariants. These are the remaining
obligations; failure of an attempted proof is not a counterexample.
```
