## Paper V

### Title

The $(3,4,\infty)$ family of complex six-spheres. V. Complex fourfolds from the family

### Abstract

We construct compact complex fourfolds diffeomorphic, with their complex orientation, to $3\mathbb{HP}^2\#(-\mathbb{HP}^2)$. They form a family of complex three-tori over $\mathbb P^1$ with monodromy in $GL_6(\mathbb Z)$: two multiple fibres, of multiplicities five and four, and one Kuhn cusp. Its periods come from the exterior square of the genus-two pencil $y^2=x^5+\lambda^3x+\lambda^4$. The family is parametrized exactly by the half-plane $\operatorname{Im}\xi>c_*$, where $c_*\approx1.09454$ is minus the minimum of an explicit real-analytic period function. We compute the Hodge numbers, the tangent cohomology (the Kuranishi germ is smooth of dimension one) and the automorphism group $\mathbb C\times\mathbb Z/2$, and we show that the family extends analytically across no boundary point of its parameter domain. We also construct a compact complex fourfold outside Fujiki's class $\mathcal C$ as a small crepant resolution of a Weierstrass model, bimeromorphic to the fibre product of a complex six-sphere with an elliptic surface; give an effective classification of the integral monodromy data of this kind that yield three-connected eight-manifolds, all of which are diffeomorphic to $3\mathbb{HP}^2\#(-\mathbb{HP}^2)$; and describe shifted fibre products of complex dimension five.

## Paper I

### Title

The $(3,4,\infty)$ family of complex six-spheres. I. Periods, moduli and the two ends

### Abstract

We determine the periods, moduli and two limiting regimes of the $(3,4,\infty)$ family of complex six-spheres. A theta expression for its elliptic period gives the exact parameter disc $0<|w|<216$. Integral affine and cusp comparison maps classify the discrete data, parameter identifications and real structures, including the fixed three-spheres. We compute the integral monodromy group, its maximal domain of proper discontinuity and the algebraic dimension of every smooth fibre. Logarithmic direct images and explicit relative differential modules determine the Dolbeault, Bott–Chern, Aeppli and tangent cohomology, all Picard twists and the deformation parameter. Global Dolbeault representatives extend through the multiple fibres and cusp. At the puncture, the corner monodromy obstructs every proper Hausdorff analytic filling after any finite parameter cover; the analogous obstruction holds for the coprime higher-multiplicity sphere families. At the outer boundary, the intrinsic Albanese invariant obstructs continuation, while the dual period lattices determine the local Hausdorff reflection and all additive limits. The arithmetic norm satisfies exact division and height identities; we describe its torsion values, critical points and level curves.

## Paper II

### Title

The $(3,4,\infty)$ family of complex six-spheres. II. Torus-fibred threefolds and the sign theorem

### Abstract

We study holomorphic fibrations of compact complex threefolds with vanishing first and second Betti numbers. For a torus general fibre, every degeneration has nonnegative Euler number, with arbitrary multiplicities and support singularities, and the total Euler number is zero or two. We describe the equality supports and the equality forced by identity monodromy, through integral component counts, local parity, conductor maps and compact constructions. A local nodal family gives a counterexample to a vanishing implication that omits the cotangent-torsion connecting map. Under the integral Campana--Demailly--Peternell hypotheses we prove the exact normalization-Picard criterion for generic tangent-cohomology vanishing outside degree one. We compute tangent twists and deformation spaces, prove persistence of the fibration in the stated families, and determine the two-turn covering parameter of the Liu--Rollenske family. We construct a compact torus fibration with three doubled nodal supports and elliptic ruled normalizations. A flat-kernel and conic two-jet calculation gives a nonempty open family of twelve-tooth smoothings with kernel $\mathcal O(-8)\oplus\mathcal O(-9)^2$ and Hodge numbers $h^{1,1}=49$, $h^{1,2}=48$. We state the remaining splitting problem for general and special comb members.

## Paper III

### Title

The $(3,4,\infty)$ family of complex six-spheres. III. Maps and the resulting manifolds

### Abstract

We compute the homotopy classes of three maps associated with the $(3,4,\infty)$ family of complex six-spheres. The torus quotient represents the element of order two in $\pi_6(S^3)$, the holomorphic map to $\mathbb P^1$ is null-homotopic, and the cusp map that keeps the angular coordinate represents the nonzero element of $\pi_6(S^4)$. Normal framings and integral attaching maps give these classes and a finite cell model. For every cyclic universal cover we calculate the integral cohomology ring and the complete smooth classification invariant. We classify the natural section-line sphere fillings over twenty-one elliptic profiles: there are $59$ marked patterns on $50$ initial data, with twelve profiles admitting unbounded multiplicity. We also determine weighted smooth types and the homology of every free affine cap over the elliptic surface with fibres $IV^*,II,I_2$, including its additional three-primary torsion. A weighted member over the surface with fibres $I_8,4I_1$ has zero refined Pontryagin class, as follows from an embedded Hopf sphere and its critical-cycle linking number. The local Kummer cover has no smooth compact global extension at degree greater than one; an explicit finite flat normal extension has additional components above the cusp. Finally, moving the zero of the line-bundle linearization gives a conifold smoothing diffeomorphic to $S^3\times S^3$, of algebraic dimension one.
