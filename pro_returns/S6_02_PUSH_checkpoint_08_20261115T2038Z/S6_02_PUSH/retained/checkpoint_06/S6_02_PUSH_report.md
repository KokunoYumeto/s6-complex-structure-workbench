## E.2.9

The elementary vanishing is valid on a compact reduced irreducible analytic space: the decreasing finite-dimensional section spaces stabilize; their intersection is zero by Krull intersection at a point of the nonempty effective Cartier divisor and analytic continuation on the irreducible normalization. Flatness over the smooth curve follows from torsion-freeness over its local DVR. A Cartier fibre is Cohen–Macaulay and Gorenstein. The dualizing bundle is K_X restricted to the fibre, up to the harmless constant target tangent factor; therefore compact analytic Serre duality yields exactly the stated H2/H0 pairing for every fibre, including its nilpotents.

The bad sets for H2 are closed by upper semicontinuity and nested by injectivity of multiplication on dual sections. Their empty intersection and compactness of P1 give a common N.
