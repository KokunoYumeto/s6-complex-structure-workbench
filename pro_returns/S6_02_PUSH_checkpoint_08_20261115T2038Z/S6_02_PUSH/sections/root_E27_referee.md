# Independent root check of the E.2.7 working draft

The draft was read on 15 November 2026 before checkpoint05. It is not yet input into the draft19 PDF or included in its112-statement registry. The following review accepts its conditional conclusion; integration is planned after the checkpoint freeze.

The extra hypothesis is essential: the intrinsic maps of the punctured known family extend to a relative holomorphic map whose central restriction is nonconstant. E.2.6 supplies this for positive canonical slope. The result does not assert that every smooth specialization has that property.

On a proper submersion to the disc, the rank-deficient locus of the relative fibration is analytic. Its proper image meets the central target curve in finitely many critical values, so it has no vertical target-curve component. The three labelled punctured critical branches extend as degree-one finite curves after normalization. Collisions cause poles in the cross-ratio gauge but do not cause an essential singularity. A fixed central regular target value therefore defines an actual proper smooth surface family over the full disc. Its noncentral fibres are the ordinary tori, so its H1 monodromy is the identity.

The corresponding marked base coordinate is meromorphic. Its small loop is null-homotopic or conjugate to a power of one peripheral generator. The classifying parameter is well defined by the fixed-data shift group2kl Z; translations in the family comparisons act trivially on torus H1. A zero of order d would contribute S_{-h}, h=2kld>0. The two finite peripheral matrices commute with this shear and have orders3 and4, so a finite peripheral power cannot cancel it. At the cusp, N(u)=w and N(gamma)=-delta. The identity I+nN-hE first forces n=0 on u and then h=0 on gamma. Conjugation does not affect this argument because the monodromy group preserves E. Thus a zero parameter is impossible.

Boundedness supplies a holomorphic extension of the normalized period, and the maximum-modulus principle excludes a value on the outer circle. This conclusion does not require a separatedness theorem. An interior period limit still does not identify the central complex manifold with the existing model at that period.
