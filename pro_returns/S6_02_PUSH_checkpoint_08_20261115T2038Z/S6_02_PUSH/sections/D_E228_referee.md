# Independent referee: E.2.8

Referee: boundary_d. Date: 2026-11-15. Outcome: PASS at the stated necessary-condition scope.

Alexander duality and the graph-base Leray sequence give r=s−1+dim(H1(F)^mon), with the invariant subspace retained. When b1(F)=1, r≥s forces equality, reduced fibre irreducibility, and trivial H1 monodromy. Adding a regular value if necessary handles an empty original discriminant.

A horizontal prime intersects every fibre and cannot contain its irreducible reduced support. Its defining section is a non-zero-divisor even at local self-branches: vanishing on one branch would pull back to vanishing on an open set of the irreducible normalization and hence identically. Its pullback to a Kähler resolution is therefore a nonzero effective divisor, although its integral Chern class is zero by H2(S6,Z)=0. Positivity gives the contradiction. Passing first to the reduced support handles nonreduced fibres.

For D<0, the canonical-root identity gives a nonzero effective antipluricanonical power with c1 zero. A positive pluricanonical section would multiply with it to a nonzero constant possessing zeros. Thus the general fibre has Kodaira dimension minus infinity and is non-Kähler; the stated surface-classification consequence b1=1 is appropriate. The result does not exclude non-Kähler singular-fibre normalizations and does not close arbitrary deformation-chain invariance.

The tempting stronger argument that an elliptic fibre of a resolved horizontal divisor must remain elliptic on the fibre normalization is not justified: exceptional elliptic curves may be contracted. No such assertion appears in the accepted source.
