# Independent signed-cap audit

The separate signed comparison, subsequently numbered C.3.22, passes independently of any proposed value of the actual delta-line square.

## Calibration and corrected diagnostic

An intermediate sign audit incorrectly treated a boundary frame as the same transition as a coordinate clutch. That produced a possible J=2 instead of the anticipated cancellation. This was a useful diagnostic, but the two transitions are inverses; no orientation may be selected merely to obtain a desired value.

The correction was checked directly with connections. Write the boundary frame as f=t B in a global frame. The connection flat in f has matrix -dB B^(-1). With p1(F)=-tr(F wedge F)/(8 pi^2), the corresponding relative boundary transgression is minus tr(B^(-1)dB)^3/(24 pi^2). By contrast, a mapping-torus coordinate clutch K has endpoint connection K^(-1)dK. Interpolating the connection t K^(-1)dK on an interval gives the positive transgression tr(K^(-1)dK)^3/(24 pi^2). The elementary line-bundle calibration agrees: the boundary frame exp(i n theta) on a disc has relative c1=n, while the same coordinate clutch on a mapping torus oriented with the interval first gives -n.

In D's convention t=f_aff A, the frame matrix is B=A^(-1). Since the four-manifold boundary is negative the ordered gamma-u-w torus, its relative Pontryagin number is -a0, where a0 is the ordered-torus period of the coordinate-clutch suspension of A. The annular derivative clutch is A(psi p)^(-1)A(p). Its relative trace is -dchi wedge beta. Pairing with the actual Euler curvature dg wedge da, with chi decreasing outward, also gives -a0. Thus the mapping-torus Pontryagin pairing equals the relative frame defect, with no independent sign choice.

The possible additional line-curvature term vanishes: it pairs the closed degree-three clutching class with the exact two-form Delta=deta on the closed double DP. The same geometric cut-and-reglue bordism carries both the stable tangent bundle and the actual line bundle. Consequently its one overall boundary sign multiplies both p1*x and x^3. The resulting formula is

J = epsilon_cap * (p_rel - 12*s_delta)/24.

This audit proves the sign relation only. The identification and evaluation of s_delta require the separate actual-line argument and are not used anywhere in the calibration.
