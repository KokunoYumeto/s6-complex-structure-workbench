# Root referee of A.2.5 and A.4.9

Date: 15 November 2026. Both proposed statements were read in full and accepted within their explicitly marked finite toroidal category.

For A.2.5, the new projective-three-space component supplies an actual counterexample to the proposed universal blow-up shape: a degree-one holomorphic map to R would inject its rank11 second cohomology into rank1. The replacement algorithm uses a finite marked strict cone complex and ordinary smooth stars and inverse stars. Its termination is supplied by weak factorization of each proper subdivision map. It does not invoke strong factorization. The chart substitutions determine multiplicities and exceptional projective bundles, and the cotangent-complex formulation correctly handles nonreduced central equations.

For A.4.9, the exceptional Gysin maps and powers of the tautological class give an integral derived sheaf map whose stalk basis is1,−ξ,…,−ξ^(c−1). Thus the splitting follows from a stalkwise quasi-isomorphism, not from equality of ranks. Proper base change restricts it to the actual inverse-image central support, including horizontal centres. The alternating local-branch resolution is integral; its orientation local system records branch permutations without division by a factorial. The explicit coordinate-projective-space restrictions explain the same unit cancellations in the normalization resolution. This is a direct central-space computation and is independent of first computing cohomology of the ambient neighbourhood.

The resulting finite algorithm supplies the requested second computation for arbitrary input fans. The proof does not claim an applicable polarized limiting mixed Hodge structure: the separately stated non-Kähler limitation of Clemens–Schmid remains necessary.
