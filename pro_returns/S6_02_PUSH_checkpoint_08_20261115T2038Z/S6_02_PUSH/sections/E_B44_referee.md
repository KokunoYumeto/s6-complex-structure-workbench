# Independent referee note on B.4.4

**Verdict: the final written proof in `B_marked_map_extension.tex` is valid in its stated meromorphic scope.** It proves extension of the marked map and a proper projective graph modification. It does not prove extension of any punctured comparison with an auxiliary threefold model.

The relative line bundle restricts to K⁻² on each smooth fibre. The audited canonical formula makes this f*O(1), and connected fibres give exactly two global sections. Constant h⁰ on the punctured disc supplies Grauert base change there. The direct image across zero is coherent and torsion-free, hence locally free of rank two; a frame gives two holomorphic evaluation sections. This does not require base change at the central fibre.

After removing the common divisorial factor in the regular local ring, the graph incidence equation in a neighbourhood U times the whole P¹ has a unique dominating component, the closure of its graph on the dense base-point-free open subset of U. It is locally a hypersurface. It can have more than one local analytic branch at a singular graph point; the proof does not require local irreducibility there. The stated maximal-Jacobian-minor construction detects exactly the nonsubmersion locus, including singular graph points, because it is applied to the hypersurface equation together with the two target coordinates in a smooth ambient space.

Proper direct image of the coherent structure sheaf of this bad locus gives its analytic image. The constant section 1 shows equality of support and image, without assuming cohomology-and-base-change for that sheaf. After removing vertical curve components, the remaining curve is proper and quasi-finite over the disc, hence finite. Intrinsic labels (multiplicities three and four and the reduced cusp fibre) prevent permutation of the three punctured branches. Their normalizations therefore have degree one and extend each critical value meromorphically through zero, even when values collide.

The cross-ratio projective transformation consequently has meromorphic coefficients and restores precisely the original marked fibration. Clearing powers of the parameter constructs its graph by the same two-section method. Its projection is proper projective and is an isomorphism over the punctured disc. No resolution, Kähler hypothesis, central base-change isomorphism, or Moishezon assertion is used. No remaining gap was found.
