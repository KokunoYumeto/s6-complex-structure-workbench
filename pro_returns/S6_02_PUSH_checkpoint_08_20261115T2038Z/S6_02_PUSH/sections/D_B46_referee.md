# Independent referee of B.4.6

Date: 2026-11-15. Source: `sections/ab_push_kato_caps.tex`. **Verdict: passes with exactly its stated cap and overlap restrictions.**

The grouped closed-cover resolution is valid locally: each block ideal is a product of a disjoint set of normal-crossing coordinate factors. On each monomial the alternating complex is the augmented simplex on those block ideals which do not kill the monomial. A coefficientwise contraction with coefficients zero or plus/minus one preserves convergence, so the argument is valid for convergent analytic power series as well as polynomials. The same stalk argument works for constants. All compact reduced intersections have only constant holomorphic functions on connected components.

The spectral-sequence comparison correctly uses surjectivity on block degree-one cohomology and injectivity on pairwise overlaps. These imply surjectivity on the E2 kernel in bidegree (0,1); the isomorphism in bidegree (2,0) then preserves d2-closedness. The five-term sequences finish the argument. The grouped horizontal pinching is necessary and is correctly used: the conductor itself would not have the required injective comparison, whereas 1−sigma* is invertible on its degree-one groups and the pinched block's surviving class comes from the identical constant-function difference map on both sides.

I independently opened the published primary PDF of Istrati, Otiman, Pontecorvo, and Ruggiero (2022), *Toric Kato manifolds*, Journal de l'École polytechnique — Mathématiques 9, 1347–1395, DOI 10.5802/jep.208, <https://www.numdam.org/item/JEP_2022__9__1347_0.pdf>. Proposition 8.4 and Theorem 8.5 supply h10=0 and h01=1 in the asserted hyperbolic case. The proof of Theorem 8.8 on page 1380 explicitly identifies the normalized invariant divisor components as compact toric varieties or hyperbolic toric Kato manifolds. Theorem 5.2 gives rational invariant curves in that case. These support precisely the normalized-overlap calculation in the manuscript.

The exact sequence from locally exact holomorphic one-forms makes the comparison H1(C)→H1(O) injective when there are no global holomorphic one-forms; combined with the source dimensions it is an isomorphism for the hyperbolic Kato blocks and surfaces. The ordered branch resolution then gives the required injectivity for their specified overlaps. No assumption that a component's fundamental-group generator survives in the union is made, and no parabolic or unrestricted cusp case is claimed.
