Primary documents actually opened and read:

1. Lin, C.-S., & Wang, C.-L. (2010). Elliptic functions, Green functions and the mean field equations on tori. *Annals of Mathematics, 172*(2), 911–954. https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2010.172.911 . The previously read Theorem 1.3 counts critical points in the elliptic variable for each fixed modulus. The journal landing page was reopened at https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2010/172-2/p03 . This does not determine zeros of both factors in D.4.4 along wp_tau(mu(tau))=0.
2. Chen, Z., Kuo, T.-J., & Lin, C.-S. (2019). Simple zero property of some holomorphic functions on the moduli space of tori. *Science China Mathematics, 62*(11), 2089–2102. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11425-018-9355-0 . Opened publisher PDF: https://files.sciengine.com/pdfs/1674-7283/D32F7CA22FD34AC2AB4CEDD492E051EB.pdf . Read Theorems 1.1–1.4, their definitions (1.2)–(1.3), and Appendix A, equations (A.1)–(A.3), Propositions A.1–A.2. Their eta1 equals our E=pi²E2/3. Their classical derivative formulas agree exactly with our independently derived wp_tau and g2_tau. Their holomorphic functions f_{k,C} and the multiple-Green Hessian curves are different from our real-analytic factor F=3ZP−2g2 on the zero section of wp. No theorem from that paper was substituted for the finite interval proof.
3. Chen, Z., Fu, E., & Lin, C.-S. (2025a). *Generic non-degeneracy of critical points of multiple Green functions on torus and applications to curvature equations* [Preprint, version 1, 10 March 2025]. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2503.06935 . Opened https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.06935 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.06935 ; read introduction and Theorem 1.1. That theorem gives a measure-zero exceptional set for degeneracy of critical points of G_n at fixed modulus. It does not exclude all critical moduli for the specific composite height function.
4. Chen, Z., Fu, E., & Lin, C.-S. (2025b). *Green functions, Hitchin's formula and curvature equations on tori* [Preprint, version 2, 20 October 2025]. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2508.17604 . Opened https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17604 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.17604 ; read Theorems A and B, Theorem 1.2, its proof, and the nearby definitions. Theorem 1.2 proves nondegeneracy of the nontrivial critical pair of a fixed torus Green function when that pair exists. It neither counts intersections with wp=0 as the modulus varies nor handles the second modulus-gradient factor.
