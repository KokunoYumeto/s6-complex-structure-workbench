The pinned SymPy routine forms the 64 commutator equations and finds a nullspace by row reduction. The prerelease Fraction routine also uses elimination, so that centralizer calculation alone is not a different algorithm.

Construct the 16 by 16 integral Gram matrix G=A^t A of the 64 by 16 commutator matrix, multiply powers and compute all 16 exact traces, then obtain the characteristic coefficients by Newton identities. This program makes no elimination, linear-solver, nullspace or numerical-eigenvalue call.

The characteristic polynomial has a zero of exact order two: its coefficient of z^2 is nonzero and its last two coefficients vanish. G is real symmetric, hence diagonalizable, so rank(G)=14. The exact identity v^tGv=sum_j(Av)_j^2 proves ker(G)=ker(A). Rational and real ranks of an integer matrix agree because the nonzero minors are the same. Direct substitution verifies the two independent generators I,E41 for homology and I,E14 for cohomology; dimension two makes these bases.

The original prerelease explicit coordinate parameterizations, norm-column generation and quotient maps already verify the integral fixed/norm/image lattices by a method distinct from the old Hermite/Smith reductions.

The exact degree-two coefficients are 1,864,673,503,312 and 783,058,281,856. Both degree-one and constant coefficients vanish. Both centralizer dimensions are therefore exactly two.
