\begin{proposition}
\label{fill:rankone}
At the $0$ and at $\alpha_+$, the punctured torus family has
a proper filling with smooth total space and a one-component
rank-one semiabelic central fiber $W$. The section $e$
extends across this filling, and specialization induces integral isomorphisms
\[
 \specialize^k:H^k(W;\Z)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^k(X_q;\Z)^T,
 \qquad 0\leq k\leq4.
\]
\end{proposition}
