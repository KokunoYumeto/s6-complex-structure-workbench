\begin{theorem}\label{intro:main}
For every sufficiently small nonzero $\lambda$, the construction gives
a compact, smooth, simply connected complex threefold $X_2(\lambda)$
with integral homology
\[
 H_k(X_2(\lambda);\Z)\simeq
 \begin{cases}
 \Z,&k=0,6,\\
 \Z/2,&k=2,3,\\
 0,&k=1,4,5.
 \end{cases}
\]
In particular, $X_2(\lambda)$ is a complex rational homology six-sphere.
\end{theorem}
