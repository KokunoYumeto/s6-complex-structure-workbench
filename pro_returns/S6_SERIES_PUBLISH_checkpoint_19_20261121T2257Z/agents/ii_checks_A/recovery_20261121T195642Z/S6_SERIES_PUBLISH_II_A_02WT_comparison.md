## Item 7: the rational curve graph and strict positivity

In the remaining case, T and D_red are connected and simply connected. The normalization realizes S as T with each finite fibre of D_red→C identified. The continuous quotient is a homeomorphism because the original finite normalization is proper and both spaces are compact Hausdorff. Triangulate the pair and the finite curve map; D_red⊂T is a cofibration, so the pushout has the homotopy-pushout fundamental group. Van Kampen gives π1(S)=π1(T)*_(π1(D_red))π1(C)=π1(C).

Every component of C is the finite nonconstant image of a rational component of D_red; the induced map of smooth normalizations is a nonconstant holomorphic map P1→C_i^ν, so Riemann–Hurwitz forces genus zero. The point-identification graph gives π1(C)=F_b. P4R.1 gives a finite-index abelian subgroup of π1(S), hence of this exact group π1(C). The Schreier rank formula, or the prerelease presentation-deficiency argument, shows b≤1: a finite-index subgroup of F_b has rank 1+d(b−1), which is at most one for a free abelian free group. The exact incidence count is e(C)=c+1−b≥c≥1. Finally T minus D_red identifies with S_reg, P4.13 gives e(S_reg)≥0, and additivity gives e(S)>0.
