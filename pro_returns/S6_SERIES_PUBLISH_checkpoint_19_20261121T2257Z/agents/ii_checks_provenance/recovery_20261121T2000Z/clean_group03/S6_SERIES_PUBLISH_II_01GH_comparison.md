## Hesse surface and smooth fibre products

For the pencil F=x^3+y^3+z^3-3txyz, a finite singular member has x^2=tyz, y^2=txz and z^2=txy. A zero coordinate would force all three to be zero. Multiplication of the three equations therefore gives t^3=1. At t=1 the three factors are x+y+z, x+omega*y+omega^2*z, and x+omega^2*y+omega*z. Replacing z by t*z transports this factorization to every t^3=1. The member at infinity is xyz=0. Each is a triangle with distinct transverse vertices. The nine base points lie on xyz=0 and x^3+y^3+z^3=0. At a base point with x=0, the gradients of the two pencil generators are (0,3y^2,3z^2) and (yz,0,0); both y,z are nonzero. Thus the generators are independent local coordinates. One blowup resolves the pencil there and produces a section. The same calculation applies at the other two coordinate axes. The triangle vertices are not base points. This proves the smooth rational surface and its four reduced I3 fibres directly.

The primary publisher edition of Artebani–Dolgachev, Section 2, pp.238–241, was also read after release. It gives the exact pencil, the nine base points, the Weierstrass coefficients and j-map, and the blowup surface with four I3 fibres. Its parameter lambda corresponds to this proof's parameter by lambda=-3t. Its nonconstant j-map therefore gives the nonconstant elliptic moduli map required here. In characteristic zero its differential is nonzero outside a finite set. The pullback of the second surface along phi(t)=1/(t-4) has critical set phi^{-1}({1,omega,omega^2,infinity})={5,4+omega^2,4+omega,4}. The sets are disjoint. At every point of the fibre product, at least one surface-to-curve differential is surjective; the difference of the two differentials cuts out a smooth threefold in the smooth product. Projectivity follows from the closed fibre-product embedding. The general fibre is the product of the two elliptic curves under the two projections. On its tangent space the Kodaira–Spencer tensor is block diagonal, with the two nonzero elliptic entries, hence has rank two.

The finite double cover C_s is defined by the algebra O plus O(-3), whose second generator has square equal to the displayed sextic. In the infinity chart the exact transition is y_infinity=t^{-3}y. The six roots remain distinct on a sufficiently small parameter disc. At branch points its local equation is y^2=u times a unit, so the curve is smooth; infinity is unramified and smooth. The branch values avoid the critical sets of the threefold. Thus the fibre product with this cover is smooth both over the branch points, where the threefold map is a submersion, and over the critical values, where the cover is étale. These equations give a proper smooth family over the parameter disc. The connected general torus fibre is preserved by the projection. A regular unramified point away from the finite zero sets of the two elliptic moduli derivatives supplies the stated rank-two value.

The genus-two canonical system gives the hyperelliptic projection: its basis dt/y and t*dt/y has ratio t. ## Exact coherent and tangent-sheaf computations

On the blowup of P2, let H be the pullback of a line and E_i the nine exceptional divisors. The fibre class is F=3H-sum(E_i), so K_S=-F and omega_{S/P1}=O(F)=pi^*O(1). Smooth elliptic fibres and reduced triangles both have h^0(O)=h^1(O)=1. The latter follows from the normalization exact sequence: three copies of P1, with differences of values at the three nodes, have H^0 kernel dimension one and H^1 cokernel dimension one. Cohomology and base change and relative duality therefore give pi_*O=O and R^1 pi_*O=O(-1). The base automorphism used for the second surface preserves O(1).

Relative Künneth is applied to the actual Cartesian square of the two flat elliptic-surface maps. Their direct-image cohomology sheaves are locally free, so tensoring has no Tor terms. Its cohomology is O, O(-1) plus O(-1), O(-2) in relative degrees 0,1,2. Flat base change along C_0->P1 transports these sheaves to O_C, L^{-1} plus L^{-1}, L^{-2}. This proves the displayed direct-image formula with its two separate summands.

On a smooth elliptic fibre, the relative invariant vector is dual to the relative canonical form. At a node with map t=xy, a vector a*partial_x+b*partial_y lies in the relative kernel exactly when ay+bx=0. In the regular local ring, y divides b; writing b=-yc then gives a=xc. Thus the kernel is freely generated by x*partial_x-y*partial_y. The relative dualizing form dx/x=-dy/y pairs with this generator to 1. These local pairings glue T_{S/P1}=omega_{S/P1}^{-1}. Under the Cartesian projections, a relative tangent vector to the product is exactly a pair of relative tangent vectors to its two factors. Hence T_{Y/C}=g^*(L^{-1} plus L^{-1}), including at the nodes.

The double-cover canonical formula is also obtained by its local differential: dt/y transforms as a section of pi_C^*(O(-2) tensor O(3)), so K_C=L. For each integer a, the exact finite map gives pi_{C*}L^{-a}=O(-a) plus O(-a-3). Its two direct summands are represented by 1 and y with the transition above. The two-chart Laurent Čech complex for O(d) sends (f(t),g(1/t)) to f(t)-t^d*g(1/t). The first chart spans exponents n>=0 and the second exponents n<=d. The kernel has one basis vector for each 0<=n<=d; the cokernel has one for each d<n<0. Every remaining exponent has a unique chart preimage, giving an explicit contracting pair. Thus this determines all dimensions, including the infinite tails, and gives h(L^0)=(1,2), h(L^{-1})=(0,3), h(L^{-2})=(0,5), h(L^{-3})=(0,7).

The Leray sequence has only the two base columns p=0,1, so all its higher differentials vanish. For a pullback L^{-a}, the three relative rows have multiplicities 1,2,1 and exponents -a,-a-1,-a-2. Summing degrees p+q gives respectively (1,2,6,5) for O_Y, (0,3,10,7) for g^*T_C=g^*L^{-1}, and twice the latter for T_{Y/C}. The exact dimensions are therefore (0,6,20,14) in the last row. All higher cohomology vanishes by the base and relative dimensions. The same computation gives H^0(T_C tensor R^1g_*O)=0 and the three-dimensional isomorphism in the proof.

Finally, differentiating the exact source and target transition identities for g_s gives dg(theta_Y)=g^*theta_C. The Leray edge map in the printed input E5 is injective; the preceding dimensions also prove it is an isomorphism here. The nonzero class obstructs extension with fixed target. This agrees with the independently frozen argument and proves the printed statement without changing it.
