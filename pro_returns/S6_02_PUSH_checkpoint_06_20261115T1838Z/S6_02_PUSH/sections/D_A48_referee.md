# Independent referee of A.4.8

Date: 2026-11-15. Source: `sections/ab_push_all_fan_homology.tex`. Verdict: **passes in the explicitly specified class of finite toroidal outputs of A.2.4**. This is not a statement about arbitrary analytic fillings.

The primary source check was made directly against Abramovich–Karu–Matsuki–Włodarczyk (2002), Theorems 1.6.1 and 2.7.1, the proof on page 552, and analytic Section 5.5 on page 565, together with Matsuki's corrected Section 2. The proof of Theorem 2.7.1 works with the finite polyhedral complex, its integral barycentric embedding, a fan cobordism, and nonsingular star subdivisions. Section 5.5 supplies the analytic realization of those modifications. The displayed complete-analytic formulation in Section 5.6 is not being misquoted as a relative theorem. The manuscript explicitly constructs the finite complex and its analytic stars, which is the needed additional argument.

Deepest-strata-first barycentric modification is valid for the normal-crossing analytic boundary with self-identifications. Deeper intersections are separated before taking a stratum closure as the next smooth centre. Temporary pure-q centres are allowed; this is essential because strictification need not preserve the punctured r-family. The gluing uses monomial ideals, so analytic bundle units do not obstruct its descent.

The centre classification has no hidden period quotient in the mixed or pure-bottom case. A stabilizer power fixes each primitive ray. A positive q-height ray then forces first the G1 exponent and then the G2 exponent to vanish. Only a pure-top two-cone retains G1, and its global plumbing extension is birational to the rational surface S. Pure-bottom centres are smooth toric surfaces or curves proper over the r-disc; the primitive character gives connected generic fibre P1 or a point, with rational-chain or point central support. Thus all the listed centres have free even integral cohomology, and precisely the compact ones have the top cohomology needed to contribute to degree six.

The ordinary integral blow-up splitting applies to these noncompact closed centres. Tubular excision, integral Thom and exceptional Gysin maps, the projective-bundle theorem, and proper blow-down pushforward via Borel–Moore duality require neither compactness of the ambient space nor a Kähler hypothesis. The claimed b2 and b6 increments follow in every complex codimension.

The reference normalization calculation is consistent with A.4.1. Eliminating the intermediate identity blocks leaves the original difference map and the functional a·(m−F), which is already an integral combination of its primitive coordinates. The rank-seven kernel and all primitive-image claims therefore survive for arbitrary n; the finite Smith computations are supplementary checks, not the general argument. The final Euler reconstruction of b4 and the monodromy substitution I+nN are correct.
