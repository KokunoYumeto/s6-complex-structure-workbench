# Independent referee: C.3.16 and C.3.17

Reviewed on 2026-11-15 against the displayed sources, including the final explicit weighted Hopf overlap calculation in C.3.17.

## C.3.16: actual cusp linking — accepted

The central orbit cycle is compact and locally contractible, with integral top cohomology Z and multiplicity one at a generic smooth point. Alexander duality therefore identifies the complement's H1 with Z, generated by that point's positive normal meridian. The globally defined continuous function h = G2^3 - G1^4 has precisely that zero set. Its cusp expression is q times a nowhere-zero factor, so h/|h| evaluates to +1 on the local meridian and hence is the integral linking class. Evaluating the actual boundary expression gives the row (12, 0, 1) on (gamma, u, q). No smoothness of the descended map at fixed points is required. The distinction between this globally defined function and the merely meromorphic coordinate q is necessary and correctly retained.

## C.3.17: marked product cusp quotient — accepted

The fixed-fan deformation changes only bounded period units. Uniform invertibility, free deck translation on the fan, the finite fundamental collection of charts and compact central normalization give an actual proper smooth family after a uniform shrink. Its outer-collar transport fixes real marked period coordinates. A disjoint fixed tubular neighbourhood can independently carry unitary normal transport. Patching invariant horizontal fields preserves the parameter derivative and the boundary marking.

The zero-unit fibre is the specified small resolution of the product of two nodal Tate families. In the conifold chart u=xA, y=Av, quotient coordinates (x, Av, (|A|^2-|v|^2)/2) make projection to the first nodal family a submersion. The second chart gives the complementary check. The two fixed preimages of the base node are distinct, so they do not require a spurious identification of fixed charts. Away from those charts, the normal coordinate changes are diagonal units pulled from the projection base. The added Hermitian metric calculation makes their weighted Hopf real coordinates agree and makes the quotient overlaps smooth. This is a chosen valid Hopf smoothing, not a use of a generally false functorial smoothness statement for arbitrary equivariant maps.

The second Tate identity section avoids the node and lifts through the small resolution; it trivializes the oriented circle bundle. A positive vertical length form, normalized on each compact fibre, extends the exact marked boundary translation action. The cutoff of this action produces the actual annular map as an ambient isotopy endpoint, but the isotopy moves the fixed sphere. Thus it does not prove a relative isotopy or a vanishing monodromy class.

Finally the regular first elliptic fibre is a primitive generator of H2 of the nodal elliptic neighbourhood. Its product with the marked circle is the primitive H3 class. The gamma-u-w torus consequently does not bound inside the cusp quotient. This specifically prevents using an unproved local bounding chain to discard its stable framing contribution.

## Remaining scope

Neither proposition evaluates the cubic or Pontryagin characteristic number of the actual cap in C.3.14. The extension gives a loop of embedded fixed spheres and a concrete immersed trace, but an actual framed compression or an equivalent characteristic-class transgression is still required.
