# B.4.29: what invariant forms do not control

## Proven scope

The proper Tate family is squared over one disc and its unique conifold is replaced by the explicit proper small resolution. Both charts have smooth total space and reduced normal-crossing centre. Ordinary fibres are products of elliptic curves, with rank-two nilpotent monodromy. Translation by exp(2πi/t) in the first factor preserves every invariant relative one- and two-form and the full integral Gauss–Manin variation. The absolute volume dt∧dlogZ∧dlogW also extends without zeros and is unchanged.

The zero section has two distinct central limits after translation, along t=1/j and t=1/(j+1/2). A hypothetical meromorphic total map would give a proper graph; its pullback to the zero section has a unique dominating curve, whose normalization is finite of degree one over the disc. It would extend the translated section holomorphically, a contradiction. Replacing 1/t by exp(1/t) makes the extra horizontal differential essential and retains the same failure.

## Exact remaining issue

The example changes the affine marking of one existing cap. It does not concern a difference between two sections that both extend holomorphically into that same cap: a common essential translation cancels in such a difference. Consequently the possible two-section logarithmic extension theorem remains a distinct question and is not refuted by this proposition. No conclusion about the existence of the actual six-sphere origin cap is inferred from this example alone.
