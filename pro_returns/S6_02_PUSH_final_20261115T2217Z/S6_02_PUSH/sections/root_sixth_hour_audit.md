## Further proved advances before checkpoint06

The nonzero-degree strengthening of B.4.13 was independently checked by root: an automorphism preserving an elliptic axis induces an isomorphism t_b^*N=N on its normal line. For degree d the Abel--Jacobi difference is minus d times b, so an infinite-order translation cannot occur when d is nonzero. Finite cycles of marked caps are excluded by taking a power. This remains a statement about a preserved marked axis, not a proof excluding caps that occur only in the central parameter.

B.4.14 is integrated after two independent reviews. The holomorphic Lefschetz calculation uses the actual compact parabolic surface, the fixed elliptic curve of normal degree minus eight, and the eight rational-cycle nodes of weights plus/minus one. Its equivariant Euler characteristic is minus eight. A direct Hartogs and contracting-germ calculation shows that the sole global vector field is the transverse Euler field. Consequently H1(TlogE) has nine trivial characters and H1(ThetaY) has eight. The associated compact Y-bundle over P1 has H2 of its relative tangent sheaf zero, also after fixing one entire fibre. The nonisolated local-to-global and analytic effectivity problems are explicitly not inferred from that vanishing. The exact independent program ab_push_kato_weights.py and root rerun both pass.

E.2.9 resolves the horizontal-divisor obstruction itself. On each irreducible reduced fibre, twisting a vector bundle by minus a sufficiently large multiple of a nonzero effective Cartier divisor kills sections. A finite filtration extends this to all nonreduced fibres. Singular Gorenstein duality then kills fibre H2, uniformly by nested closed loci on the compact base. Coherent direct images and a positive base twist kill global H2 and H3. A negative divisor twist separately kills H0. An explicit affine line of exponentiated additive Cech cocycles joins the two line bundles, and the three proper analytic jumping sets cannot cover that line. For B=TX this forces chi(TX) at most zero, contradicting the exact Riemann--Roch value one. Root and boundary_d both read and passed the complete proof, including the nonreduced and analytic-base-change steps. The exact HRR program and root rerun pass.

E.2.10 therefore proves relative-pencil persistence for both signs of the nonzero canonical slope. The zero-period endpoint is excluded for either sign, and distinct ordered multiplicity pairs cannot have a common smooth one-sided limit. These statements do not prove uniqueness of a smooth completion at an interior period or rule out new deformation branches on which the pencil disappears. Those are the remaining E.2.4 gaps.

E.2.8 was strengthened with a second use of the divisor restriction argument: every component of a reducible fibre on a complex six-sphere has normalization outside class C. Another meeting component cuts a nonzero effective Cartier divisor on it, pulled back from a topologically trivial line on the threefold; a Kähler resolution would give a positive integral equal to zero. This is a necessary constraint on possible critical-value collisions, not a classification of their non-Kähler components.

The sixth-hour source comparisons also explicitly retain two limitations. Cathelineau1978 concerns deformations with a fibrewise action over a trivial-action base, so it does not supply a moving-base action on the singular Y smoothing. Tziolas2009, Section9 and Remark9.7, warns that vanishing of H2 tangent alone does not preserve arbitrary specified local lifts for a nonisolated singular set. Neither result is stretched into the missing compactification theorem.

Fujimoto1988 Example3.4, read together with Example3.1 Step4 on printed pp98--99, provides an isotrivial punctured elliptic-surface family with non-Kähler central components. Choosing the elliptic product gives an abelian-surface instance. Thus even an isotrivial projective torus on the punctured disc does not justify a meromorphic comparison with every proper filling. The source does not construct the generically simple prescribed torus family.

## Actual regular-place insertion reviewed before checkpoint06

The separate working proof reserved for B.4.15 is in ab_push_regular_cap_working.md and is deliberately not counted among the 121 integrated draft23 statements. Root read it in full and reran its exact chart/incidence program successfully. boundary_d independently passed the actual semiabelian action, the affine-to-Jacobian comparison on the local punctured bidisc, the invariant blow-up ideals, both resolution charts, the Cartier transition cocycle, properness and the scalar Hopf identification; the full referee record is D_B4415_referee.md.

The construction blows up a regular point of the two-dimensional parameter base and twists by the exceptional line using an actual flat cocharacter u. It is canonically isomorphic to the prescribed local family off that base point. Twelve compact vertical normalization components appear, each a projective-line bundle over a scalar Hopf surface or its blow-up along two disjoint elliptic curves. Their first Betti number is one. The total fourfold is smooth and its divisor r=0 is reduced normal crossings; the map is flat over r and proper over the bidisc. It is not jointly flat over the bidisc, and no properness over the r-disc alone is claimed before a global insertion over a compact q-base.

This is a construction directly on the prescribed torus germ and preserves generic simplicity. It therefore rules out generic simplicity alone as an exclusion of non-ddbar vertical components in this two-parameter setting. It preserves component labels and does not repair the cusp rotation obstruction. It supplies neither a global S6 filling nor topology for such an unconstructed filling.
