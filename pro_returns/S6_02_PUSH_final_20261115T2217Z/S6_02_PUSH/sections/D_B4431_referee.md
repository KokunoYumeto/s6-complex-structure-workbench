The graph modification and resolution can be supported on r=0 because the marked map is already holomorphic and the total space smooth on the punctured parameter family. Central multiplicities after resolution are explicitly retained.

The finite exceptional set is obtained from proper maps of the closed normalized branch-intersection spaces. This is the necessary distinction from nonproper open strata. Nonconstant maps from these smooth compact spaces to P1 have a proper analytic critical-value subset; constant components and deeper zero-dimensional strata contribute finitely many further values. Proper submersions of the double-branch surfaces give finite local systems of connected compact curve components.

On a double stratum, the displayed primitive angular circle for r=x^a y^b is correct. Moving its base point within a connected curve with finitely many triple points removed transports the actual normal circle. Normal-frame changes can rotate its parameter, and ordered-branch reversal can reverse its orientation, but neither operation adds a tangential H1 cycle. Based-path changes only conjugate the loop. The finitely many monomial-fibre components account for the remaining ambiguity.

There is therefore a finite set of actual H1 classes, not merely abstract stratum labels, permuted by q-monodromy. Compact generator paths give a common small nonzero r on which all the transports take place. The analogous finite local r-orbit is sufficient: for the actual unipotent T_r=I−mE, a periodic vector satisfies E v=0, since the lattice is torsion-free. No global reduced semistable model or additional r-root is assumed.

On ker E/Zδ, the matrices are exactly

    M1 = [[0,1],[-1,-1]],  M2 = [[0,-1],[1,0]].

Their product and its conjugate have distinct rational fixed lines. A finite orbit is fixed by positive powers of both unipotents, so its vector is zero. The primitive integral δ line then gives the claimed integral, rather than merely rational, conclusion.

At a higher crossing, the rational angular kernel is generated by the pairwise angular vectors. Each is represented by an actual open double-stratum circle after keeping the other branch coordinates small and nonzero. The q-coordinate is retained because it is a submersion on the closed triple stratum away from the deleted values. This proves the same conclusion for the whole local angular torus. Ordered branch spaces correctly handle self-crossing.

The final scope limitation is necessary: the result constrains vanishing periods and hence differences of extending sections, but does not identify an affine origin in an arbitrary cap with the chosen origin of B.4.19. A common nonextending translation cancels from all pairwise differences. No unrestricted Part B noncompletion follows from this proposition alone.
