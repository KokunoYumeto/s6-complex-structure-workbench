# Source and scope audit for E.2.18

15 November 2026. The normalization argument contracts only three disjoint (-1)-curves on the central compact complex surface. It does not invoke a relative contraction theorem or assume that the central surface is projective before applying the analytic contraction.

Grauert, H. (1962). Über Modifikationen und exzeptionelle analytische Mengen. *Mathematische Annalen, 146*, 331–368. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01441136

Opened the original article transcription at https://de.scribd.com/document/864372889/grauert1962, specifically §4, no.8(a), printed pp.364–365. This treats a projective space with normal O(-1) in a complex manifold and explicitly proves that its contraction is a smooth point. With dimension two this is the analytic (-1)-curve contraction used here. The additional projectivity assertion in the source is conditional; it is not used. The compact-surface classification input is the already opened official BHPV2004 Chapter VI preview, Theorem1.1 and Table10, recorded in E_hopf_normalization_references.bib and its source audit. The proof establishes κ=-infinity directly from a nef rational curve C with C²=1 and K.C=-3, then uses b1=0 to obtain rationality. It needs no separate Kähler criterion or projectivity theorem.

The relative toric logarithmic frame follows from constant-cohomology base change, in the exact version already read and audited in E.3.1, and fibrewise evaluation. It does not require a relative blowdown or an unproved simultaneous projective-coordinate normalization.
