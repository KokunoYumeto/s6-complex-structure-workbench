# Independent referee: B.4.12

Referee: boundary_d. Date: 2026-11-15. Outcome: PASS at the stated proper-family and normal-line scope.

The two distinct degree-two line systems give an injective immersion of the marked elliptic curve into P1×P1: a shared identified pair or shared branch point would equate the line systems. Taking a degree-four square root of N inverse then makes the anticanonical normal exactly N inverse.

The anticanonical section ring of Q is normal and generated in degree one. The restriction sequence has vanishing H1 in every degree, including d=0 because H1(O(-2,-2))=0. Its quotient by the section is therefore exactly the elliptic section ring, without an overlooked degree-zero term. The cone map is flat, and its nonzero fibre is Q minus E.

The vertex blow-up is Tot(K_Q), with parameter xi·e. Its central components are Q and Tot(N), each reduced, with equation xi·z=t near E. B.4.11 globalizes this particular local component, so the compact strict transform is exactly S8 and the other component exactly Q. Properness and smooth total space follow as stated. The normals K_Q and O_S(-E), and the three pairwise products including one triple-curve factor, have the correct signs and degrees.

The integral Thom sequence gives H1(Q minus E)=Z/2, H2=Z^3 and H3=0: H2(Q)→H0(E) is (a,b)↦2a+2b, while H4(Q)→H2(E) is degree one. The local Euler number is four. The compact nearby surface has Euler number twelve, not two.

The scope limitation is necessary and correctly explicit: a proper family over an open disc is not itself an absolute compact threefold, and the line-bundle identities do not construct a global compact NC threefold or the prescribed punctured S6 comparison. No such stronger inference is used.
