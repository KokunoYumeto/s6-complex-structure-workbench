# Independent referee: C.3.18–C.3.19

Reviewed on 2026-11-15. Both statements pass at their expressly stated scopes.

## C.3.18: the actual bounding manifold and affine-frame defect

The product circle coordinate selected by the second Tate identity section agrees exactly with the marked gamma character on the outer collar. The rebuilt cusp map therefore glues to the actual exterior two-torus quotient. Its only critical model is (x,y,theta) -> (xy,theta), and the finite root charts remain submersions. The Milnor page of the nearby weighted knot meets the critical knot in twelve transverse positive points. Its inverse image is consequently an actual embedded positive genus-one Lefschetz four-manifold, with Euler characteristic twelve and the specified torus boundary.

The relative determinant calculation does not assume that the whole four-manifold is algebraic or globally complex. A compatible fibrewise elliptic complex structure and positive nodal charts provide a Hodge line on the base. Evaluation of the elliptic differential is nowhere zero in the relative dualizing line; in a node chart the identity dt wedge dx/x = -dx wedge dy explicitly extends this identification. The complex determinant, including its chosen boundary determinant frame, is thus pulled back from the base. Its relative square is zero. One boundary complex vector is outward pointing, so the relative top Chern number is the Euler characteristic. Hence the relative p1 evaluation is -24.

The final comparison retains the actual normal framing: along the boundary the page normal has q-angle equal to a constant minus twelve times gamma. Adding the hypersurface normal gives one SO(2) block of winding -12. This is null after stabilization, since its image in pi1(SO(5)) is the even class. Horizontal changes are contractible shears. The boundary orientation is (gamma,w,u), opposite the ordered marked (gamma,u,w) torus. Thus the stated defect and the equivalent reversed-orientation transgression are consistent. This does not yet compute a closed-cap characteristic pairing by itself.

## C.3.19: the actual cap Pontryagin pairing

The chosen stable frame on S2 times D4 and a global stable orbit frame have homotopic restrictions to P, since P retracts to S2 and pi2(SO)=0. In the actual affine nilmanifold frame the derivative is a shear, contractible relative both ends. The full change of frame remains in the clutching map A(rho_chi p)^(-1) A(p). Flattening the radial dependence of A preserves the identity at both ends.

The map Gamma on S1 times L is identity at the chosen circle basepoint. The relative H3 class is therefore precisely its H2(L) slant summand; surjectivity of H2(S1 times L) -> H2(L) removes any extra relative-lift ambiguity. Applying the primitive suspension of the actual universal p1 class gives the gamma-translation trace, with the sign fixed by the displayed inverse. This avoids silently replacing p1 suspension by a primitive generator, which would risk a factor of two.

The positive-q coefficient deck is (g,u,w,d,a) -> (g,u,w-u,d+g,a+1). Its invariant connection dd-a dg has curvature dg wedge da. Pairing the relative trace with that actual Euler class selects exactly the gamma-u-w period of the frame comparison; all other H3(L) components vanish in the pairing. Consequently its absolute value is the actual defect 24 from C.3.18, and Wall's formula gives absolute framing coordinate six.

## Remaining scope

The absolute-value statement does not by itself fix the sign relative to the cubic pairing. A common cut-and-reglue orientation must be used for both. Nor does it identify the actual delta line on the Lefschetz four-manifold with a Poincare line on a holomorphic Jacobian or torsor. Those comparisons are separate and are not assumed in either accepted result.
