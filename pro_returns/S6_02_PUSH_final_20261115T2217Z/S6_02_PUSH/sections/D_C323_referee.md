# Independent referee: C.3.23, integer additivity

**Result:** pass. Referee: boundary_d. This argument closes the gap between the integer invariant of the six-loop cap and the original one-loop ordinary mapping class. It does not infer the latter from an equation only in the group of order 28.

## Normalization

An equivariant normal derivative for the fixed scalar circle representation on C2 is complex linear. Its real determinant is positive. Thus an orientation-preserving equivariant map of the six-sphere restricts to an orientation-preserving map on its fixed S2. Smale's connectedness result permits removal of that restriction by an equivariant tubular isotopy.

Once the fixed sphere is pointwise fixed, the normal derivative is a map from S2 to GL2(C). Polar decomposition and pi2(U2)=0 give a homotopy to the identity. It is realized by local equivariant vector fields and an invariant cutoff. The remaining germ can be rescaled to its derivative; the tangent-normal mixed derivative vanishes by the absence of equivariant linear maps between the trivial and scalar representations. The rescaled germ is smooth, and the resulting local isotopy extends equivariantly. Consequently every component has a representative equal to the identity on a fixed tube.

## Independence

Equivariantly isotopic clutching maps give equivariantly diffeomorphic clutched pairs. Variable unitary normal tubes along the isotopy supply compatible smooth Hopf charts. This uses an adapted smoothing near the fixed set, rather than the false assertion that every equivariant map induces a smooth map in a preassigned raw quotient atlas. Equivalently, interpolate the normal metrics and use positive linear gauge changes near the fixed set with a tubular cutoff; the resulting comparison is unitary between the chosen endpoint metrics.

The ambiguity in identifying an orbit homotopy six-sphere with S6 does not alter the knot integer. An orientation-preserving diffeomorphism can be isotoped to fix the germ of a point. Its compact support in the complementary stereographic chart can then be conjugated, through diffeomorphisms isotopic to the identity, into a ball disjoint from the specified knot. The representative fixes that knot, while the isotopy gives the required ambient isotopy of its images.

## Addition

After normalization, choose a fixed point outside the supports. The stereographic action is scalar on the C2 summand of R2 plus C2. Positive dilations and translations in the fixed R2 commute with the circle and extend smoothly to the one-point compactification. Conjugating the two compact supports places them in disjoint invariant balls centred on the fixed plane. This does not change their equivariant components.

The composition with disjoint supports is the equivariant connected sum of the two clutching constructions. The separating collar is the identity both upstairs and on the orbit. The fixed discs are joined by their ordinary boundary connected sum, so the orbit knots are connected-summed with the stated orientations. The Haefliger integer is therefore additive as an integer, before any reduction modulo 28.

## Passage to the ordinary mapping class

For tube-relative representatives, C.3.14 identifies this integer with Wall's unframed coordinate, minus J. The remaining framing generator has an explicit SU2 lift extending over the seven-disc; it contributes nothing to the ordinary homotopy-sphere group. The Davis generator comparison then gives the fixed signed reduction modulo 28.

Thus q(phi^6)=0 implies 6q(phi)=0 in Z, hence q(phi)=0 and the ordinary class vanishes. The application must retain the fact that the actual six-loop representative is related to phi^6 by equivariant isotopies. This holds for the transports, finite/cusp corrections, and the invariant logarithmic gauge isotopy of C.3.6/C.3.10, but should be stated in the final corollary.
