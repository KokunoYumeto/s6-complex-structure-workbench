# Independent referee: E.2.21

Reviewer: boundary_d. 15 November 2026. Full source reviewed:
`sections/E_global_completion.tex`, with the prior E.1 comparison
and E.2.13/E.2.20 inputs.

**PASS at the stated one-sided-specialization scope.** The ordinary
Jacobian comparison is an identity of holomorphic period kernels, so
does not require an affine origin to extend. The finite affine action
has a fixed linear part and a locally constant integral cyclic norm
class; after matching it, finite averaging solves the vector-sheaf
cocycle. This is the cyclic H2 lattice class, not an unsupported claim
that every torus translation has a globally single-valued Lie lift.

On fixed annular collars, simply connected refinements admit the needed
Lie lifts. Their integral defects vary continuously in the parameter
and are therefore constant in the marked integral local system. The
finite and cusp identifications determine the allowed changes of lifts.
The actual cusp comparison supplied by E.2.20 is needed here; identifying
the abstract central surface would not suffice.

After these discrete classes agree, the remainder is exactly the
extension sheaf from E.1.2. Its quotient is O(-infinity), including for
the enlarged finite multiplicities, and both end terms have vanishing
H1. E.1.3 already proves the parameter-dependent version on a Stein
disc. The final Laurent/Cauchy and finite-averaging argument is therefore
valid holomorphically in the parameter. The residual automorphisms are
the connected scalar translation group.

The last scope remark is necessary and correct: openness plus closure
under full punctured-disc specializations does not alone establish
closedness in every analytic family. A separate generic-disc propagation
argument is still needed for arbitrary deformation chains.
