## 1. Reduction from an arbitrary proper filling

The punctured total space is smooth and connected, so only one irreducible component dominates it. Retaining its reduced closure, normalization, and proper resolution preserves the prescribed punctured family. The smooth dominating fourfold has a nonzero parameter, which is a nonzero divisor. B.4.4 therefore applies without a hypothesis on central multiplicities; its coherent bi-anticanonical pencil and three distinguished critical-value branches extend the actual marked map. Resolving its graph and the central support changes only the parameter-zero locus.

On any dominating central threefold component, a nonconstant map to the marked base curve has fibres of dimension two. A three-dimensional fibre thus requires a whole central component with constant marked value. There are finitely many such values. Shrinking near the cusp removes all the others, and gives equidimensionality away from the single base origin. The explicit coefficient-ideal argument of B.4.30 applies to the remaining excess-one fibre and permits finite point blowups of the surface base. Neither projectivity of the cap nor a general analytic semistable-reduction theorem is being assumed.

Finite local multisections exist for the resulting equidimensional map by transverse analytic slicing. Their normalized two-dimensional sources are Cohen--Macaulay, hence finite flat over the smooth surface base. The compact inverse image of the origin admits a finite cover of such constructions; a smaller bidisc has its full inverse image in their union by properness. This justifies a single common positive degree and a single finite set of later root/norm choices.

## 2. The cap-derived differences extend in the exact sheaf

The proof correctly separates three types of boundary divisor.

* At each exceptional divisor over the origin, both base valuations are positive. The actual monodromy has primitive invariant lattice generated by w and delta. The B.4.30 logarithmic-form, integral-residue, and chart-chain proof applies.
* At the strict q axis, the same full-rank condition holds, and the completion away from the missing origin is the specified actual cusp. Proper modifications over the origin do not change that fact.
* At the strict r axis, the monodromy kernel is larger. The proof does not replace it by the cocharacter lattice. Instead B.4.31 shows that all actual local angular classes lie in the primitive delta line. Hence the first normalized form has zero residue and the second has integer residue. The resulting character sheaf is precisely `O* x M_r^gp`, not the full log sheaf in both coordinates.

Under a local cyclic root and toric subdivision, a new angular class maps rationally into the span of the old local angular classes. Its actual nearby-fibre homology remains integral, so primitivity of delta gives the same integral conclusion after these operations. The simultaneous root accounts for both central multiplicities and splitting of the finitely many multisections before subdivision. Multiplicative norms multiply the difference by a positive integer and preserve the zero first-character order along the strict r axis.

The proper normal dominating models have trivial finite Stein factor over the normal surface base, since ordinary torus fibres are connected. This supplies connected central fibres for the finite chart-chain comparison, including sections with limits on different components. Hartogs is applied to sections, not to torsors. On a punctured small bidisc the constant period lattice has zero H1; a lift to the exact bounded-character sheaf exists. Divide boundary monomials, extend each unit and its inverse, and restore the same monomials. Uniqueness modulo the constant period lattice preserves the cocycles.

The glued object is therefore a torsor under the specific associated sheaf `A_S`. It is built from differences of extending local traces. No log-group presentation of the unknown proper space is used. Its ordinary restriction is the actual positive contracted power of the original torsor, with the actual trace sections as identifications.

## 4. The two distinct origin comparisons

B.4.27 gives `H1(S,A_S)=0`, so the trace-derived torsor has a global section h. This h need not correspond to a holomorphic section of the original unknown cap. The proof only uses that its difference from a multiplied trace is an associated-log-torus section.

Along the strict r axis, B.4.32 gives zero normalized loop class for a multiplied trace relative to the actual ordinary B.4.19 origin. An associated section there changes the radial label only by delta. The rotating reference origin has the same loop class because its actual comparison is `G(s)+6v delta`. Thus the gamma evaluation of `h-D o` is zero. No claim of boundedness or meromorphic extension of this difference is needed.

Along the strict q axis, the known actual sigma origin extends through the central open orbit of its root fan. Comparison with traces uses the same two-extending-sections argument and can be included in the finite common norm multiplier. Hence `h-D sigma` is an associated section on the whole punctured q-axis neighbourhood. The q-order map on the constant period lattice is diagonal `(12m,12m)` and injective. The ghost-cocycle argument then makes its radial Y-connecting class zero. Projecting the additive homology connecting class to Y kills w and delta but does not alter its gamma evaluation. This gives the second zero.

These two controls are different. The first uses the full global marked base and its peripheral monodromy; the second uses the actual cusp and its injective ghost valuation. Neither identifies the unknown central model with B.4.19.

## 5. Exact contradiction and finite-cover scope

The coordinate change from the actual sigma-based chart to the rotating chart is `zeta -> zeta - sigma + F`. Therefore, as torsor points, `sigma-o=F`; the sign in the displayed difference equation is correct. For every positive parameter-cover degree n,

`F(s,v+n)-F(s,v) = n gamma - 6n(n-1) delta`.

Its gamma label is n. After the common positive trace and norm degree D, the two zero evaluations would imply `0 = Dn`, impossible. The independent exact code verifies this symbolic identity for arbitrary positive n and D. All extra roots and trace multipliers have positive degree and cannot annihilate the integer obstruction.

The proof consequently covers every initial finite parameter cover, arbitrary singular centres, additional vertical components, and central multiplicities. Retaining the main component at the start means none of those additions evades the argument. Its use of the actual global monodromy and exact affine clutching is explicit.
