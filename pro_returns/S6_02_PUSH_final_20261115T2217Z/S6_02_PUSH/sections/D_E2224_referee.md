# Independent referee: E.2.24 continuous complex structures

Reviewer: boundary_d. 15 November 2026.
Full source read: `sections/E_continuous_deformations.tex`.

**PASS**, conditional only on the now independently accepted E.2.23.

The original published Meersseman article was opened independently:
`https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/jep.108.pdf`, retrievals
turn530view0--turn530view1. Theorem 3.4, printed p. 898, explicitly gives
a connected neighbourhood in the space of integrable structures, a
finite-dimensional Kuranishi subspace and a retraction, with inverse
representation by a diffeomorphism in Diff0. Remark 3.6, pp. 898--899,
explains the Sobolev construction and its restriction to smooth
operators. These are precisely the hypotheses needed here.

The continuous retract K is connected. The proper smooth Kuranishi
family may be restricted to its reduction without changing its fibres.
If the neighbourhood contains any known-pair structure, its Kuranishi
representative is biholomorphic to it. E.2.23 therefore identifies every
fibre over K, and the local Diff0 representation identifies every
structure in the neighbourhood with that pair. This argument applies
at initially unknown structures and proves the claimed clopen locus,
not merely openness near known members.

A continuous inverse image of a clopen set in a connected parameter
space is empty or the whole space. Saturation under Diff0 or Diff+
makes the image clopen for the quotient topology. Neither step assumes
that the fixed-pair locus itself is connected, and the stated limitation
on the number of marked components is appropriate.
