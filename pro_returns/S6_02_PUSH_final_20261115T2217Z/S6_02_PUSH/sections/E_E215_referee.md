# Independent full-source referee: E.2.15

15 November 2026. Read `sections/E_one_critical_volume.tex` in full. Verdict: **PASS**.

On the simply connected ordinary base C, the integral marking is global. The period subbundle takes values in one countable integral-change-of-basis orbit. A connected subset of a metric space with more than one point is uncountable, by composing with distance to one of its points, so the marked period subbundle is constant. The corresponding invariant relative volume descends under translations of local origins. Its fibre integral is therefore the same positive constant on every ordinary torus, with no need for a torsor section or polarization.

The actual line-bundle identity K_X^A=f*O(D) produces σ with divisor Df*(p). Consequently Ω_A=q^(−D)σ is a holomorphic nowhere-zero A-canonical form on the entire inverse image of a small disc, including nonreduced fibres. This is stronger than a merely numerical canonical relation and is exactly the input available from E.2.10.

The coordinate-invariant density |Ω_A|^(2/A) is smooth and positive on the actual smooth compact total space. Fibre integration of its expression on the punctured neighbourhood gives the asserted L^(2/A) bound for P=q^(−D−2A)φ(1/q). The factor q^(−2A) is the A-th power of dt=−q^(−2)dq; the sign (−1)^A is harmless. No volume bound on an unconstructed comparison graph is assumed.

For every positive exponent, |P|^(2/A) is subharmonic. The mean-value estimate on a disc of radius |q|/2 gives |q|^A|P(q)|→0 uniformly. Therefore q^A P extends holomorphically with a zero, and P has pole order at most A−1. It follows that φ(1/q)=q^(D+A+1)H(q). If D≥−A this extends with value zero at infinity, contradicting the nonzero entire function φ. The conclusion D<−A and the application D/A≥−1/2 are correct.

The proof is independent of a Kähler metric, meromorphic extension of a comparison with a reference filling, and classification of non-Kähler singular fibres. It excludes the ordinary constant-image collapsed target, while not asserting uniqueness of a noncollapsed completion or persistence on unrelated deformation branches.
