## Logical boundary

The proven one-sided smooth-specialization statements do not assert the known period locus is closed in every Kuranishi space. For D<0 the central general surface may, on the present argument, be Hopf type with an effective anticanonical restriction of the common divisor. For D>0 the relative fibration persists, but another irreducible deformation branch through its limit could lose the canonical-root sections. Two positive known families cannot share one smooth limit, and opposite-sign known families cannot share one smooth limit; this does not exclude a chain with additional unconstructed components.

No fixed-fibre algebraic-dimension-zero assertion was used. The inequality κ(F)≤0 follows instead from globally generating a twisted coherent pluricanonical pushforward: generic rank at least two supplies a meromorphic function nonconstant along the algebraic reduction. No general invariance of plurigenera or Kodaira dimension is assumed.
