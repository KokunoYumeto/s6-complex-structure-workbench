# B.4.31: global control of every r-central meridian

The initial statement was strengthened, with root's explicit approval, to every proper analytic filling after a finite parameter cover. Retaining the reduced main component, normalizing and resolving gives a smooth connected dominating fourfold and therefore r-flatness. The B.4.4 proof only used a nonzero divisor to make its direct image torsion-free; its central reduced-NC assumption was unnecessary and has been removed with that explanation. Graph resolution and principalization then produce exactly the normal-crossings support with multiplicities already treated in the proof. No semistable-reduction theorem is used. No unrestricted nonexistence conclusion is included without the separately audited affine-clutching bridge.

The intrinsic marked fibration extends by B.4.4, and graph resolution gives a smooth global model over P1 with normal-crossings r-central support, possibly with multiplicities. After excluding finitely many base values, compact ordered double-branch strata form proper smooth families of curves. Their connected fibre components, branch orderings and monomial-fibre choices have finite monodromy. Moving a normal meridian along one connected punctured double curve changes its based loop only by conjugation, so its actual H1 class has a finite q-monodromy orbit.

The local monomial equation r=x^a y^b also shows that its r-monodromy orbit is finite. Actual r-monodromy is unipotent I-m(delta tensor gamma), so the angular class belongs to ker(gamma). Its projection to the residual elliptic lattice has finite orbit. The exact finite-place quotient matrices generate the lower and upper elementary unipotents; a vector fixed by positive powers of both is zero. Primitivity of delta upgrades the rational conclusion to an integral multiple of delta. Higher-crossing angular homology is spanned rationally by the pairwise double-branch circles.
