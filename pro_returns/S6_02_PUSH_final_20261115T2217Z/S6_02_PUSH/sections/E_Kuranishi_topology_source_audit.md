# Source audit for the continuous deformation conclusion

## Opened primary source

Meersseman, L. (2019). The Teichmüller and Riemann moduli stacks. *Journal de l'École polytechnique — Mathématiques, 6*, 879–945. https://doi.org/10.5802/jep.108

The original published PDF was opened at https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/jep.108.pdf on 15 November 2026. The journal-hosted endpoint returned HTTP 403; the Numdam copy is the published 68-page PDF, including its cover page, and was read successfully. The source is cited under `Meersseman2019E`.

The exact inputs are Section 2.1, pp.883–884, specifying the topology of smooth convergence on integrable complex operators; Section 3.2, Theorem 3.4, p.898, giving the classical Kuranishi product description on a connected neighbourhood; Remark 3.6, pp.898–899, explaining that elliptic regularity restricts the Sobolev construction to smooth structures; and the actual retraction in equation (3.11), p.899. The inverse comparison is explicitly by a diffeomorphism in the identity component. The local theorem has no global bounded-automorphism hypothesis. The latter belongs to different global stack results in the paper and is not imported here.

The author's arXiv version `1311.4170v4`, revised 17 June 2018, was opened before the published PDF. Its corresponding results are Theorem 3.2 and Remark 3.4, manuscript pp.11–12. E.2.24 uses the published numbering, not the earlier numbering. The published metadata and DOI were also checked on the Numdam article record.

## Use in the proof

The Kuranishi space is a continuous retract of the connected local domain and is therefore connected. Its proper smooth holomorphic deformation family falls within E.2.23 after reduction of the analytic base. If any smooth operator in the domain is a known member of a given pair, all Kuranishi fibres, and hence all operators in that domain, have that pair. This proves that the fixed-pair locus is clopen. Saturation gives the same separation in the diffeomorphism quotients. The proof does not assert that a fixed-pair marked locus is connected, does not identify Teichmüller space with a Kuranishi space, and does not assume that the quotient is Hausdorff.

## Access distinctions and verification

Attempts to open Kuranishi's 1962 Annals PDF directly through JSTOR and a guessed Annals endpoint failed. No claim is made that the original 1962 paper was read. The result is identified as classical Kuranishi theory through the opened primary research article's precise statement and explanation. Attempts to open the 2011 Meersseman Numdam PDF and a Douady Bourbaki endpoint also failed and are not used as proof sources.
