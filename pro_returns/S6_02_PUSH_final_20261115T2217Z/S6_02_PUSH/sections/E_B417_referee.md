# Independent referee: B.4.17

15 November 2026. Source read in full: `sections/B_monodromy_source_counterexample.tex`. Verdict: **PASS** at its stated scope.

The logarithmic transition formula defines a genuine holomorphic principal elliptic bundle: logarithm changes lie in the period lattice, and the line-bundle cocycle identities give the elliptic cocycle identity. The compact blow-up base and compact fibre make the total space compact; the local product construction makes it smooth and Hausdorff. The map to the second projective line has only the one stated reduced crossing fibre.

The Chern vectors on the strict transform, exceptional curve, and ordinary ruling are respectively 1, i, and 1+i. The last yields the multiplier exp(2πi·i/(1+i))=−exp(−π); the first two give exp(−2π), after reversing the cyclic generator in the second description. The canonical section of the exceptional line trivializes it on the entire complement of the zero fibre. Consequently the family there is a holomorphic product, not merely a differentiable bundle with identity linear monodromy.

The two fundamental-group maps from the crossing elliptic curve are the quotient maps by Z and by Zi. Their pushout is trivial. The degree-one Mayer–Vietoris map has determinant one, giving H1=H2=0; the degree-two elliptic class gives the additional H3 summand, so H3=Z³ and H4=Z². The total-space check b2=1 is also correct: the two independent Chern vectors kill two of the three base H2 classes, and the fibre H2 term has injective d2. No higher differential changes total degree two.

The original arXivv1 PDF was independently opened at https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.14420, especially equation (3.9) and §3.3, Corollary 3.7. The two successive inequalities do not make equality in the second imply equality in the first. The counterexample directly disproves that converse under the compact smooth hypotheses. The irreducible-fibre proof remains separate and valid.
