# Correction to the proof of A.2.1

Recorded 15 November 2026, 15:07 UTC. This is a correction to a proof of infinitude, not a counterexample to that conclusion.

The earlier paragraph said:

> If joint flatness is omitted, star-subdivide the orbit of a regular two-face generated by a bottom ray and a rho ray, using their sum. The new ray has h_u=1; regularity is preserved because replacing either basis vector by their sum has determinant one. It leaves the bottom unchanged. Repeating the operation on the resulting mixed faces gives infinitely many invariant smooth refinements, all reduced over u=0, with increasing numbers of rays modulo the period group. They are not jointly flat.

Each individual star subdivision is regular, but stars at different faces in the same period orbit can have intersecting centres. For example, the translates of the edge AE include BE, and both lie in the cone ABCE. Consequently the claimed simultaneous invariant subdivision needed an ordering or compatibility argument that was absent. The replacement uses the orbit of a maximal cone instead. Its distinct translates have disjoint interiors and distinct closed torus orbits; adding the sum of its four primitive generators is an equivariant ordinary blow-up at those disjoint points. This descends to the blow-up of one smooth central point away from the conductor. Repeating in the cone ABCv gives v_m=(m,m,3m,1). Every step is therefore an explicitly defined invariant smooth subdivision, with reduced central divisor. The program `code/ab_push_all_refinements.py` certifies the determinants, volumes, boundary compatibility and multiplicities for the displayed finite examples; the formula proves every step.
