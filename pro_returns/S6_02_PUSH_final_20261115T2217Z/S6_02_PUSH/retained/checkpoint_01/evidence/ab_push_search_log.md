## Literature comparison and limitations

The nodal chart, two elementary prism triangulations and the expected corrected normalization were proposed in the supplied AI review, not supplied there with a proof. The paper proves the direct global plumbing and its toroidal modifications, and restricts the two-model uniqueness to the stated minimal/joint-flat class. The unrestricted uniqueness in Lead 3 is refuted by explicit horizontal blow-ups.

The virtual Euler method is a general method already present algebraically in FG10. Here its analytic proper-flat version is justified by a topological perfect class and a compatible triangulated radial cobordism, and then applied to the specified pinching Q. No novelty is claimed for that method or application.

The finite-place product model is corrected to the actual generally nontrivial semiabelian bundle. The local finite quotient is constructed. The obstruction to gluing the prescribed local models is a finite component-permutation calculation, independently checked in the D referee discussion. It refutes the exact prescribed gluing, not all possible corrected cusp constructions.

The global S6 filling, its descent, and its central topology remain under investigation. The supplied R2 vanishing concerns a translation cocycle at nonzero parameter and is not a boundary-extension theorem. None of the primary texts opened so far supplies that missing implication.
