## PSA.1. Two directed maps

The ordinary-to-cap map has plus logarithm:
\(y_{\rm cap}=y_B+n\arg z/(2\pi)\). The contracting suspension
coordinate is \(\gamma_B=y_B\), and the outward coordinate is
\(\gamma_{\rm out}=-y_B\). The positive weighted Hopf angle is
\(\beta=-\epsilon y_B\).

Both actual product coordinates are plus products:
\(\xi_{\rm ord}=\zeta+o\), \(\xi_{\rm cusp}=\zeta+\sigma\).
The endpoint-to-initial closing \(C\) satisfies
\(P_vC=P_{v+1}\). Hence the cusp-to-ordinary arrow is \(o-\sigma\),
with motion \(-\epsilon K\). It is not the inverse arrow
\(\sigma-o\). The earlier determinant-minus-one diagnostic changed
the cap sign while retaining that inverse product arrow; the complete
corrected diagram changes both directed expressions.

Put \(h=2x-3y_B,\ \kappa=x-y_B\) and
\(\theta=-\epsilon(\kappa-g)\). Then
\[
 \binom h\theta=
 \begin{pmatrix}2&3\epsilon\\-\epsilon&-1\end{pmatrix}
 \binom x\beta+\binom0{\epsilon g},\qquad \det=1,
 \qquad \beta=2\theta+\epsilon(h-2g).
\]
This gives the actual positive boundary degree for either norm sign.

## PSA.2. Normal reference and full unit arrow

Use \(T_{-\epsilon}=\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-\epsilon)\) at both
initial normal references. The decreasing K height is carried in
the same reflected raw coordinate \((\Re y,\Im y,-b)\) at both ends.
The raw circle-first sign is minus and the Hopf determinant is
minus eight, giving the source-complex then orbit-sphere orientation.
The relative \(T_{-\epsilon}\) factors cancel; the signed conjugator
\((-\epsilon)T_{-\epsilon}\) belongs to SO(3).

For a full bounded torus multiplier \((Z,V)\mapsto(aZ,bV)\), the
actual Cox chart map is
\[
 (Z,A,B)\longmapsto(aZ,b^{-1}A,(b/a)B).
\]
It preserves \(q=ZAB\). Its positive factors are carried by
\(a_t=(a/|a|)|a|^t,\ b_t=(b/|b|)|b|^t\), on both Cox charts.
Only after this literal positive-factor path is the quaternion
phase formula used. With \(p=b^{-1},q_1=b/a\), choose
\(L^2=pq_1,\ R^2=p/q_1,\ LR=p\). The actual phase map is
\(q_H\mapsto Lq_HR\), and the orbit sphere maps by
\(\eta\mapsto L\eta\bar L\). All half-phase endpoints are retained.
The map is constant in the compact-sphere point and extends on its
actual cap. Higher q jets have zero normal linear part because
q is quadratic in the normal coordinates.

## PSA.3. Full normal clutch

For page degree \(M\), the actual binding argument is
\[
 \alpha=\phi-M\beta
       =\phi-2M\theta-\epsilon M(h-2g).
\]
Thus its theta degree is \(-2M\). The normal clutch is
\[
G(\alpha,\theta)=
\begin{pmatrix}
-\sin\alpha&\cos\theta\cos\alpha&\sin\theta\cos\alpha\\
 \cos\alpha&\cos\theta\sin\alpha&\sin\theta\sin\alpha\\
0&\sin\theta&-\cos\theta
\end{pmatrix}
=R_z(\alpha)G(0,\theta).
\]
The first factor passes through the actual two-torus in
\((\phi,h-2g)\), and the remaining factor passes through the
theta circle. The primitive third class of SO(3) pulls back as
the sum of these two zero classes. This proves zero relative
normal Pontryagin contribution for this precise clutch; it does
not assert that the third cohomology of a three-torus vanishes.

binding coinvariants and regular-band maps of RP.5 carry the page
line with square \(-2kh\) and \(\chi=k(3l+1)\), where
\(m=3k,\ h=6l+1,\ M=mh\). The preceding exact normal map gives

\[
 (k_M,\ell_M)=(6kh,24k(3l+1))
            =(2mh,4m(h+1)).
\]
Its index is \(3kl\), with native free pair
\((m(h+1),m(h-1))\). At \(h=1\) the same directed maps give

paired-arrow repair preserves the K76 reference pair by an
explicit normal and physical-line comparison. No value is
selected from the zero reference index alone.
