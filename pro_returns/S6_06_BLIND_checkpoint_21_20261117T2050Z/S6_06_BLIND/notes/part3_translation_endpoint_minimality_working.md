# Minimal scalar winding among the constructed source-translation endpoints

This is a scoped exact statement about the endpoint morphisms constructed in
K35 and the working K37. It does not classify arbitrary biholomorphisms of the
total threefold, or loops in the moduli of the source surface.

For a fixed whole-I1 source in the thirty F53 branches, retain the actual
source line, root caps, all finite characters, and the positive contracting
deck generator. Consider endpoint maps covering a global source translation
by an integral multiple of the primitive Mordell--Weil generator G. Among
positive scalar windings, the least winding is M/b, where M is the product
of the effective root orders and the source suspension is by -bG.

Indeed the actual source-translation lift on root i has central commutator
character chi_i, with chi_i^b=1 by its b-fold norm B0^(-1). A source translation
by pG has central character chi_i^p. On a scalar loop of winding L, the actual
root action A_i acquires the factor exp(2pi i L n_i/m_i). The endpoint conjugacy
equation at the central root fibre therefore implies

    exp(2pi i b L n_i/m_i)=1.

This equation follows from the literal root-line conjugation; an extending
scalar gauge cannot change it, because its value on the connected central
fibre is a nonzero constant. Each effective root datum is primitive, so
gcd(m_i,n_i)=1. The actual signed Seifert sphere equation also makes the
nontrivial orders pairwise coprime. Consequently m_i divides bL for every i,
and their product M divides bL. The verified catalogue has b dividing M,
so M/b divides L. K35 constructs the actual endpoint with L=M/b, including
all its ordinary, root and cusp maps. This proves the asserted minimum in
the stated class of endpoints.

The same proof applies to the actual OS49 endpoint problem. The sole root
order is m=3k, the central +G commutator has exact order three, and n=+1 or
-1. Thus every such positive scalar winding L satisfies m|3L, or k|L.
The working K37 construction attains L=k by an explicit threefold descent.
Its full geometric audit remains separate; the divisibility argument itself
uses only the already proved root character and typed endpoint conjugacy.

No division of a residue-class invariant enters this argument. The invariant
being minimized is the positive integer winding of the original scalar
