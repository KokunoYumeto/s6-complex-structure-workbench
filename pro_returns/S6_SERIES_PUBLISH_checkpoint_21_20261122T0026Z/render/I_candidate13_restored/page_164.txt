7. For existence of the corresponding compact family, start with the proper nodal plumbing of
20.6 and base-change it by u = rn. On the cusp cover apply the proper toric modification
defined by the refinement. Its support is unchanged, which is the toric properness criterion
(de Cataldo et al., 2016, Section 2, p.6), and each original affine chart is replaced by a finite
fan. On q ̸= 0 use the subdivision of the top two-dimensional fan to modify the chart ab = rnφ
fibrewise over S. Changes of trivialization multiply a and b by the given nonvanishing line-
bundle units; a torus action extends to every toric fan chart, so these modifications glue. The
same top fan occurs on the overlap with the cusp construction. The core outside the plumbing
charts is unchanged. This defines a proper modification of the base-changed compact nodal
family.

9. The normalization charts are as follows. For a maximal cone with basis (v, v2, v3, v4), let
(m1, m2, m3, m4) be the dual integral basis. The chart of the normalized component is z1 = 0
with coordinates zi = χmi, i = 2, 3, 4. On overlaps the transition matrix is the product of
the two integer basis matrices, and the original bundle units are raised to those same integer
exponents. This is precisely the star fan in N/Zv. When k > 0, (21.3) shows that the period
stabilizer of the ray is trivial. The ray lies in the interior of the full support, so its star is
complete. Its normalized component is therefore the asserted complete smooth toric threefold,
lying over the cusp. When k = 0, the stabilizer is generated by G1. Away from S∞the
resulting component is the ruled bundle PS(O ⊕Lℓ): a character on its open ruled fibre is
W ℓ/rb, which transforms by the ℓth power of the transition of L. The stated star charts
describe its replacement over S∞. The gluing maps are the original period maps on those
same integer characters.
