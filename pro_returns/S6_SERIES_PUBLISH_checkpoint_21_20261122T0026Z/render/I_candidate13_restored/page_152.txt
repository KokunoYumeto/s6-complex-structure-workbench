Proposition 20.4 (The nodal plumbing and its small modification). [tag 01R3]
The first simultaneous local plumbing at the nodal point of S∞has equation
xy = uzv,
q = zv,
(20.1)
after a holomorphic change of one normal coordinate, where q is a local coordinate on B at
infinity. This hypersurface is flat over the u-disc. For each u ̸= 0, its three-dimensional fibre
has an ordinary double point at x = y = z = v = 0. The projective modification defined by the
blow-up of (x, z) resolves that point for u ̸= 0. Its two strict-transform charts are
x = zr,
ry = uv;
z = xa,
y = uav.
(20.2)
The modified family remains flat over u. At u = 0, the normalization of its central fibre contains
the blow-up of one branch of (20.1) along the smooth curve x = z = 0; hence this particular
modification changes the normalization of the unmodified pinching.
Proof. 1. The surface S is smooth and its nodal elliptic fibre has local equation q = zv at its
node. The section ϕ has a simple zero along that fibre. Thus ϕ = zv times an invertible
holomorphic function. Absorbing the function in one normal coordinate gives (20.1). The
convergent power-series ring C{x, y, z, v, u} is factorial, and u is coprime to xy −uzv. Hence
uh ∈(xy −uzv) implies h ∈(xy −uzv). The quotient has no u-torsion. At a nonzero
parameter the identical argument uses u −u0. This proves flatness over the smooth parameter
curve. For fixed u ̸= 0 the four first derivatives vanish only at the origin, and the quadratic
form xy−uzv is nondegenerate. This proves the ordinary-double-point assertion by its defining
equation.
2. The two standard affine charts of the blow-up give (20.2), after removing the common factors
z and x, respectively. For u ̸= 0 the first chart solves for v and the second for y, so both are
smooth. The map is an isomorphism away from the origin in the fixed u-fibre: where the
ideal fails to be visibly principal, the hypersurface equation and one of y, v make it principal.
The fibre over the origin is P1. The modification is projective over (20.1), since it is a closed
subspace of its product with P1. The first strict-transform equation is coprime to u −u0 in
its convergent power-series ring for every u0; the second chart is smooth with free parameter
u. Thus flatness over u persists.
3. At u = 0 the first chart has ry = 0. The branch r = 0 maps isomorphically to x = 0 in
the original central fibre. The branch y = 0, together with the second chart, is the ordinary
blow-up of the branch y = 0 in coordinates (x, z, v) along (x, z). This description follows from
the two affine substitutions defining that blow-up and identifies the normalizations. For u ̸= 0,
the base coordinate in the first chart is q = zry/u and in the second it is q = xav. Thus the
modification also exhibits the triple-coordinate strata that the cusp filling requires.
