Proposition 22.44 (The local plumbing model). [tag 030R]

Xn = {fuv = gsn} ⊂C5,

reduced normal-crossings divisor r = 0. For fixed r ̸= 0 it resolves the cyclic base change of a

reduced; the divisor of the original function q = sn has multiplicity n.

3. The open set F ̸= 0 is the toric open corresponding to nd −a −b = 0. Its fan is the unit
subdivision of the edge i + j = n, hence the minimal An−1 resolution times C∗. This is
precisely the second chart’s resolution under Q = vF. Where that chart meets the first, the
A-type resolution is an isomorphism. These facts glue the local proper resolutions to a proper
modification of the whole local Xn.
4. The function F is a toric monomial. Above F = 1 it is submersive: in a regular toric chart a
nonzero monomial has nonzero derivative in a coordinate with positive exponent. Equivalently,
on F ̸= 0 it is the displayed torus projection. Thus r = g(1 −F) has two smooth transverse
branches in the third chart after resolution. The second chart has r = f(G −1) independently
of the resolved surface coordinates, and the first was already checked. This proves smoothness
and reduced normal crossings over r for all n.
