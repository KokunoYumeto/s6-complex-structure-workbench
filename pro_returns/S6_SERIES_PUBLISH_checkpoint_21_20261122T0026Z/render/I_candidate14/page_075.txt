Remark 11.14 (The local boundary obstruction). [tag 01QO]
The only degenerate base fibre at the parameter boundary lies over t = 1. By 11.13, its
rank-three period quotient is noncompact for rational x + 3/2 and non-Hausdorff for irrational
x + 3/2; neither quotient can be a fibre of a proper family of complex spaces. Proposition 11.15
proves that the elliptic Albanese model also has no holomorphic Weierstrass continuation across
this boundary. The obstruction to a general proper holomorphic extension is proved in lemma 19.3
and theorem 19.4 by recovering the intrinsic divisor.
Proposition 11.15 (The natural boundary of the Albanese invariant). [tag 01QP]
On its half-plane of definition, the j-invariant of Alb(S2) has no meromorphic continuation
across any point of the line Im c = (3/π) log 6. In particular the rank-three quotient at t = 1
cannot be replaced by a Weierstrass family of elliptic Albanese curves whose coefficients are
holomorphic in the same parameter across that line.
Proof. 1. The explicit Albanese lattice in proposition 7.6 is Z + (κ2/2)Z, with κ2 = c + 3/2 −
i(3/π) log 6 by 3.2. A positive-imaginary modulus is z = −κ2/2, so the invariant is j(z). At a
reduced rational r = a/b on the real boundary, choose an integral determinant-one modular
matrix with bottom row (b, −a). Its value at r+iy has imaginary part 1/(b2y). The convergent
Fourier expansion j = q−1 + 744 + O(q) therefore gives growth of order exp(2π/(b2y)) as
y ↓0. A meromorphic function at r is bounded by a fixed power of 1/y, so continuation
there is impossible. A meromorphic continuation near an irrational real point would also
continue the same function near some rational real point in that neighbourhood, which has
just been excluded. The affine change from c to z transfers this obstruction to the stated line.
A Weierstrass family with holomorphic coefficients has a meromorphic j-invariant, expressed
as the quotient of its cubic invariants, so it cannot supply the indicated continuation. This
obstruction applies to continuation of the displayed elliptic Albanese family.
