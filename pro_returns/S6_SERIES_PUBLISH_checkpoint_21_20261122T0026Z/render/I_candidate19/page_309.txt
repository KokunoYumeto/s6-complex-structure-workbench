orems 3.6–3.7, author proof, pp. 21–22) requires a finite algebraically independent family

9. The natural independent triple here is ℘(z), ℘(w), H(z, w), all of order at most two. The first
two are algebraically independent. If H satisfied a polynomial over C(℘(z), ℘(w)), fix a generic
pair (z, w) and translate only z by mω, with 0 ̸= ω ∈Λ. The coefficients of the polynomial
remain constant, whereas the absolute values of its proposed roots H(z + mω, w) grow as
exp(k|ω|2m2 + O(|m|)). This contradicts the finite set of roots of a nonzero polynomial. Thus
the triple is independent.
