2. A biholomorphism of the two surfaces lifts uniquely to a biholomorphism of their normalizations:
integral closure of the reduced local rings is functorial under an isomorphism. It preserves
the inverse image of the singular locus, the hexagon on dP6. Its permutation of the six
components preserves their cyclic incidence, hence lies in the order-twelve dihedral group.
After composition with the corresponding toric lattice automorphism, it fixes each boundary
component. Each torus character has its divisor supported on those components, so its
pullback divided by itself has divisor zero on the compact normalization and is constant. Such
an automorphism is therefore multiplication by an element of (C∗)2. These torus multipliers
cancel in the opposite-side comparison because the same character is used on both sides.

4. For the action of the subtorus with cocharacter e2, a one-dimensional toric orbit on the
normalization is pointwise fixed exactly when its stabilizer line contains e2. Among the six
rays this occurs exactly for ±e2. Their images form one double curve; all zero-dimensional
orbits map to its two endpoints. No dense two-dimensional orbit is fixed. The quotient action
has the same fixed set: a continuous orbit cannot jump between the finitely many branches of
a normalization fibre.
