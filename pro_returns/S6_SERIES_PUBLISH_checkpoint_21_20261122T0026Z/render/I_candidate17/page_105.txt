Remark 14.4 (Fibre restriction of the coefficient representative). [tag 03CT]
The formulas (14.13) and (14.14) choose global smooth forms through all three special fibres.
Their restriction statement uses the pullback map followed by the Dolbeault quotient on a
regular fibre. In particular, its translation-invariant value is the class represented in (13.13).
The specified coefficient form on that fibre can be related to the invariant representative by a
Dolbeault coboundary. The cocycle and weight formulas below describe the changes produced by
these choices.
