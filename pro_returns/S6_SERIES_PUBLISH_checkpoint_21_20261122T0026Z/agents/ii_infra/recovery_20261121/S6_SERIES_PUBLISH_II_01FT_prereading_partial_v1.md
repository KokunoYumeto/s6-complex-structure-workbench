## Regular resonance and its local complex

The marked period columns are (6mu,beta), (tau,mu), (1,0), (0,1), and M_z is the exponential of z times the marked gamma^{0,1}. Its restriction to a regular torus is holomorphically trivial exactly when its scalar character differs from a holomorphic linear functional by an integral character. Eliminating the coefficients on the last two columns gives exactly q tau+r mu=p and z=n-6q mu-r beta, with integers n,p,q,r. For z nonintegral, (q,r) is nonzero. The derivative of these two equations is [[tau',mu'],[-6mu',-beta']] times(q,r). The rank-two ordinary KS matrix away from the three critical values makes this vector nonzero. Thus each regular resonance is an isolated simple intersection of the relative Picard section with zero. A final global finiteness argument still needs to control accumulation at the three critical values by their actual local extension modules.

Near a regular resonance, choose a local base parameter t and a holomorphic cohomology frame in which the twisting Koszul vector is (t,0). A separate vertical frame change makes the invertible KS matrix the identity. The zero Fourier mode Dolbeault complex for T_X tensor M_z has degree0 basis v1,v2,h, degree1 basis v1xi1,v1xi2,v2xi1,v2xi2,hxi1,hxi2 and degree2 basis v1xi12,v2xi12,hxi12. Its differentials are
D(v1)=t v1xi1, D(v2)=t v2xi1, D(h)=v1xi1+v2xi2+t hxi1;
D(v1xi2)=t v1xi12, D(v2xi2)=t v2xi12,
D(hxi1)=-v2xi12, D(hxi2)=v1xi12+t hxi12,
and D(v1xi1)=D(v2xi1)=0. These formulas give D squared zero exactly.

In H2 the relations kill v2xi12, identify v1xi12=-t hxi12, and give t^2 hxi12=0. Thus H2=O/(t^2), with the explicit generator hxi12. A degree-one cycle has coefficients (a,0,c,d,td,0), so it is generated by u=v1xi1, v=v2xi1 and w=v2xi2+t hxi1. The boundaries impose t u=t v=0 and u+w=0. Hence H1=C^2. H0 is zero. This is an exact generators-and-relations calculation of both regular lengths; merely counting fibre dimensions would not determine the nonreduced H2 module.

## Finite central values from the supplied matrices

Solving Pi A_j=J_j Pi gives at p1 tau=rho, mu=(2-rho)/3 and J1=[[rho-1,0],[mu,1]]. The invariant holomorphic linear form is ell1=zeta1+3zeta2. Its four marked periods are (lambda1,2,1,3), where lambda1=3beta+4-2rho. Therefore central resonance is z=n+k lambda1, with q=-k,r=-3k,p=-2k. The admissibility inequality Im beta-6(Im mu)^2/Im tau<0 gives Im lambda1<0, so n,k are unique.

At p2, tau=i, mu=(1-i)/2 and J2=[[-i,0],[1-mu,1]]. The invariant linear form ell2=zeta1+2zeta2 has periods (lambda2,1,1,2), with lambda2=2beta+3-3i. Thus z=n+k lambda2, q=-k,r=-2k,p=-k, uniquely since Im lambda2<0. Nonintegral z has k nonzero.

The affine-vector compatibility is exact: ell1(Pi v1)=lambda1 and ell2(Pi v2)=-lambda2, while the gamma coefficients of v1,v2 are1,-1. Consequently the holomorphic exponential gauge for k ell_j cancels the ratio between the z and n characters on both the lattice and the finite affine lift. The resulting central equivariant line therefore has exactly the M_n character fixed by the root setup. This avoids an unproved sign change between clockwise deck characters and O(n).
