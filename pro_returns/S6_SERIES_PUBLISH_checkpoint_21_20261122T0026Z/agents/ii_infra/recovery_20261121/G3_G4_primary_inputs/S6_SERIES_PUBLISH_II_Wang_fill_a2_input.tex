\begin{proposition}\label{fill:a2}
The punctured family at $\alpha_-$ admits a proper filling with
smooth total space. Its central fiber $W$ has normalization
$\overline W=dP_6$, with opposite boundary curves identified.
The section $e$ extends across this filling, and
specialization induces integral isomorphisms
\[
 \specialize^k:H^k(W;\Z)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^k(X_q;\Z)^T,
 \qquad 0\leq k\leq4.
\]
\end{proposition}
