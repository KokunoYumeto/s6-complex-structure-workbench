# 01FS / cdp:st:C5b: prereading weighted-bundle derivation draft

Target proof, C.8/C.9 and historical target programs remain closed. This working derivation uses only the stated standard smooth X, M_n=barf^*O(n), the supplied current-I E9 root-bundle/direct-image input, the C5a normalization exact sequence, and the original-family Kodaira--Spencer ranks already checked in GN. The final computational certificate and freeze are still pending.

1. On Broot=P(3,4), let x,y have weights3,4, with y=0 at the order-three point p1 and x=0 at p2. The standard construction gives E=O+O(-1), V=R1barf_*O=O+O(-1), R2barf_*O=O(-1), and base logarithmic tangent L=O(-5). The relative log tangent sequence is
0 -> barf^*E -> T_X(-log W) -> barf^*L -> 0.
Its vertical map evaluates translation-invariant fields. On the root torus covers it is the ordinary tangent sequence. At a cusp toric chart q=z1...zr, dlog q maps sum a_i z_i d/dz_i to sum a_i, and its kernel is generated by z_i d/dz_i-z_r d/dz_r. These explicit maps glue under the integral monomial changes of the marked toric charts and identify the extension with the cited E.

2. The connecting morphism delta0:L->E tensor V has entries of weights5,4,4,3. The weight-five section space is zero, the weight-four spaces are spanned by y, and the weight-three space by x. Write its coefficient matrix as [[0,A y],[B y,C x]]. The already verified rank-two value at p2 gives A B nonzero; rank one at p1 gives C nonzero. The exact constant frame changes e0'=A e0, e1'=C e1, eta0'=(B/C)eta0, eta1'=eta1 put the coefficients into [[0,y],[y,x]]. Their inverse scalings are displayed by these nonzero constants, so no unproved matrix identification is used.

3. In the ordered bundle O + O(-1)^2 + O(-2), delta0 is h -> (0,yh,yh,xh). The middle-pair coordinate map (a,b,c)->(a,b-a,c), inverse (a,d,c)->(a,a+d,c), separates an O(-1) summand. The remaining quotient is given explicitly by
O(-1)+O(-2) -> O(2), (a,c)->x a-y c.
Its kernel is h->(yh,xh) from O(-5), and it is surjective because x and y never vanish together. On x nonzero its inverse lift is (z/x,0); on y nonzero it is (0,-z/y). Therefore coker delta0=O+O(-1)+O(2).

4. The next connecting map is exterior multiplication by the same KS class. In the bases eta0,eta1 and eta0 wedge eta1 it is
O(-5)+O(-6) -> O(-1)+O(-2), matrix [[-y,0],[-x,y]].
Its determinant -y^2 is nonzero, so it is injective. Away from p1 it is invertible. On the x-unit chart the relations are -y u-x v=0 and y v=0. Thus v=-y u/x and y^2 u=0; the cokernel is exactly Q=(O/(y^2))u with u carrying the O(-1) fibre character. The maps a mod y^2 -> a u and u->1, v->-y/x are inverse. After tensor by O(n), invariant local sections are precisely combinations y^j u with j=0,1 and j congruent n-1 mod3. There is one invariant for n=1 or2 mod3 and none for n=0 mod3. The coarse parameter y^3 annihilates this module, giving C_p1^{epsilon_n} rather than an assumed reduced quotient of a longer module.

5. The long direct-image sequence of the log tangent extension yields
R0barf_*Tlog=E,
R1barf_*Tlog=O+O(-1)+O(2),
0 -> Q -> R2barf_*Tlog -> O(-6) -> 0.
The last extension splits because Ext1(O(-6),Q)=H1(Q(6))=0. This splitting need not be canonical.

6. C5a gives 0->Tlog->T_X->nu_*K_dP6->0, since the ambient canonical line pulls back trivially on the rational normalization. H0(K_dP6)=H1(K_dP6)=0 and H2(K_dP6)=C. Therefore the first two direct images stay unchanged and R2barf_*T_X is an extension of C_p0 by O(-6)+Q. The Q support p1 is disjoint from p0, so it splits off. The remaining local extension is nonzero: the supplied untwisted E9 statement says the coarse R2f_*T_X=O, which is locally free at p0, whereas a zero extension would give O(-1)+C_p0 there. The nonzero extension 0->O(-6)->?->C_p0->0 is O(-6)(p0)=O(6), since O(p0)=O(12). Locally its injection is multiplication by the base coordinate, with quotient C. Consequently
R2barf_*T_X=O(6)+Q.

7. Projection formula tensors these exact root direct images by O(n). The coarse map has exact invariants in characteristic zero. A weighted line O(k) has a unique residue presentation
k=12d+4r1+3r2, r1 in{0,1,2}, r2 in{0,1,2,3},
with r1 congruent k mod3 and r2 congruent3k mod4. Its invariant local frames are obtained by removing the corresponding root vanishing orders; their transition is the coarse line O(d). Thus the three coarse direct-image formulas in the supplied statement follow, including the invariant Q term in step4.

8. On P1, h0(O(d))=max(d+1,0) and h1(O(d))=max(-d-1,0). The two-column Leray sequence gives, in degrees0..3, H0R0; H1R0 plus H0R1; H1R1 plus H0R2; H1R2, respectively. This gives each stated formula for h0,h1,h2,h3. It does not assert a canonical splitting of those global cohomology exact sequences.

9. The normalization map is, on R2, the quotient O(n+6)->C_p0; the Q summand at p1 maps to zero. Leray surjects global H2 onto H0R2. Hence the global map has rank one exactly when a section of O(n+6) has nonzero value at p0. Both x and y are nonzero there, so any nonzero weighted monomial has nonzero value. The semigroup generated by3,4 is {0,3,4} union all integers at least6. Therefore n is exactly in {-6,-3,-2} union Z_{>=0}. The explicit exceptions are a calculation of this evaluation morphism, not an inference from the dimension of its target.

10. For n=-1, d(n)=d(n-1)=d(n+2)=d(n+6)=-1 and epsilon_n=1. All u/v terms vanish, giving h=(0,0,1,0). Its only R2 contribution is the p1 skyscraper, so the p0 normalization map is zero. This checks the exceptional line directly.
