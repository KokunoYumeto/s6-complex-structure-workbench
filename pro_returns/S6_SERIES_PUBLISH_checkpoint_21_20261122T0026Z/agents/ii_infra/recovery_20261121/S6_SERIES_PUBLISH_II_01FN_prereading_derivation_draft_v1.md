# 01FN / cdp:st:C4d: derivation before target-proof release

Status: the stated full-CDP manifold domain is being bound separately; the derivation uses connected smooth compact Y, H1(Y,Z)=H2(Y,Z)=0, algebraic dimension one, and holomorphic connected-fibre algebraic reduction to P1. These are the explicit current A.4.9 input hypotheses. Target proof closed. Common analytic arguments are in S6_SERIES_PUBLISH_II_C4_prereading_common_arguments_v1.md.

1. The supplied beta is the coefficient lift of the nonzero integral transgression generator. M_z=exp(2 pi i z beta) is a holomorphic line family in z. For each critical full fibre, proper semicontinuity makes B_c a closed analytic subset of C. A proper such subset is discrete and countable. Consequently finitely many proper B_c omit some irrational real parameter r.

2. On every regular fibre, current A.4.9 says H0(Omega_F^1 tensor M_r)=0 for every irrational r. The line M_r itself is nontrivial unitary by D.1.9, so H0(M_r)=0. The ordinary full-fibre conormal sequence 0->O_F->Omega_Y|F->Omega_F->0 makes the ambient H0 zero. This argument uses the actual cotangent-vanishing input across all CDP general-fibre types and imposes no torus-only assumption.

3. Choose the irrational r outside all B_c. Then the ambient H0 groups vanish on every full fibre. Set L=K_Y M_r^-1. Full-fibre duality gives H2(F,(T_Y L)|F)=0 everywhere. The common base-change and positive-base-twist argument supplies a line with global H2 zero. Pic(Y)=H1(Y,O) is connected because H2(Y,Z)=0. Analytic semicontinuity therefore gives a dense open H2 vanishing locus in all Pic(Y). Intersect with the primary generic H0/H3 vanishing loci. The conclusions chi<=0 and c3<=0 follow from the same index identity as in FM.

4. The reduced conormal and full-thickening tests are precisely the long-exact-sequence maps described in the FM derivation and in current A.1.1/A.3.1. On a smooth reduced full fibre, A.4.9 and the nontrivial unitary line make the ordinary conormal sequence vanish. On a full mS with m>=2 and smooth support, current A.3.4 supplies the full scheme-theoretic vanishing, including every nilpotent layer. Thus when all reduced supports are smooth, every B_c is proper, witnessed by any real irrational r.

5. Positive c3 contradicts step3 when all B_c are proper. Hence a fixed critical value has B_c=C. Its reduced support cannot be normal. Indeed, a connected normal reduced fibre support has a single irreducible component: the irreducible components of a normal space are disjoint open-and-closed sets. On a smooth threefold, that support S is a Cartier divisor and is Gorenstein. Adjunction gives c1(omega_S)=0 rationally because both c1(K_Y) and c1(O_Y(S)) vanish in H2(Y,Q). Moreover chi(O_S)=chi(O_Y)-chi(O_Y(-S))=0: the exact divisor sequence and Riemann--Roch have equal Chern characters for the two line bundles. Current A.5.2 therefore makes a normal S smooth. But step4 makes its B_c proper, including the case of a multiple full fibre. This contradicts B_c=C and proves the stated nonnormality without a restriction on the other singular supports or their multiplicities.

No change to a target hypothesis is proposed by this draft; the source-domain binding remains explicit and separate.
