\begin{proof}

\begin{enumerate}[label={$\langle1\rangle$\arabic*.},leftmargin=*]
\item First consider a normalization component $T_i$ whose conductor $D_i$ is nonempty. If $T_i$ is Kähler and has nonnegative Kodaira dimension, choose a nonzero section of $\omega_{T_i}^{\otimes n}$ and denote its effective zero divisor by $E$. The effective divisor $E+nD_i$ has rational Chern class zero by (C.NC.6). Its integral against a Kähler form is positive because $D_i$ is nonempty, whereas its cohomology class gives integral zero. This is impossible. A Kähler $T_i$ is consequently rational or a blown-up ruled surface. For a ruling $T_i\to B$ and a general ruling fibre $F$, one has $K_{T_i}\cdot F=-2$, hence $D_i\cdot F=2$. Some conductor curve therefore dominates $B$. Its normalization has genus at most one by \ref{cdp:st:C.2.NC.2}, so Riemann--Hurwitz forces $g(B)\le1$. The Kähler possibilities have
$\chi(\OO_{T_i})=1$ in the rational case and $0$ in the elliptic ruled case; their Euler numbers are nonnegative.

\item For a non-Kähler $T_i$, the Enriques--Kodaira table \cite[Theorem~2.2]{Peters2021} leaves class VII, Kodaira and properly elliptic minimal models. Class VII has $c_1^2=-b_2$ and $c_2=b_2$, so Noether's formula gives $\chi(\OO)=0$. Kodaira surfaces have both Chern numbers zero. A minimal non-Kähler elliptic surface has topological Euler number zero by Kodaira's theorem in the precise form \cite[Theorem~3.2]{BMS2012}; its canonical square is zero by the same classification table, so Noether's formula again gives $\chi(\OO)=0$. Blowing up preserves the holomorphic Euler characteristic and adds one to the topological Euler characteristic. Thus every non-Kähler $T_i$ has $\chi(\OO_{T_i})=0$ and $e(T_i)\ge0$. This derives all numerical classification facts used here from the cited statements.

\item A rational normalization component cannot meet a different global component of $S$. Indeed, for $j\ne i$, pull back the canonical section of $\OO_Y(S_j)$ to $T_i$. It is a nonzero section whose zeros are precisely the inverse image of $S_i\cap S_j$. Its line bundle has zero rational first Chern class. For a smooth rational surface $T_i$, $H^1(T_i,\OO)=0$ and $H^2(T_i,\Z)$ is torsion-free, by the corresponding facts for $\PP^2$ or a Hirzebruch surface and the blowup formula. The exponential sequence therefore makes that pulled-back line bundle trivial. A nonzero section of a trivial line bundle on a connected compact surface is a nonzero constant and has no zeros. Hence $S_i\cap S_j$ is empty. This proof places no restriction on intersections between two local branches belonging to the same $S_i$.

\item The intersection graph of the global components of a connected reducible fibre is connected. Each component has nonempty conductor, and the preceding paragraph excludes rational normalizations. Thus every $\chi(\OO_{T_i})$ is zero, and (C.NC.4) gives $\tau=0$. Each $C_\alpha$ is now elliptic and has no marked point. The conductor covering is étale, so the normalized conductor components are elliptic; there are no nodes of $D_i$, since these would be preimages of triple points. Consequently $e(D_i)=0$. The weighted Euler formula now reads
\[
 0=e(Y_t)=\sum_i m_i e(T_i).
\]
All summands are nonnegative and every $m_i$ is positive, so each $e(T_i)=0$. Formula (C.NC.5) gives $e(S)=0$.

\item For a singular irreducible support the conductor is nonempty and the same classification argument gives $\chi(\OO_{T_1})\in\{0,1\}$, with value one exactly in the rational case. Formula (C.NC.4) then gives $\tau=0$ or $2$, respectively. This proves the final assertion and also proves that any triple point lies on an irreducible support with rational normalization.

\end{enumerate}
\end{proof}
