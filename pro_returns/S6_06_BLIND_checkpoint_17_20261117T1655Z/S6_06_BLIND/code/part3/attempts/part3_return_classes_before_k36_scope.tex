\subsection{Actual return classes for the verified semifree sphere families}
\begin{statement}{P3F.25}{Proved}
The actual real trace gives $A=(12s_\delta-p_{\rm rel})/24=-R\cdot O$, with both mapping-torus and bordism boundary reversals included. The original, coprime higher, Viaclovsky and Liu--Rollenske values are zero. The positive weighted OS47 loop has $A=-4$, oriented class $24\pmod{28}$, and a mapping torus not diffeomorphic to the product.
\end{statement}
\begin{proof}
Fix the positive Hopf, product-boundary and Eells--Kuiper conventions of P3F.22, and the positive gamma turn of P3K.6--7. The actual return invariant for such a turn is \[
 A=\frac{12s_\delta-p_{\rm rel}}{24}
   =-R\cdot O
   =-\frac{d\,h(R_{\rm source})-2\chi(\mathcal O)
                  +\sum_v\operatorname{contr}_v(R)}2. \tag{P3F.25.1}
\] Here \(d\) is the actual regular-binding cover degree and \(R\) is the section specifying the line, rather than the section specifying the suspension translation. Thus the sign \(\epsilon\) in the prompt's expression is \(-1\) in these conventions. The local height contributions have the positive sign inside the last numerator.

The OS47 row refers to its specified positive \(r\)-loop, \(\lambda=r^4\). It has order seven in \(\Theta _7\), and its mapping torus is not diffeomorphic to \(S^1\times S^6\), including without an orientation requirement.

\textbf{Proof: the actual circle actions.} Away from the scalar-zero fibre, use the actual complement of the zero section of the source line bundle, divided by its contracting lift of translation. A Hermitian norm and a sufficiently small scalar make every positive iterate decrease the norm strictly. A unit-circle rotation can equal an iterate only for the zero iterate, hence is free. This argument works at every smooth point of the original elliptic surface, including the nodal charts and the unchanged type \(II\) chart of Liu--Rollenske. For OS47 it applies to the whole \(I_7\) neighbourhood, with the cyclic component permutation retained, as proved in P3F.21. All these nonzero-linearization local models therefore have the required free circle action.

At a finite good-reduction cap of order \(m\), any putative line circle stabilizer must equal an affine cyclic deck transformation. Its invariant gamma displacement is \(jn/m\), where the logarithmic coefficient satisfies \(\gcd(n,m)=1\). The line circle has zero gamma displacement, so \(m\mid j\). The residual circle action on the root torus is free. This excludes all extra stabilizers at the finite caps, including the higher coprime multiplicities.

At the unique scalar-zero \(I_1\) fibre the valuation vectors form an integral basis. The unit-square triangulation gives exactly one fixed \(\mathbb P^1\) with normal \(\mathcal O(-1)\oplus\mathcal O(-1)\), of weights \((-1,+1)\), and is free off that curve. This is the exact local model in P3F.7. Thus each action in the table is semifree with precisely this fixed curve. The local orbit product and the real compression in P3K.6 apply at its chosen \(I_1\) cusp; the other singular fibres lie in the graph page and require no cusp replacement in that construction. The line matching in P3K.6 uses the integral meridian class on the entire complement, so it also retains their original circle line.

\textbf{Proof: characteristic numbers and the parameter direction.} The actual spin bordism P3K.7 compares the positive geometric family, with its circle Euler line, with the normal sphere bundle of the capped real trace. Its boundary directions are part of the comparison. Let \(f\) denote the quotient of the physical positive return in P3F.8. Write \(Q^+\) for its geometric mapping torus, identifying \((z,1)\sim(f(z),0)\). Then \(Q^+=M_{f^{-1}}\) in Jin's convention. With the circle-first quotient orientation and the complex orientation on the graph page, the capped complement of that graph has oriented boundary \(-Q^+\sqcup(-B)\). Indeed \(\partial(Y^5\times D^2)=-Y\times S^1\), and the inner normal sphere is a deleted-tube boundary. Hence the characteristic number on \(Q^+\) is the negative of that on \(B\), whereas it is also \(-A(f)\) by the reverse mapping-torus convention. These two signs cancel. Thus the actual physical return satisfies \[
 A=\frac{3b^2-p_1(T\bar X)}{24},\qquad
 b^2=4s_\delta,\quad
 p_1(T\bar X)=p_{\rm rel}.
\] The comparison is through that explicit bordism, not an identification of its cap with the usual elliptic cap. The relative square argument allows the numerical evaluation on the latter. On the compact elliptic page, the section line is \(R-O\) up to an integral fibre class. Since \(R^2=O^2=-\chi(\mathcal O)\), its square is \(-2\chi(\mathcal O)-2R\cdot O\). The tangent comparison gives \(p_{\rm rel}=3\sigma=-24\chi(\mathcal O)\). Substitution proves the first two equalities in \(P3F.25.1\). The third is exactly the height formula and degree multiplication in P3F.2.

For the original family its explicitly oriented sixfold parameter cover makes the positive gamma turn by P3F.13. The table gives zero for that cover. Additivity in the integral Torelli group, P3F.11, implies zero for one original parameter loop. The same argument with the actual \(12kl\) scalar period treats the coprime higher multiplicities; its page data are P3F.17. The one-log families close on the scalar cover \(\lambda=r^4\): the same finite-cap equation as in P3F.23 gives the ordinary deck \(-\log(t-t_c)\delta/(2\pi i)\), and their nodal periods have multiplier \(q r^4\). The two-origin subtraction of P3F.23, with the corresponding holomorphic \(\Gamma,C\), gives their actual gamma turn. Their values are zero; any smaller descended parameter loop has zero as well by integral additivity. The locally labelled surface-deformation parameter of Viaclovsky ranges over a disc, so its loops are contractible. The separate labelled comb-smoothing loop has the explicit identity trivialization of P3F.16.

For OS47 the exact subtraction P3F.23 establishes one positive gamma turn for the positive \(r\)-loop. Its actual page has \(s_\delta=-14\) and \(p_{\rm rel}=-72\). Hence \[
 A=\frac{-168+72}{24}=-4,
 \qquad\mu(\Sigma_\Phi)=-\frac4{28}.
\] P3F.22 gives the displayed oriented homotopy-sphere class, and P3F.24 gives its nonproduct mapping-torus obstruction for both spin structures.

For the primitive one-log extensions of the two \(III^*\) inputs with order \(4k\), the same actual page calculation gives respectively \(12kI_1\) or \(4kII+4kI_1\), degree \(4k\), \(\chi=k\), height \(2k\) and \(R\cdot O=0\). Thus their gamma-return integer is zero by the identical trace calculation. This page computation does not replace the separate integral topology check selecting sphere members among all logarithmic data.
\end{proof}
