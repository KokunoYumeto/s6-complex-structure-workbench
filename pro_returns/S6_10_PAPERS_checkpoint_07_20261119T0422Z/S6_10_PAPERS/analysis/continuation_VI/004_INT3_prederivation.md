1. INT.2 removes Enoki minimal models. Each normalization resolution is a blowup of a minimal Hopf or non-Kähler elliptic surface, because the adjacent-divisor argument excludes Kähler models and the nonempty rationally trivial divisor classifies the remaining class-VII model. INT.1 makes every surviving strict elliptic curve map onto an elliptic-normalized curve and makes the actual ambient Picard restriction nonconstant.
2. With ν:⊔T_i→S, A_i the reduced inverse conductor and C its reduced image, topological additivity for the actual finite normalization gives e(S)=Σ_i e(T_i\A_i)+e(C). No conductor scheme multiplicity enters this topological identity, but it must remain in the coherent calculations used to establish the signs.
3. On a smooth minimal Hopf or non-Kähler elliptic component e(T_i)=0. Any union of disjoint elliptic curves has e=0. Under a point blowup of a smooth surface, e increases by one; a blowup on a curve replaces its point by an exceptional P¹, so the full inverse-image curve e also increases by one and the complement e is unchanged. A blowup off the curve increases complement e by one. Contracting a connected rational tree reverses these counts; contracting an elliptic curve changes e by +1. A proof of nonnegativity must match each contraction to its preceding actual blowups so that no negative remainder is lost.
4. The conductor image is a union of curves with elliptic normalization plus possible images of exceptional rational curves. The exact formula is e(C)=Σ_D(2−2g(D̃))−Σ_p(r_p−1). Thus e(C)≥0 is not supplied merely by elliptic normalization: branch identifications contribute negative terms. A coherent argument using the actual conductor scheme and a generic ambient Picard twist is required to rule out or compensate these terms.
5. Choose an ambient topologically trivial L avoiding the finitely many triviality kernels on the elliptic curves; nonconstancy is INT.1. On each minimal elliptic model the restriction is nontrivial on the elliptic fibre, so fibre cohomology and then total cohomology vanish; on Hopf, avoid the discrete effective-character loci. Blowups preserve these cohomology groups. The normalization-conductor exact sequence, plus finite duality and Serre duality on the full fibre, must determine H⁰(C,O_C) or H¹(C,O_C) sufficiently to force the displayed sign. I have not yet derived that exact connecting morphism.
6. Once the two nonnegative terms in step 2 are established, their zero cases must force smooth minimal normalizations and smooth disjoint elliptic conductor curves, with unramified finite maps. Conversely those descriptions make both terms zero by additivity and Riemann–Hurwitz. The forward equality implication cannot be inferred without the exact contraction and conductor argument in steps 3--5.
