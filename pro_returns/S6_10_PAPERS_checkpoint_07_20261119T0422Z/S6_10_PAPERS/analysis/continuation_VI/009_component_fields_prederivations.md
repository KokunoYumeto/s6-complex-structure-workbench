## UN.3

Construct V=Bl_{(p,0)}(E×Δ), with exceptional R and strict central elliptic curve T. Let L_n=O_V(nR). The quotient L_n^*/⟨q⟩ for a constant 0<|q|<1 is a proper principal elliptic bundle over V; multiplying a nonzero fibre coordinate by q is free and properly discontinuous, and its quotient fibre is C*/q^Z. On V away from R, the defining section trivializes L_n, so the punctured family is the same product E×(C*/q^Z)×Δ*. Its two central components are the elliptic bundle of Chern degree n over T and the elliptic bundle of degree −n over R≅P¹. Their common fibre is elliptic. All three Euler numbers vanish, so the reduced central support has Euler number zero.

For n=1 the first component is a primary Kodaira surface (nonzero elliptic-bundle Chern class over an elliptic curve) and the second is the primary Hopf quotient of O_{P¹}(−1)^*≅C²\{0}. For n>1 the elliptic-base component remains a primary Kodaira surface with H₁≅Z³⊕Z/n, computed from the circle-bundle Gysin or fundamental-group commutator relation [a,b]=c^n. The rational-base component can have a finite Hopf quotient; no primary claim is needed for it in the infinite family.

An actual bimeromorphic correspondence of smooth threefold fillings has a common smooth-centre resolution after shrinking around the compact central support. Every exceptional prime is ruled over a curve or is a blowup thereof, or is rational. Thus a non-uniruled central component survives as the same bimeromorphism class in both fillings. The Kodaira component cannot correspond to the smooth torus component: their H₁ ranks are 3 and 4. Different n have different H₁ torsion and so cannot be bimeromorphic surfaces. In a finite base change, the strict component over a multiplicity-one generic point is unchanged: locally x=s^d after absorbing a unit. The additional resolution primes are ruled. Hence the same obstruction persists after any finite base change. Compact realization follows from the identical line-bundle construction over a blowup of E×P¹; the assertion about the particular D.2.7 model still requires comparison with its explicit gluing.

## UN.15 and the field construction of UN.16

At a generic smooth point of a multiplicity-m component, f=u z^m. In the normalized root base change t=s^m, choose a local mth root of u; the chosen branch is z=s/u^{1/m}, so the strict component has multiplicity one. Resolve away from the smooth locus over Δ*. The coherent direct image of the vertical tangent sheaf has generic rank two, since every smooth torus has exactly its two translation fields. Over the disc it gives a rank-two meromorphic vector space over C({s}). A section can be divided by its exact vanishing order along E; it becomes a meromorphic field regular and nonzero at the generic point of E. It is vertical, hence tangent to E. On nearby tori its restriction is a scalar multiple of a translation and is complete. On the open ambient locus on which it is holomorphic, Guillot's closed uniformizability theorem extends semicompleteness to E. This is an actual field on the chosen covering component.

A nonzero semicomplete meromorphic field excludes general type. An algebraic leaf closes in a compact curve; semicompleteness forbids a pole there (a pole of order k gives local time z^{k+1}, with branching), so its normalization has genus at most one. A fibration by these curves has Kodaira dimension at most one. When there is a leaf not contained in any proper analytic set, the assumed general-type covering surface is projective, so Guillot's 2019 theorem applies with exactly its Kähler and dense-leaf hypotheses; its holomorphic-field or rational/elliptic-fibration alternatives both exclude general type. Generically finite pullback of pluricanonical forms preserves their growth, hence a general-type original component would give a general-type covering component, the contradiction.

To obtain two C-independent fields, let M be a free rank-two lattice of vertical sections and L the submodule of M⊗C({s}) regular at the generic point of E. The determinant of two chosen fields has finite vanishing order d; a Cramer's-rule bound gives M⊂L⊂s^{−d}M after choosing the initial fields regular. Thus L is a free rank-two C{s}-lattice. Its restriction kernel is sL, so L/sL is two-dimensional and injects into meromorphic fields on E. Commutators vanish on every nearby torus and hence as meromorphic fields, since their coefficients depend only on s and all fields are vertical. Every linear combination has the same semicompleteness argument.

If the generic rank is one, write Y=hX with h nonconstant meromorphic; C-linear independence forces nonconstancy. Then [X,hX]=X(h)X gives X(h)=0. Resolve the graph of h and take Stein factorization. The general connected fibre inherits a nonzero semicomplete meromorphic field and therefore has genus zero or one. If a(E)=0, this rank-one alternative cannot occur. Guillot's compact-surface Theorem 9.2 sends each meromorphic field birationally to a holomorphic one because there is no meromorphic fibration. Pushing each field to the unique minimal model retains independence. The opened DOT classification and dimension bound leave a torus or Hopf surface: K3 has no holomorphic field, positive-b₂ class VII has at most one, and Inoue has at most one. The exact Inoue dimension statement is a cited-result gate pending its locator; it must not be replaced by an unverified general class-VII assertion.

## UN.17

The rank-one direct image f_*K_U is torsion-free on the disc. A generator evaluates to a nonzero holomorphic canonical section, nowhere zero on any smooth torus fibre; its divisor is Σa_jS_j with a_j≥0. Adjunction with the intrinsic conductor gives K_{Z_i}+D_i=h_i*(K_U+S_i). The principal full fibre gives m_i h_i*S_i=−Σ_{j≠i}m_j E_ij. Multiplying adjunction by m_i gives exactly Σ_{j≠i}[m_i a_j−m_j(a_i+1)]E_ij. Pushing forward the divisor of the resulting meromorphic pluricanonical section to the minimal model retains support on the displayed curve images.

For a positive-b₂ minimal class VII model with negative-definite curve matrix Q and nonnegative off-diagonal entries, adjunction gives K·C≥0 for every irreducible curve C (smooth rational curves have selfintersection at most −2; singular rational or elliptic curves have arithmetic genus at least one). A pluricanonical divisor supported on these curves has coefficient vector v with Qv≥0. The inverse of such a negative-definite matrix is entrywise nonpositive, so v≤0. Thus −v is effective and nonzero, yielding the required numerically anticanonical divisor; the residual zero Chern class is complex flat after a positive tensor power. The semidefinite exceptional alternative is the Enoki rational cycle: a canonical divisor supported only on that cycle would have square zero, contradicting K²=−b₂<0, so the parabolic case with elliptic curve is forced and K+E+C=0 supplies the divisor. For b₂=0, a curve-free Inoue surface would force a torsion canonical line, which its explicit universal-cover character forbids. Precise minimal-class-VII curve-matrix and Inoue source points remain to compare. The opened Dloussky numerically anticanonical theorem then gives the global spherical shell, without assuming an unrestricted class-VII classification.

## UN.19

The root cover's cyclic stabilizer G gives the exact quotient of its chosen component; normalization of that quotient is bimeromorphic to S_i^ν. Finite maps preserve algebraic dimension zero because meromorphic functions on the cover satisfy their orbit polynomial over the base, and conversely pullback is injective. Hence UN.16 makes the covering minimal model a torus or Hopf. Birational automorphisms of a non-uniruled minimal surface are holomorphic, so G descends to that minimal model. A finite torus quotient has even first Betti number: H¹ of the quotient is the invariant subspace of the torus H¹, invariant under its complex structure; resolution adds rational exceptional curves and preserves H¹. Thus it cannot yield the stated class-VII component with b₁=1. A finite Hopf quotient is again bimeromorphic to Hopf via its lifted contraction and finite action on C²\{0}; the exact primary/secondary branch-locus analysis is a preread gate, to be compared rather than silently assumed.

## UN.20

The stabilizer acts semilinearly on the actual s-valuation lattice L; modulo s it preserves its two-dimensional field space. In rank one its action on h=Y/X is a fractional-linear transformation. Therefore the field C(h), and its algebraic closure in the meromorphic field of E, are G-invariant. Resolving the graph equivariantly yields the Stein fibration q:Ehat→B with an action on B and quotient map to B/G. For a general point of B/G, the finitely many fibres of q over its orbit form a single G-orbit, and their quotient is connected. More exactly q_*O=O_B gives (q_*O)^G=O_{B/G}, so B/G is already the Stein base. The quotient curve of a rational or elliptic fibre has genus zero or one by Riemann--Hurwitz. A rational general fibre would make the resolved original surface uniruled by its displayed ruling, and thus have Kodaira dimension −∞; the stated nonnegative-Kodaira or non-uniruled cases therefore have elliptic general fibre.
