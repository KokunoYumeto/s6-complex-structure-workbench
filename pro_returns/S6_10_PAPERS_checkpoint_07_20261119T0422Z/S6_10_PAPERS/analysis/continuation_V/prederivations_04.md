## maps:src:F.3.8 — spanning entire lines in D3

Use the graph chart Z=iI in Mat_3(C). For a real rank-one matrix Q=u v^t with v^t u=0, the entire line Z=iI+zQ lies in the period domain: Im Z=I+(Im z)Q has determinant one. Six off-diagonal matrix units together with Q_7=(e_1+e_2)(e_1-e_2)^t and Q_8=(e_2+e_3)(e_2-e_3)^t are eight independent traceless directions. Take Q_9 with leading 2-by-2 block [[1,-1],[1,1]] and last row and column zero. Then det(I+yQ_9)=(1+y)^2+y^2 is positive for all real y; its trace is two, so these nine directions span Mat_3(C). The linear map C^9 -> Mat_3(C), (z_j)->iI+sum z_jQ_j, is a submersion; the inverse image of D3 is open and contains every coordinate line. Thus it restricts to a spanning tube of complex lines. Conjugation by GL^+(6,R) transports the construction to any point of the chosen component. The exact cited LSAP consequence of domination by these tubes must be checked in the primary theorem; the geometric construction above is complete.

## maps:src:F.3.9 — sections over an open Riemann surface

The base has the homotopy type of a one-dimensional CW complex. A bundle with connected fibre has a continuous section on this complex: choose a point over every vertex and join its transported endpoints over each edge by a fibre path. Pull back through the homotopy equivalence. The stated relative LSAP section theorem, applied to the associated holomorphic flat bundle, produces a holomorphic section with the approximation/interpolation in its scope. Given F transverse to conjugation, the quotient of the rank-six flat holomorphic vector bundle by F is a rank-three bundle in which the real lattice has injective discrete cocompact images. Quotienting by those lattice translations gives the prescribed torus family and its zero section. The properness of each torus fibre follows from the real-linear isomorphism R^6 -> C^6/F.

## maps:src:F.3.10 — isolated extension points

Choose an exhaustion of the open Riemann surface by Runge compact bordered domains, starting with a neighbourhood of K and with no critical point on S. All later bumps and handle arcs are disjoint from S. On every such addition the bundle has its LSAP fibre, so the usual approximation-with-sprays step applies there. Gluing to the already holomorphic section near the old compact is local in the open relative Grassmannian: identify nearby planes with graphs in a fixed complementary holomorphic subbundle, split their small transition maps, and approximate their coefficients while imposing the prescribed finite jets. Choose successive errors summable and smaller than the distance to the complement of the open subset on each prior compact. The limit is holomorphic and remains in the open subset, including on the retained neighbourhood of S. Homotopies are concatenated with summable errors and retained there. No extension of a fibre-bundle structure across S is used. Exact global availability of the complementary subbundle and the cited spray/gluing statements remains to be checked against the printed proof and primary references; this is a derivation of the local-to-global mechanism, not yet a blanket proof certificate.

## maps:src:F.2.36 — algebraic gate for simultaneous conjugacy

The rational intertwiner space of two tuples is the solution of a finite linear system, and integral intertwiners form its intersection with Mat_6(Z). A unimodular intertwiner is exactly a lattice-module isomorphism. For a tuple containing N^2=0 of rank three, its endomorphism algebra embeds in the centralizer of N, namely Mat_3(Q[epsilon]/epsilon^2), after a rational cusp marking. Projection to Mat_3(Q) has a square-zero kernel. The semisimple quotient of the image acts on the semisimplification of a three-dimensional rational module; each simple matrix factor is over a division algebra with a nonzero rational module of dimension at most three. A noncommutative division algebra in characteristic zero has square dimension at least four over its centre, so no such division factor occurs. Therefore all division factors are number fields. This verifies the structural hypothesis for the cited lattice-isomorphism algorithm, provided the source's exact input hypothesis is the semisimple endomorphism-algebra quotient just identified. Primary-source algorithm scope remains to be checked; do not upgrade this paragraph alone to a full independent proof.

## maps:src:F.2.38 — involutions and the number 56

Fix the primitive cusp N=[[0,I],[0,0]]. The product relation gives M2=M1(I-N). Both M1 and M2 square to I precisely when M1NM1=-N. Solving yields M1=[[P,Q],[0,-P]], P^2=I, PQ=QP, and M2=[[P,Q-P],[0,-P]]. Conjugation by [[A,B],[0,A]] changes P to APA^-1 and changes Q, after A transport, by an anticommutator PR+RP. Integral involutions P on rank three have modules Z_+^a + Z_-^b + Z[C2]^c with a+b+2c=3: the fixed and anti-fixed eigensublattices have an overlattice quotient killed by two, and each glued pair supplies one regular block. This follows by choosing dual bases for the mod-two overlap and lifting them; the remaining eigenspace basis vectors give the fixed and anti-fixed summands.

On a regular block [[0,1],[1,0]], commuting Q matrices [[u,v],[v,u]] are all anticommutators with arbitrary integral R. The same direct block equations kill every cross-term involving a regular summand. Between the plus and minus one-dimensional summands a commuting Q has zero off-diagonal terms. The remaining Q is therefore a pair of matrices over F2 of sizes a and b, modulo independent similarity by GL_a(F2) and GL_b(F2); the integral centralizer induces all those invertible reductions. Conversely these two similarity classes are invariant under every integral conjugation preserving P, by the quotient of End_{Z[C2]}(P) by anticommutators. The sizes 0,1,2,3 have 1,2,6,14 similarity classes. Thus c=0 contributes 14+12+12+14=52 and c=1 contributes 2+2=4, giving 56.

Every M_i has a rank-three fixed lattice. Choose a primitive integral fixed vector u. The affine lift x->M_i x+u/4 has actual order four. Its square is translation by u/2, which is nonzero; an odd power could have a fixed point only if u/4 belonged to the projection of the lattice to the fixed real space. Twice that projection is contained in the integral fixed lattice, contradicting primitivity of u. Thus both affine lifts are free. Every actual affine order is divisible by the linear order two, so two such orders have gcd at least two and their orbifold quotient prevents simple connectivity.
