## night:P1A.1, before reading its proof

On the (5,4) root curve put L=O((4/5)[infinity]-(3/4)[0]). For every integer r the coarse direct image has degree floor(4r/5)+floor(-3r/4); characteristic zero makes the finite-root pushforward exact. Thus line cohomology reduces to O(d) on P^1, with h0=max(d+1,0) and h1=max(-d-1,0). In particular r=-1,-2,-3 have d=-1 and no cohomology, while r=0 has one section and no H1.

The de Rham Hodge bundle splits on the (5,8) curve as T^3+T+T^-1+T^-3 once its holomorphic extension obstruction vanishes. The positive Hodge part is T^3+T; H1 of the positive tensor factors controlling that extension vanishes by the root-floor calculation. Taking the image of the T^3 line wedged with the full de Rham bundle and then the primitive quotient yields weights T^4,T^2,O, namely L^2,L,O. Its lift through the invariant-line flat extension is isomorphic to this bundle because the projection is injective. Consequently F=O+L+L^2 and E0=F^vee=O+L^-1+L^-2. The ambient rank-six holomorphic bundle, and the quotient by F, split because the relevant H1 of the line-weight differences vanish; the quotient has weights O,L^-1,L^-2. The quotient is R^1h_*O on the smooth locus.

At the Kuhn central fibre, normalized strata are complete toric varieties, whose higher O-cohomology vanishes, and the normalization resolution for O of a reduced normal-crossings divisor computes the cohomology of the dual T^3. It gives H^q(O_C)=wedge^q C^3 with its ring structure. The multiplicities at the other two points are removed on the root charts. Grauert base change and this exterior product then extend R^qh_*O=wedge^q G across all fibres. The rank-three vertical cotangent determinant is det F=L^3. The relative logarithmic canonical determinant and the base logarithmic canonical determinant cancel their cusp terms, giving K_M=h^*(K_B tensor det F)=h^*(L^-9 tensor L^3)=h^*L^-6. Finally the Leray sequence over a curve, with all negative weights -1,-2,-3 acyclic and O contributing only H0, gives h^q(O_M)=(1,1,0,0,0).

The splitting assertions here are the precise points to compare against the printed proof: their cohomology classes and the relevant orbifold extensions must match this calculation. The central-fibre normalization calculation requires the normal-crossing Kuhn model already established in P1.9.

## maps:src:F.2.7, before reading its proof

The punctured-base torus bundle has a map of fundamental groups to the free fundamental group of the punctured sphere. Killing the fibre lattice throughout the van Kampen presentation leaves each multiple-fibre meridian x_i of order m_i and the product relation. The Kuhn cusp meridian is killed by the disc filling. This gives exactly the displayed orbifold quotient. With two multiple points, substitution x2=x1^-1 gives a cyclic quotient of order gcd(m1,m2). With three orders at least two, the orientation-preserving triangle group is nontrivial: its sphere, Euclidean plane or hyperbolic plane triangle realization provides a nonidentity rotation of the specified order. For multiplicity one the local action is trivial and there is an ordinary disc bundle, so deleting that marked point has no effect on the fibration problem.

## maps:src:F.2.15, before reading its proof

The affine multiplicities m1,m2 are coprime by the preceding quotient. Each linear order divides the respective affine multiplicity. The cusp determinant is one, so det A=det B. At least one affine order is odd; that matrix has determinant one, so both do. In a finite real representation, nonreal eigenvalues occur in complex conjugate pairs. Determinant one forces an even multiplicity of -1, and rank six then forces an even multiplicity of +1 as well. A real basis pairs the +1 and -1 eigendirections into invariant real two-planes; the nonreal pairs already carry the complex structure given by multiplication by i. The direct sum is an explicit real complex structure commuting with the individual matrix. The two constructed structures are local choices and no common period plane is inferred.

## night:P1A.2 and night:P1A.3: prereading gates

The previous direct-image argument determines only the (0,q) Hodge row and, by Serre duality, the (4,q) row. It does not determine all intermediate Hodge numbers. The logarithmic cotangent extension has base line Omega_B^1(log cusp)=L^11 and vertical part F=O+L+L^2. Its exterior powers and residues along the Kuhn strata provide a finite complex of root-line bundles. To establish the intermediate rows one must compute its actual connecting maps, not just Euler characteristics. The stated Hodge table satisfies the independent necessary values chi_y=(0,-1,4,-1,0), Euler number 6 and signature 2; these checks alone do not establish it.

For tangent cohomology the logarithmic tangent extension has vertical E0 and base T_B(-cusp)=L^-11. Ordinary tangent fields fit into the quotient by the Jacobian ideal of the normal-crossings fibre. This ideal, rather than the whole structure sheaf of the fibre, must enter the boundary calculation. A nonzero Kodaira-Spencer map from a one-dimensional smooth family to a Kuranishi germ with tangent dimension one forces its defining analytic obstruction ideal to vanish: a nonzero convergent one-variable germ has an isolated zero, incompatible with the nonconstant family. This derives the unobstructedness implication after the stated h1 and nonzero KS map are established. The exact log-to-ordinary connecting maps remain the prereading gate.
