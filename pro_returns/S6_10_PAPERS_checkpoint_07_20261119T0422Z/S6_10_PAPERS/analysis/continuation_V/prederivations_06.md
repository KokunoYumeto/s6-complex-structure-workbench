# Prereading derivation, batch 06 — polarized-source obstruction

Written from the statement of maps:src:F.3.4 and the cited semisimplicity/fixed-part result before opening its printed proof.

For the proposed construction over the punctured base, the degree-zero line bundle is topologically trivial on each abelian surface fibre. The C*-bundle therefore gives a rank-five first-homology extension 0 -> Qe -> E -> H -> 0, where H is the rank-four abelian-surface system and e is the circle in C*. Taking the quotient by the lifted translation adds the deck-generator quotient, giving 0 -> E -> T -> Q -> 0. The circle and deck generator are globally fixed, so the outer lines have trivial monodromy. This is the precise filtration 0 subset Q subset E subset T supplied by the geometric operations, without presuming the two sections proportional.

Deligne's cited polarized algebraic semisimplicity implies H is semisimple. Its invariant part K is a direct summand and has zero pairing with the sum H' of the nontrivial irreducible constituents: any such pairing would give a nonzero equivariant map H' -> a trivial module. The polarization is nondegenerate, so its restriction to K is nondegenerate and dim K is even. The cusp has square-zero NH of rank two on H. On H' its rank is at most half dim H'; since K contributes zero rank, rank NH=2 and dim H=4 force dim K=0. The dual invariants vanish as well.

Consequently E has exactly the invariant line Qe. A second invariant in T would map nontrivially to the trivial top quotient, and after scaling would split T=E plus Q as representations. On E, write NE(e,h)=(beta(h),NH h). Square-zero gives beta NH=0. Since NH has rank two and kernel=image in dimension four, beta vanishes on ker NH. Thus the map h -> (beta(h),NH h) has kernel ker NH and rank two. A split top line would therefore make rank NT=2, contradicting the required rank-three cusp. Hence dim T^Gamma=1. Apply the identical argument to the dual filtration: the dual middle H* is again semisimple without invariants, and the dual cusp has the same rank. Therefore dim (T*)^Gamma=1.

The Leray/parabolic Euler relation already independently derived for F.3.1/F.3.2, under b1=b2=0, reads sum r_i=6k-8+b with b=dim T^Gamma=1. Every local finite action is holomorphic, hence r_i is even; the right side is odd. This contradiction proves the stated obstruction. This argument uses the exact geometric filtration and semisimplicity, not an assertion that every arbitrary extension is self-dual.
