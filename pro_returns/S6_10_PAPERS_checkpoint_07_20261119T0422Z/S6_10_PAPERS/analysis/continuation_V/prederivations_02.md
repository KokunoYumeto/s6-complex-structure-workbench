The common invariant group is the kernel of the vertical stack of M_i-I. The coinvariants are the cokernel of their horizontal concatenation. Transposing yields the corresponding cohomology module. ## night:P1A.4: independent necessary tests before reading its proof

The claimed group is C x Z/2. An automorphism preserving a torus fibration acts on the lattice by an integral monodromy centralizer and conjugates translations by its complex linear derivative. A nontrivial automorphism of the base would have to preserve the three distinct special fibres of orders 5,4 and the cusp, hence fixes three points and is the identity. The rational centralizer of the explicitly supplied rank-six monodromy therefore constrains the component group. If the nontrivial component is represented by inversion on the torus, its conjugation sends a translation t to -t; this is an exact semidirect action and must be checked against the printed product symbol. In addition, the rank-one invariant cohomology class transgresses nontrivially to H^2 of the base because b_1=b_2=0; a base-preserving automorphism must preserve that transgression. It therefore acts as +1 on the invariant cohomology line. These two necessary tests are computable without the printed argument. They do not by themselves establish the full automorphism theorem or the existence of a nontrivial component.

## night:P1A.6: bounded parameter and descent

The Cayley transform of the parameter half-plane is a bounded holomorphic function on a punctured disc, so extends holomorphically to the full disc. A boundary value at the puncture has modulus 1; the maximum modulus principle would force the map to be constant on the full disc, contradicting that its punctured values lie inside. Hence the extended value is interior. To convert a family of isomorphism classes to an actual pullback requires local miniversality with nonzero Kodaira-Spencer map and triviality of a torsor under the automorphism group. The connected additive group has H^1(Delta*,O)=0 by Stein vanishing; a component group of order two is killed by a degree-two cover. The numerical degree bound therefore depends on the exact component group in P1A.4. With connected automorphism group alone, no cover would be needed. The extension of the parameter map has been rederived; the remaining statements require checking the claimed automorphism group and the global coarse-moduli map.
