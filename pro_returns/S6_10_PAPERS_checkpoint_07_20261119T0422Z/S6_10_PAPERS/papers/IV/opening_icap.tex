\subsection{Every reduced cap for the selected-I3 loop}
\RungZero{The selected-I3 result extends to every $M_6$ reduced
cap $m=3l+2$, $l\geq0$, both primitive norm signs, every admitted
finite character and the full prescribed annular reference. Each
cap uses its common strict scalar range with the quadratic
reference. Both characteristic integers and every native fan map
are preserved. Each such cap therefore realizes all twenty-eight
classes, with the same explicit witness words.}
\RungOne
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
\item On the unchanged original source, the selected discriminant
  height is $q_c=t-t_c(s)$, where
  $t_c=(1-36s-(1+12s)^{3/2})/108$.
  The other height uses
  $t_o=(1-36s+(1+12s)^{3/2})/108$.
  On $q_\infty=z^m$, the full coefficient ratio is
  $H=(1-t_cz^m)/(1-t_oz^m)$. Its deck-invariant logarithm
  vanishes at $z=0$ and defines the unique full root $H^{1/m}$
  (\cref{night:P3K.108}, steps 1--2).
\item The actual original-source rescaling is
  $t=z^{-m}$, $x=z^{-2(l+1)}X$, $y=z^{-3(l+1)}Y$.
  It gives the complete $UV=X^3$ root resolution. The proper
  graph of $(\tau',z^{2l+1})$ retains both Cartier generators
  through their coincidence, with coefficients
  $u/(u-s\ell)$ and $(1-vs\ell)^{-1}$.
  The central-one chord lift and the full Miller norm give
  $B'_c=HB'_o$ and
  $\widetilde F_c=H^{1/m}\widetilde F_o$
  (\cref{night:P3K.108}, steps 2--4).
\item The signed lifts retain $A^m=B^n$ and
  $J=A^n$, $J^m=B$, for $n=\pm1$.
  Their complete sector equation carries the original source
  period, line lattice, integer cochain and prescribed reference.
  The weighted norm for $J$ gives the compact proper cap
  quotient. Pulling the same boundary horizontal field through
  each full annular derivative and integrating its relative
  circle-invariant extension gives the actual cap products
  with exactly matched boundaries
  (\cref{night:P3K.108}, steps 5--6).
\item The signed meridian matrices have the literal integral
  comparison
  \[
  \begin{pmatrix}m&1\\n&0\end{pmatrix}
  \begin{pmatrix}n&0\\2-mn&1\end{pmatrix}
  =\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
  \]
  The physical Euler restriction remains $(0,-1)$ through the
  held-Cox tautological line. The explicit primitive Hopf and
  conifold restriction maps retain the orientation, and the
  ordered source arc carries the actual E6-B sphere reference
  (\cref{night:P3K.108}, step 7).
\item On each physical cap quotient the matched line and tangent
  connections pull back from its five-dimensional body.
  The forms $x^3$ and $p_1x$ therefore vanish separately there;
  the full collar arrows match their boundary transgressions
  with the identical exterior connections. Both completed
  characteristic integers consequently agree with the
  quadratic reference (\cref{night:P3K.108}, step 8).
\item The selected fixed tube and its physical Hopf completion
  remain the same. Every IVP height inverse and every AFI Cox,
  quaternion and native-reset map therefore applies in that
  identical tube. Induction along the finite fan word carries
  the full integer algorithm and all twenty-eight witnesses.
  The exact point-line reflection
  $(Z,V)\mapsto(Z^{-1},P^6V)$, followed by its target period
  to $(Q/Z,V)$, carries the other marked branch
  (\cref{night:P3K.108}, step 9).
\end{enumerate}
