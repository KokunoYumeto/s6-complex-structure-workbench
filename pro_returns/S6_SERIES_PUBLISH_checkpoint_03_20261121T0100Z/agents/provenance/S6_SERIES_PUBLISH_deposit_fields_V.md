## Paper V

### Title

The $(3,4,\infty)$ family of complex six-spheres. V. Complex fourfolds from the family

### Abstract

We construct compact complex fourfolds diffeomorphic, with their complex orientation, to $3\mathbb{HP}^2\#(-\mathbb{HP}^2)$. They form a family of complex three-tori over $\mathbb P^1$ with monodromy in $GL_6(\mathbb Z)$: two multiple fibres, of multiplicities five and four, and one Kuhn cusp. Its periods come from the exterior square of the genus-two pencil $y^2=x^5+\lambda^3x+\lambda^4$. The family is parametrized exactly by the half-plane $\operatorname{Im}\xi>c_*$, where $c_*\approx1.09454$ is minus the minimum of an explicit real-analytic period function. We compute the Hodge numbers, the tangent cohomology (the Kuranishi germ is smooth of dimension one) and the automorphism group $\mathbb C\times\mathbb Z/2$, and we show that the family extends analytically across no boundary point of its parameter domain. We also construct a compact complex fourfold outside Fujiki's class $\mathcal C$ as a small crepant resolution of a Weierstrass model, bimeromorphic to the fibre product of a complex six-sphere with an elliptic surface; give an effective classification of the integral monodromy data of this kind that yield three-connected eight-manifolds, all of which are diffeomorphic to $3\mathbb{HP}^2\#(-\mathbb{HP}^2)$; and describe shifted fibre products of complex dimension five.
