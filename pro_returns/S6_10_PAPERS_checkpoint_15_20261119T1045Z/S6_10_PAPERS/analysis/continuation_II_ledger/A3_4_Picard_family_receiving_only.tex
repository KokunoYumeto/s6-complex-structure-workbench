Fix the root cover above each smooth multiple fibre and write its smooth central surface as $S'_c$. The exponential sequence identifies $\Pic(Y)$ with the complex vector space $H^1(Y,\OO_Y)$. Exponentiating linear combinations of a basis of holomorphic Cech cocycles gives the actual Picard family. On $S'_c$ consider the holomorphic family of bundles
\[
 E_B=(\Omega^1_{U'_c}\otimes p_c^*B)|_{S'_c}.
\]
