1. Introduction
01DX
In this document we work out some topics on cohomology of sheaves on topological
spaces. We mostly work in the generality of modules over a sheaf of rings and we
work with morphisms of ringed spaces. To see what happens for sheaves on sites
take a look at the chapter Cohomology on Sites, Section 1. Basic references are
[God73] and [Ive86].
