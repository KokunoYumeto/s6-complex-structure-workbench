\noindent\textbf{Step 5.} There are finitely many non-submersion values. For each of these fixed full fibres the coherent ambient bundle varies in the holomorphic Picard family constructed in \ref{cdp:st:A.4.9}, and proper semicontinuity makes its nonvanishing locus analytic. Each locus is proper because it excludes every $L_r$ with $r$ real irrational. Their finite union is proper, proving the simultaneous generic assertion. This proves the smooth-multiple conclusion of Remark~7.2. For a singular support the identical root cover retains its singularities and the kernel $N^*\otimes\mathcal J^{-1}$; the present proof does not erase that term in Remark~7.4.
