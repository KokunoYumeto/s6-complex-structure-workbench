2. Suppose an analytic divisor Z in K∗, proper over the disc, restricted over the punctured
disc to the union of the Cs. Its horizontal irreducible part does not contain E, since the
generic intersection is the one transverse point (22.35). Both are Cartier locally in the smooth
threefold, so their intersection is an analytic curve, proper over the disc. Its reduced horizontal
part has generic degree one. An irreducible horizontal analytic curve cannot have a positive-
dimensional fibre without being contained in that fibre. Hence its normalization is finite
of degree one over the disc and is isomorphic to the disc. It extends p(s), a contradiction.
Vertical components of the intersection have no effect on this horizontal component.
3. If an additional finite proper modification admitted a proper analytic closure, its proper image
would give such a Z, by the proper mapping theorem; the image has dimension two and
the same generic exceptional curves. The horizontal exceptional locus of a relative proper
holomorphic blowdown likewise gives Z. For a meromorphic contraction with proper analytic
graph, resolve the graph and apply the same argument to its horizontal exceptional locus and
its proper image in K∗. This is a direct marked-intersection proof of a familiar nonproperness
phenomenon for exceptional curves in GSS surface deformations; see Dloussky and Teleman
(2012, arXiv version 2, Section 1.2, PDF pp. 3–5) for the primary context.
