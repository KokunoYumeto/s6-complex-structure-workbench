Proposition 8.3 (The double cover of the linearization parameter). [tag 01QD]
Fix the sphere logarithmic data in (Engel, 2026, Remark 8.1, p. 22). The resulting family has
a proper holomorphic realization on every simply connected admissible u-open set and globally
over the punctured w-disc of proposition 7.5, with u = w2. The order-two class of d in ΛA2/N2Λ
obstructs descent of this family to the punctured u-disc.
Proof. Local inverse logarithms of u give the proper local families of proposition 7.5; its coherent
even shifts give their global family over the punctured w-disc. Descent through u = w2 would
furnish an isomorphism between the two pulled-back fibres at w and −w, namely Xc and Xc+1.
Proposition A.2 excludes that isomorphism by the nonzero class of d in ΛA2/N2Λ. This class
has exact order two on the normalized finite cover. The same obstruction applies to all sphere
logarithmic data by proposition 8.1, because r2 is odd.
