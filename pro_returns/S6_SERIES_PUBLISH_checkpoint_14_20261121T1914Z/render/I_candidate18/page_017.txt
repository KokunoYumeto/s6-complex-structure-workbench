How the paper is organized
The marked period and quotient coordinates lead to the exact norm, its admissible domain,
and the proper family. The intrinsic classification is followed by discrete logarithmic data,
the monodromy quotient and the exceptional fibres. Sections 12–15 compute the cotangent,
Bott–Chern and tangent cohomology; the complete coefficient construction precedes its use in
the Picard family. The integral circle quotient and the real structures then lead to the puncture
and outer-boundary problems. Their local models, component obstructions and topology are
developed with the specified marked comparison maps.
The remaining main-body sections treat other multiplicities and finite covers, rational CM
heights, the critical level curves, and arithmetic periods. Appendices A–C contain the credited
proofs of parameter equivalence, tangent cohomology, local deformations and automorphisms.
The final part states the unresolved arithmetic questions. Each arrow in the diagram identifies a
particular supplying and receiving body argument in the table below it.
