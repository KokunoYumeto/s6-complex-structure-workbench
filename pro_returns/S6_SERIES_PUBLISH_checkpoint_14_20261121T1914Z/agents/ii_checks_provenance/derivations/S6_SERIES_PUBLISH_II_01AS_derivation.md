# Derivation before target proof: 01AS

Owner `cdp:st:A.4.3`; checker `i_provenance`. Exact target/supplement read;
target proof and previous checking programs remain unopened. Related Paper I
normalization and conductor exposure is disclosed.

Let p:T=P_E(O_E direct-sum P)->E be the stated ruled surface. The projective
bundle Picard decomposition sends a line N to its fibre degree and its base
line: N=O_T(k) tensor p^*M. Since eta^*A is topologically trivial, k=0 and
M has degree zero. Thus eta^*A=p^*M with M in Pic^0(E). This is the actual
pullback map from the base, not an identification of arbitrary line bundles.

The relative cotangent sequence is
0 -> p^*Omega_E^1 -> Omega_T^1 -> Omega_(T/E)^1 ->0.
Its last term, tensor p^*M, restricts to O_P1(-2) on every fibre, hence has
zero direct image and zero global sections. Since Omega_E^1=O_E, projection
formula identifies H0(T,Omega_T^1 tensor p^*M) with H0(E,M). A degree-zero
line on the compact elliptic curve has a nonzero section precisely when it
is trivial: its effective zero divisor would have degree zero. The space is
then one-dimensional, generated by p^*dz.

At a double crossing xy=0, the map from Omega_S^1 to the sum of differential
modules on the two branches has torsion as its kernel. Its image consists
of pairs whose tangential restrictions on the double curve agree. This
follows directly by writing a function on the crossing as a pair of branch
functions with equal restriction, differentiating, and observing that the
normal branch differentials dx,dy remain independently available. Tensoring
by the invertible A gives the same agreement with its gluing isomorphism.
These charts globalize to the injection of the torsion-free cotangent into
eta_*Omega_T^1 and its conductor compatibility condition.

For nontrivial M the normalization has no section, so neither does S. For
M=O_E, choose a trivialization of eta^*A. Its descent isomorphism along the
two sections is multiplication by a constant lambda in C*: invertible
holomorphic functions on the compact connected elliptic curve are constant.
The gluing translation tau of E satisfies tau^*dz=dz. Hence a nonzero
multiple of p^*dz descends exactly when lambda=1 (changing the convention
replaces lambda by its inverse and gives the same condition). With
lambda=1 the same constant trivialization itself descends, proving A=O_S.
Conversely A=O_S has lambda=1 and p^*dz descends. This proves exactly the
two dimensions and the actual descent maps in the target.
