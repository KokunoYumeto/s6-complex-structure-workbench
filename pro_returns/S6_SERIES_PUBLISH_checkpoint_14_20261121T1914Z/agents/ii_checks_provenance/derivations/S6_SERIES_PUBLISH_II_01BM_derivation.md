For the stated Weierstrass surface E, the invariant differential calculation
gives omega_(E/P1)=h^*O(1), and hence K_E=h^*O(-1). The connected genus-one
fibres give h_*O_E=O_P1 and R^1h_*O_E=O(-1) by relative Serre duality (the
family is flat and Gorenstein, including its nodal fibres). Thus H^1(E,O_E)=0
by Leray. Also H^0(E,2K_E)=H^0(P1,O(-2))=0. Castelnuovo's rationality
criterion gives that E is rational. For a precise opened published source,
Brendan Hassett, Castelnuovo's Criterion, Theorem 2.26, printed p.178 of the
Clay Mathematics Proceedings 8 volume gives the smooth projective minimal
version; taking a smooth minimal model preserves q and P2, so it applies to
E. The birational morphisms are point blowups/down on smooth surfaces.
Primary URL: https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/cmip08c.pdf .

Point blowups preserve H_1 and add a free Z summand to H^2. A smooth rational
surface therefore has H^1(E,Z)=0 and torsion-free H^2(E,Z). The smooth elliptic
fibres have Euler number zero. Each of the twelve nodal cubics has Euler
number one, from its normalization P1 with two points identified. Additivity
over the finite critical-value stratification gives e(E)=12. Poincare duality
and H^1=H^3=0 then give rank H^2(E,Z)=10. Kunneth for Y=E times P1 gives
H^1(Y,Z)=0 and H^2(Y,Z)=Z^10 direct-sum Z=Z^11. It also gives
e(Y)=e(E)e(P1)=24. Projectivity implies its algebraic dimension is three.

The map g=h composed with the first projection is holomorphic and proper;
the Weierstrass fibres are all nonempty and connected, so its product fibres
are connected and it is surjective.
