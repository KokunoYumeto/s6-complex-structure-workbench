Write a=y^2-xz, b=yz^2-x^4, c=z^3-x^3y. The two exact relations

z^2 a-y b+x c=0, and x^3 a-z b+y c=0

are verified by expansion. The curve algebra R0/I is finite over C{x}:
y^5=x^6 and z^5=x^7 follow from the three binomials. Modulo x it has basis
1,y,z,yz,z^2, so Nakayama gives these five generators over C{x}. Under
(x,y,z)=(t^5,t^6,t^7) their powers of t have distinct residues 0,1,2,3,4
modulo five. Any C{x}-linear relation therefore has every coefficient zero.
It follows that the quotient is exactly C{t^5,t^6,t^7}, a domain, with
normalization C{t}. The fresh polynomial elimination independently verifies
that the kernel of the parametrization is the three-generator ideal I.

Its height is two. The three generators are minimal at the origin: a has
initial degree two, and the independent degree-three initial monomials yz^2
and z^3 of b,c do not belong to the degree-three span of x a,y a,z a. Hence
dim_C I/(x,y,z)I=3, exceeding the height two, and D is not a local complete
intersection at the origin. Off the origin its normalization parameter has
nonzero derivative and is bijective, so the curve is smooth there.

The exact derivative identities are

F_y=-15x a^2,
F_x=-28x^2 b+14xyz a-3y a^2,
F_z=20z c+10x^2y a.

They are checked by the fresh program. At any critical point with x nonzero,
the first identity forces a=0 and the second then forces b=0; the relation
x c=y b-z^2 a forces c=0. At x=0, the original partial derivatives reduce to
F_x=-3y^5 and F_z=20z^4, forcing y=z=0. Conversely every partial derivative
vanishes on I. The weighted Euler identity
35F=5xF_x+6yF_y+7zF_z shows the critical set lies on F=0, and direct
substitution on D gives zero. Thus its reduced critical locus is exactly D.

F is reduced. A repeated analytic irreducible factor would divide every
partial derivative and produce a two-dimensional component of their common
zero set in the smooth three-dimensional ambient germ. The set just computed
has dimension one, excluding such a repeated factor.

Weights (5,6,7) give F weight 35 and carry every nonzero parameter t on D to
(1,1,1) by the corresponding invertible weighted scaling of local germs.
The slice x=1 is transverse to D there. Put y=1+A,z=1+B. Direct expansion
has quadratic part 25 B^2 and cubic part

-20 A^3+30 A^2B-15 AB^2+40 B^3.

The quadratic form has rank one. The holomorphic splitting lemma removes its
nondegenerate B-direction; the remaining one-variable germ has leading term
-20 A^3, since the needed change in B begins at order two. A holomorphic
cube root of its nonzero unit factor changes that germ to a nonzero multiple
of A^3. Thus the exact transverse analytic type is the ordinary A2 cusp at
every nonzero point of D. The program verifies the complete quadratic and
cubic expansions, including the nonzero cubic on the Hessian radical.

At the generic point of the smooth curve D, its ideal is generated by two
regular transverse coordinates. F and its derivatives vanish on D, so its
constant and linear transverse coefficients are zero. Hence F belongs to
I^2 R0_I, and therefore to the symbolic square I^(2)=I^2 R0_I intersect R0.
This assertion uses the exact localization morphism at the prime I.

Ordinary-square nonmembership follows from the positive weights, not from a
numerical approximation. The weights of a,b,c are 12,20,21. In weighted
degree 35, the only possible coefficient of a quadratic generator product
is weight 11 multiplying a^2. Its only monomial is xy. The products ab and
ac would require coefficients of weights 3 and 2; all other products have
weight greater than 35. Such coefficients do not exist. Thus the weight-35
part of I^2 is spanned by xy a^2. But its x^7 coefficient is zero whereas
the x^7 coefficient of F is four. Positive-weight decomposition applies to
convergent power series as well, so F is not in the analytic ideal I^2.
The fresh program enumerates every allowed nonnegative coefficient monomial
at these six finite target weights and confirms exactly this one-dimensional
possibility.

The failure of ordinary-square membership therefore occurs for a reduced
critical curve with generic A2 transversal; the additional complete-
intersection hypothesis in the supplied A.5.5 statement has a precise role.

realization assertion. Printed-proof comparison and event classification
