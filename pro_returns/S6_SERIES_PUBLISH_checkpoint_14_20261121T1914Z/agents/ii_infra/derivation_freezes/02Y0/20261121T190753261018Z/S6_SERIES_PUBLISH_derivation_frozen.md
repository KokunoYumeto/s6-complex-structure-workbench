## Smooth irreducible supports

Let the full fibre be mS with S smooth. In local coordinates t=z^m u, take the cyclic base change t=s^m and normalize. Taking a local m-th root of the unit shows that its projection to X is unramified and its map to the s-disc is a submersion near the central fibre. Its central fibre S' is a finite unramified cover of S, and the nearby fibres are the original complex two-tori. Proper smooth Ehresmann transport therefore makes S' diffeomorphic to T4, so b1(S')=4. P4.12 gives omega_S^m=O; pulling back gives torsion canonical bundle on S'. Such a surface is minimal, because adjunction on a hypothetical exceptional curve would give K·E=−1, contradicting torsion. Its Kodaira dimension is0, and BHPV Table10 with b1=4 identifies it as a complex torus. Thus S has the asserted finite unramified torus cover. Euler characteristic multiplies by the covering degree, so e(S)=0. This also follows from P4.6 since the support is smooth.

## Singular irreducible supports

Its normalization S is a single smooth surface, since the support is normal crossing. The conductor D is nonempty. The exact normal line map from P4.6 gives K_S≡−D, c2(S)=K_S², e(S)=6 chi(O_S), and the nodal normalization computation gives T=2 chi(O_S). These are equalities of rational Chern numbers and Euler characteristics, with all local branches retained. P4.12 supplies Kodaira dimension−infinity for the minimal resolved normalization; here the normalization is already smooth.

BHPV Table10 gives rational, ruled of genus g≥1, or classVII minimal models. For a rational surface chi(O_S)=1, invariant under blowup, so T=e(F)=2. For a ruled surface of base genus g≥1 and k blowups, e(S)=4−4g+k and K²=8−8g−k. Their equality gives k=2−2g. Nonnegativity of k gives g=1 and k=0. Hence S is smooth minimal elliptic ruled and T=0. For a classVII surface, b1=1 and chi(O_S)=0, so e(S)=b2(S) and K²=−b2(S). Equality forces b2(S)=0; in particular no exceptional curve is present and S is minimal. The BBK theorem supplies a finite étale cover S_hat→S that is Hopf or Inoue. The nonempty effective conductor pulls back to a nonempty effective curve on S_hat, because a finite surjective étale map preserves the local codimension and has nonempty inverse image. Inoue's opened no-curves theorem excludes the latter alternative by this precise pullback. A Hopf cover has universal cover C² minus0; composing the universal covering with S_hat→S gives the universal cover of S as well. Thus S is a Hopf surface in the defining universal-cover sense. Its Euler characteristic and T are0. This exhausts all singular irreducible cases and proves the exact Euler values.

## Reducible supports

Let S_i be any normalized component. Since the whole support is connected and has at least two global components, S_i meets another global component F_j. Pulling back the canonical section of O_X(F_j) to S_i gives a nonzero effective curve divisor. Its real Chern class is0, since H2(X;R)=0. If S_i carried a Kähler form, integrating that form over this nonempty effective divisor would be positive and also the zero Chern pairing. Hence S_i is non-Kähler, by this explicit pullback and integration argument.

Take its minimal model S_i0 and let k_i count the blowups. OVV Theorem5.1 makes S_i0 isotrivial elliptic or classVII. In the elliptic alternative Theorem4.2 and its local multiple-fibre description give elliptic reduced fibres everywhere, so its Euler characteristic is0; its canonical self-intersection is0, as in the elliptic/Kodaira rows of BHPV Table10 or directly from the vertical canonical formula. Blowing up changes e by+k_i and K² by−k_i. The equality e(S_i)=K_Si² from the conductor normal sequence forces k_i=0. Thus this component is smooth minimal non-Kähler elliptic. In the classVII alternative the same equality gives b2(S_i)=0, including the blowup contributions. Its conductor is nonempty, so the same finite étale pullback argument above identifies it as a smooth minimal Hopf surface.

Consequently every normalization component has chi(O)=0. The identity T=2 sum chi(O_Si) gives T=0 and e(F)=0. There are no triple points. The conductor curves are therefore smooth and disjoint on each S_i. Adjunction with K_Si≡−D_i gives, for each conductor component C, 2g(C)−2=(K_Si+C)·C=0, since all other conductor curves are disjoint. Hence all are elliptic. The normalized branch-pair map is locally the identity from either branch intersection to the double locus; it is globally a finite unramified cover of smooth curves (a connected ordered-branch component may give degree2). This proves exactly the stated finite unramified branch maps, including branch exchange for self-intersections.

## Equality for the total space

P4.7 identifies c3[X] with the finite sum of all T_b, each nonnegative. Thus c3=0 holds exactly when every T_b=0. By the cases above these are precisely the stated smooth torus-covered, singular minimal elliptic-ruled/Hopf, and reducible minimal Hopf/non-Kähler-elliptic forms. Conversely each of those forms has e(F)=0: its normalizations and elliptic conductor have Euler0 and no triple contribution. The maps and classification in both directions refer to the original reduced supports, without changing fibre multiplicities.
