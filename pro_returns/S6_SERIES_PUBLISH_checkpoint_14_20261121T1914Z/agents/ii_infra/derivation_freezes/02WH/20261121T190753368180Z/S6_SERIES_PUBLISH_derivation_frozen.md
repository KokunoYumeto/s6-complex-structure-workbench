# Independent derivation: night:P4.10 (tag 02WH)

Target proof unopened. The exact statement asks for one smooth projective example; it cites no construction. I construct a projective small resolution of an explicit elliptic self-product and verify every required property.

Start with the Weierstrass elliptic surface S→P1 having affine equation y²=x³+x+t^6 and its zero section O. Its fundamental line bundle is O(1). The discriminant is−16(4+27t^12), with twelve distinct nonzero roots. At each root c4=−48 is nonzero, so the fibre is an irreducible nodal cubic; the nonzero derivative in t makes the total surface smooth there. At infinity put s=1/t, X=s²x, Y=s³y; the equation is Y²=X³+s⁴X+1, smooth at s=0. The projective Weierstrass compactification is therefore a smooth projective elliptic surface, with exactly twelve nodal fibres and no other singular fibres.

For later use b1(S)=0 can be checked without its rational classification. The local canonical form dt∧dx/(2y) extends over the nodal point because the surface equation has nonzero t-derivative, and over the zero section by the invariant elliptic residue. At infinity it becomes −s^-1 ds∧dX/(2Y). These transitions give K_S=π*O(-1). Every fibre has h0(O)=h1(O)=1, including the irreducible nodal cubic by its normalization sequence; proper coherent base change gives a line bundle R1π_*O=O(b). Absolute Serre duality on S, with every twist π*O(m), and top Leray give h1(O(b+m))=h0(O(-m-1)). For sufficiently negative m this forces b=-1. Hence h1(S,O)=0, and projective Hodge theory gives b1(S)=0 and H0(S,Ω1)=0.

Let W=S×P1 S. It is projective. Away from pairs of nodes in the same fibre it is smooth, and near each of the twelve such pairs it has equation xy=uv, where the base parameter is t_local=xy=uv. Blow up the diagonal Δ_S⊂W. The diagonal is Cartier away from these nodes, so the blowup is an isomorphism there. Near a node its ideal is(x−u,y−v). Put α=x−u, β=y−v. The equation becomes αy+βu=0. In the blowup chart β=kα, the strict transform is y=−ku and v=−kx, with free coordinates(x,u,k), and base parameter−kxu. In the other chart α=lβ, it is u=−ly and x=−lv, with coordinates(y,v,l), and base parameter−lyv. These charts are smooth and glue by l=1/k. The exceptional set over the node is P1, not a surface. Thus the blowup Z is a smooth projective threefold and resolves precisely the twelve nodes.

Each regular fibre is E_t×E_t, an abelian surface. The fibre over a nodal value is the proper transform of the irreducible product C×C of the nodal cubic with itself. It remains irreducible because the resolution is an isomorphism on a dense open subset and its exceptional curve lies in the closure, as the two chart equations show. Those equations give a reduced normal-crossing divisor, with two ordinary triple points corresponding to k=0 and l=0 and only ordinary double crossings elsewhere. Thus every reduced special fibre has exactly the required irreducible normal-crossings form. The fibres are connected.

The nodal cubic has Euler characteristic1, so C×C has Euler1. Replacing its single singular-product point by P1 adds e(P1)−e(point)=1. Hence each resolved special fibre has Euler2. The regular fibres have Euler0. Additivity over the base stratification yields e(Z)=12·2=24, and the complex tangent Euler-class identity gives c3[Z]=24.

Finally let θ be a holomorphic one-form on Z. The two inclusions S→W, p↦(p,O(π(p))) and p↦(O(π(p)),p), avoid the twelve nodes and lift to Z. Their pullbacks of θ are zero because H0(S,Ω1)=0. On each regular fibre E_t² a holomorphic one-form is invariant and is determined by its restrictions to the two elliptic axes, so θ restricts to zero on every regular fibre. It therefore pulls back from a base one-form on the smooth locus. Pullback along the global section (O,O) shows that this base form extends holomorphically to P1, where it is zero. Thus θ=0 on a dense open set and hence everywhere. Projective Hodge theory gives b1(Z)=0. An ample divisor has positive cubic self-intersection, so its real degree-two cohomology class is nonzero and b2(Z)>0. This exhibits the exact failure of the c3∈{0,2} conclusion after removing b2=0, while retaining b1=0 and both stated fibre classes.
