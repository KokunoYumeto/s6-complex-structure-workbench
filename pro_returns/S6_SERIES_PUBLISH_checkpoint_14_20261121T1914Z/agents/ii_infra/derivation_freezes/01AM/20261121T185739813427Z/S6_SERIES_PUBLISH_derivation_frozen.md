Fix any m>=3, choose an abelian surface T and a point a of exact order m,
and choose z on P1 with primitive m-th root zeta. The cyclic group acts on
T times P1 by (p,z) |-> (p+a,zeta z). Every nonidentity power translates T
by a nonzero point, so the action is free even at zero and infinity. The
quotient Y is a smooth compact projective threefold, and the finite quotient
map pi:T times P1 ->Y is etale. The invariant function t=z^m defines a map
g:Y->P1. Its general fibre is isomorphic to T: the m choices of z are
identified by the cyclic action, with translations giving the exact
identifications. Thus the general fibres are connected abelian surfaces.

The reduced support S over zero is T/<a>, and the full fibre is mS because
the invariant local base parameter is z^m. The conormal N* is the descent
of the trivial line generated by z with deck character zeta. Its order is
exactly m. Indeed its m-th power is trivial. If a smaller positive power
were trivial on S, pullback along the connected finite etale cover T->S
would give a nonzero constant global function on compact T transforming by
that nontrivial character, which is impossible. This proves exact order via
the actual character descent map.

Define a global line B on Y by descent of the trivial line on T times P1
whose pullback descent datum multiplies its chosen frame by zeta. All the
cocycle relations hold because zeta^m=1. Use the convention that an invariant
section satisfies this descent datum applied to its deck pullback. The two
translation-invariant differentials on T have character one, while dz has
pullback character zeta. Tensoring by B therefore gives character exponents
1,1,2 on the three constant differential frames over T times {0}.
For m>=3 none is trivial, so the reduced support has no global sections.

Over the full fibre, pi^*(mS) is T times Spec C[z]/(z^m). Because pi is etale,
pi^*Omega_Y^1 is the cotangent bundle of T times P1. Global sections of its
restriction, tensor the pulled-back line B, have basis
z^j alpha_1, z^j alpha_2, z^j dz for 0<=j<m, with the line frame understood.
Their character exponents are j+1,j+1,j+2. Exactly one exponent in each of
the three rows is zero modulo m: j=m-1,m-1,m-2 respectively. Taking finite
group invariants (an exact operation in characteristic zero) therefore gives
h^0(mS,(Omega_Y^1 tensor B)|mS)=3, while the restriction to S gives zero.
This computes the actual sections and the restriction map: all three
invariant monomials have positive z-order and therefore restrict to zero.
