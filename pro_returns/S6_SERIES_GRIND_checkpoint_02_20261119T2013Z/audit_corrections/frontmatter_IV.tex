\subsection{The main theorems}
\label{series:IV:main-theorems}
We identify $\Theta_7$ with $\mathbb Z/28$ by
$[\Sigma]\mapsto28\mu(\Sigma)$, using outward boundary orientation
and the positive generator $\mu=1/28$. Every return uses the indicated
positive parameter loop and its actual endpoint identification.
The physical line is $M$; the meridian-one line is its dual $U=M^{-1}$.
For a completed physical quotient write
\[
 k_M=\langle c_1(M)^3,[N]\rangle,\qquad
 \ell_M=\langle p_1(TN)c_1(M),[N]\rangle.
\]
For $P=(v\ w)\in\mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb Z)$, the periodic fan $T(P)$
has triangles $\{z,z+v,z+w\}$ and $\{z+v,z+w,z+v+w\}$ for
$z\in\mathbb Z^2$. Put
\[
 T_j=T\!\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\j&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 (x,y)^t=P^{-1}e_2,\qquad S(P)=x^2+xy+y^2.
\]
The fan always includes the ordered period lattice, its full unit
coefficients and the marked collar maps. A retained cap includes its
signed primitive norm, finite line character, integer annular cochain
and complete prescribed reference map.

For Theorems D--G the E6 source and point line are
\[
 E_r:\ y^2=x^3+rx^2+tx+t^2,\qquad G=(0,t),\qquad
 M_a=\mathcal O([aG]-[O]).
\]
The notation $[aG]$ denotes the multiplied section. Every comparison
uses the actual point-line and tensor-line morphisms in its body proof.

All admitted finite characters and complete annular references remain
part of the theorem data.
