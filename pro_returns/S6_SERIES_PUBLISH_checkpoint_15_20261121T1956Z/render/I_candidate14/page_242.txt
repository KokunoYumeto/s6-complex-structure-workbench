3. Ramification away from the axes. Over the ordinary locus the torus family is a smooth
holomorphic group family. Near a branch point, choose small neighbourhoods of the finitely
many preimages in V and local Lie lifts of their maps to the torus. The trace of these
holomorphic functions under the finite map is holomorphic, and its exponential is the ordinary
sum. Changes of lifts add periods, which disappear in the group quotient. Thus the ordinary
trace extends across the ordinary branch locus. Equivalently this is the usual addition map
on the relative symmetric power, applied to the finite multisection.
