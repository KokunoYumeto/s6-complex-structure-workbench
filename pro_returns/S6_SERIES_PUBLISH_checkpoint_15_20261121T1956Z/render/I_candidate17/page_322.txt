By (Douady, 1974, §VII.8, principal theorem, pp. 598–599; §VIII.1, Theorem 1 and its
corollary, p. 600), there is a minimal versal analytic germ with tangent space H1(TX). The
identification of this tangent space uses tangent-valued Čech cocycles of transition maps, with
changes of trivialization acting by coboundaries. Its local algebra is C{t}/I. The parameter
family induces t 7→g(c) with g′(c0) ̸= 0. By the analytic inverse function theorem, substitution
by g is injective; it annihilates I, so I = 0. The classifying map is an isomorphism of germs.
Pullback of the versal family realizes every sufficiently small deformation and proves local
miniversality.
