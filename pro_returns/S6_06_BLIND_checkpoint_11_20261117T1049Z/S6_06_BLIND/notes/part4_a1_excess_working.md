# The excess-one curve calculation and the uniformly A1 ruled case

Current-run proof for independent audit, 2026-11-17.

## 1. Exact analytic classification at excess one

Let A be the local ring of a reduced complex curve germ with r branches,
and let B be its normalization, the direct sum of r convergent power-series
rings C{t_i}. Let R be the subring of B in which all constant terms agree.
Then A is a subring of R, and

    length(R/A)=delta(A)-(r-1).

Suppose this length is one. The finite A-module R/A has length one, so
m_A R is contained in A. Choose v in m_R whose class spans R/A, and write
v^2=a+c v with a in m_A and c in C. If c is nonzero, the convergent
square-root series solves b^2-cb-a=0 for b in m_A. Then
(v-b)(v+b-c)=0 in R, whose second factor is a unit. Thus v=b, a
contradiction. Hence c=0. Since m_R=m_A+C v, we obtain m_R^2 contained
in A.

It follows that A is exactly the inverse image of a hyperplane

    sum a_i f_i'(0)=0

in m_R/m_R^2=C^r. Rescaling each parameter with nonzero a_i puts all its
nonzero coefficients in a prescribed standard form. This is an actual
analytic ring isomorphism, not only a tangent-cone identification.

For r<=3 the possibilities are as follows.

* One nonzero coefficient: the corresponding branch has ring
  C{t^2,t^3}; all other branches are independent smooth transverse
  branches, joined only at their constant terms. The embedding dimension
  is r+1. Thus r=1 is a plane cusp, r=2 is a cusp plus a transverse line,
  and r=3 cannot embed in a smooth threefold.
* Two nonzero coefficients: the corresponding pair has equal first
  derivatives. Its ring is generated by (t,t) and (t^2,-t^2), hence is
  exactly the plane tacnode y^2=x^4. The other r-2 branches are transverse
  independent lines. Thus r=2 is a plane tacnode and r=3 is a tacnode plus
  a transverse line.
* Three nonzero coefficients (r=3): the two-dimensional first-jet space
  is the restriction of linear forms to three distinct lines in a plane.
  Products of these first jets span all three second-jet coordinates, so
  the resulting ring is exactly the ordinary plane triple point. One can
  also verify equality by the plane triple point's delta invariant three:
  its normalization subring has the same constants and first-derivative
  relation and hence the same colength three in B.

These exhaust the possibilities because the hyperplane has at least one
nonzero coefficient. Every abstract analytic isomorphism just obtained is
induced by an ambient analytic coordinate change for embeddings in C^3:
choose minimal algebra generators, lift the images of their linear parts,
and apply the inverse-function theorem; if the embedding dimension is two,
first straighten the smooth containing surface.

All the realizable excess-one germs are therefore among the two exact
local classes excluded by P4.38 for uniformly ordinary-double transverse
type.

## 2. Global application to the remaining ruled case

Suppose an actual irreducible support with positive-genus ruled resolution
has generic transverse type A1 on every critical-curve component. The
horizontal pole section has coefficient two and therefore cannot map
nonconstantly to the conductor: an ordinary double point has conductor
exponent one on both normalization branches. The section is contracted.
P4.33 consequently gives chi(O_Csch)=-1.

The conductor image is Cohen--Macaulay and generically reduced in type A1;
it is therefore reduced. Thus Csch=C and chi(O_C)=-1. All its normalized
components are rational. The already proved fundamental-group and Euler
calculations leave, in the requested global scope where the total Euler
sum is at most two, exactly two normalized rational components and one
point-identification cycle. The rational-tree case gives Euler number at
least three by P4R.4, and the one-component cycle is excluded by P4.35.

For a connected reduced curve with two rational normalized components,
the normalization sequence gives

    chi(O_C)=2-sum delta_p=-1,

so sum delta_p=3. Its one graph cycle gives

    sum (r_p-1)=2.

Hence the nonnegative local excesses delta_p-(r_p-1) sum to one. There is
exactly one point of excess one, and every point has at most three local
branches. Section 1 classifies that excess-one point by an exact analytic
ring isomorphism. P4.38 excludes every resulting germ. This contradiction
rules out the uniformly A1 positive-genus ruled-resolution sector.

The argument does not replace generic A1 by generic A2 merely because the
image conductor is reduced in both cases. Its local deformation step uses
the actual ordinary-double boundary condition.
