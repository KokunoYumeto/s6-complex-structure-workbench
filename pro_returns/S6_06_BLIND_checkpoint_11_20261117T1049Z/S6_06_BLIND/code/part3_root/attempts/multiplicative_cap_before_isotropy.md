## Actual exterior and the ordinary comparison cap

Let the retained source fibre be I_N, with the marked G component c prime to N. Write P for its source complement and W=T_(t_G)P; reversal of the suspension gives the actual negative-translation mapping torus. The component sublattice is primitive: it is a direct primitive sublattice of the full trivial lattice, whose quotient in NS is the torsion-free Mordell--Weil group. The complement pair sequence gives H1(P)=0. The elliptic fibration with section over a disc presents its fundamental group as a quotient of the abelian elliptic lattice, so pi1(P)=0.

The ordinary cap U is the mapping torus of the I_N neighbourhood under t_G. On the central fibre, translation cyclically permutes all N components and preserves the oriented graph circle. The integral Wang sequence gives H1(U)=Z^2, H2(U)=Z^2 and H3(U)=Z. The component-orbit generator C and a lift T of graph-times-suspension give its H2 basis. The source fibre maps to N C. The invariant sum of the components maps to the actual source fibre in the full-source Wang sequence, so H3(U) maps primitively onto H3(T_(t_G)S)=ZF.

Use actual boundary generators a (vanishing circle), b (oriented graph period), alpha (positive base meridian), gamma (suspension). Choose the outward gamma sign so the boundary presentation has

    [alpha,b]=a^N, [alpha,gamma]=a^(-c),
    [a,b]=[a,gamma]=[b,gamma]=1.

The boundary is the oriented a-circle bundle over T^3_(b,alpha,gamma) with Euler form d alpha wedge (N d b-c d gamma). Its integral homology is torsion-free since gcd(N,c)=1. In degree two use

    z=F/N, R=a wedge alpha, B=b wedge gamma,
    L=(c b+N gamma) wedge alpha.

Here z is an actual integral primitive class: the mapping-torus exterior-square relation is c(a wedge b)+N(a wedge gamma)=0, so F=a wedge b=N z and a wedge gamma=-c z. With orientation (a,b,gamma,alpha), the only nonzero pairings among the four displayed basis classes are z.L=1 and R.B=1. The ordinary cap maps z to C, R and L to zero, and B to T after choosing the lift T. On H1 it kills alpha and maps b,gamma to its two primitive generators.

P3K.22 gives H2(T_(t_G)S)=Z[O]. The boundary map of [O] is the primitive meridian alpha, by its single transverse intersection with the removed fibre. The preceding primitive H3-cap map and this primitive H2-source boundary map show, directly in the ordinary-cap Mayer--Vietoris sequence, that

    H2(boundary) -> H2(W) direct_sum H2(U)

is an integral isomorphism. Consequently H2(W)=Z^2, and the exterior images of R,L form an integral basis.

## Relative line frame: removing the unresolved rim coefficient

The source line M=O(dG-O) has degree zero on every I_N component and has an actual Tate-unit representative P(q) of its Picard point. On the Tate cover its line transition is

    (Z,V) ~ (q_T Z,P(q)V).

Choose an analytic logarithm of the nonzero unit P(q). A smooth boundary line frame is obtained from

    V exp[-log(P(q)) log|Z|/log|q_T|].

It is unchanged under the stated transition. A translation lift (Z,V)->(G(q)Z,beta(q)V) acts in this frame by

    beta(q) exp[-log(P(q)) log|G(q)|/log|q_T|].

This function depends only on the base. Its logarithmic exponential has zero winding, and beta is the actual nonvanishing holomorphic unit on the retained I_N neighbourhood. Thus its winding on the graph and vanishing circles is zero.

Over the complementary base disc, divide the specified degree-one Hom section by a base equation for its single zero at the selected cusp. This gives an invertible lift on P. In the boundary frame its only winding is the single base-meridian winding of that equation; its two elliptic windings are zero. The relative first-Chern-class connecting map, followed by Poincare--Lefschetz duality, therefore gives an actual preimage K of [M] with

    (t_G)_* K-K=+/-F

and no rim term. The sign is reversed by replacing the suspension generator, and the assertion needed here is F=0 in H2(W). Since H2(W) is torsion-free and F=N z, the exterior image of z is zero.

The kernel of an oriented-boundary inclusion in a compact oriented five-manifold is isotropic for the boundary intersection form. The preceding exterior basis therefore gives

    i(z)=0, i(R)=r, i(L)=l, i(B)=e r

for an actual retained integer e. Indeed any l coefficient of i(B) would make the kernel generated by z and B-eR-fL have intersection -f, forcing f=0. This is an exact use of the boundary pairing, without extending an inversion over a marked cap.

## Marked transitive logarithmic cap

After P3K.19's actual finite-kernel cancellation, retain its primitive pair (m,n), its elliptic period j and all line phases. Its actual meridian is

    mu=m alpha+j b+n gamma.

The root has I_(Nm), and the Tate norm equation gives the affine source-component displacement

    A_comp=N j-c n.

The primitive mapping-torus generator acts transitively on these components. Its Wang sequence therefore again gives H2(cap)=Z C direct_sum Z T, with F mapping to Nm C. Choose integers p,q with m p+n q=-j. The actual H1 cap map is

    alpha -> p eta-n h, b -> eta, gamma -> q eta+m h.

The projection of H2(cap) to its graph-times-suspension summand T is the exterior square of this map. It sends B to mT and L to A_comp T. The vanishing-circle tori z,R have zero T projection. Since F=N z maps to Nm C, the C coefficient of z is m.

Write the still arbitrary C coefficients of B,L as s,t. Isotropy of the kernel of this cap-boundary map forces the R coefficient to be -A_comp: apply the boundary pairing to the zero-T combinations of z,R and of B,L, clearing denominators to make integral cycles. The full cap map is therefore

    z -> mC,
    R -> -A_comp C,
    B -> sC+mT,
    L -> tC+A_comp T.

In the full Mayer--Vietoris map, eliminate the exterior basis columns R,L. Its remaining degree-two matrix, on the exact exterior kernel basis z,B-eR, is

    [[m, s+e A_comp], [0,m]].

Its determinant is m^2, for the actual retained e,s,t,j and n. Thus every order m>1 leaves a nonzero finite degree-two cokernel, of order m^2, already in this one-site calculation. The map of this cokernel into orbit H2 is injective by Mayer--Vietoris. The standard fixed-sphere orbit criterion then excludes sphere topology for such a one-site cap. Order one removes this obstruction; its parameter return and simultaneous caps still require their own actual gluing comparison.
