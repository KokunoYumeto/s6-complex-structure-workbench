% Bounded actual-cycle calculation supplied to maps for the c4 smooth question.
For OS55/default, $P=5G,Q=4G$, zero index $2$, all primitive
Mumford caps and ordinary clutch $e=1$, the regular three-torus
spanned by $(e_1,e_2,\gamma)$ maps to a generator of
$H_3(X;\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z/4$.

Choose the regular fibre in the interior of the ordinary order-one
clutch disc. In the full marked H2 presentation of D.2.41 every
monodromy relation and every normal or fibre-kernel relation from
another cap has a bounding three-chain outside that disc.
The four clutch normal Thom chains are the products of a lattice
loop $\lambda$ and the disc. The redundant fifth normal chain
has the loop vector $v=(0,0,0,-1)$.
If $M c=d z$ is an integral bounding relation for a torsion
two-cycle, its intersection with a three-torus in this regular
fibre is therefore the wedge pairing of the corresponding clutch
loop vector with that three-torus. Any additional chain used to
identify the relation in $H_2(V)$ lies in $V$ and is also disjoint
from the chosen fibre.

The exact unimodular Smith decomposition in
\path{code/manifolds_regular_three_torus.py} gives $d=4$ and
clutch vector $(-16,0,1,0)$. Thus its pairing with
$e_1\wedge e_2\wedge\gamma$ is $1$ modulo $4$, up to the
common global orientation sign. The linking pairing is
therefore a unit multiple of $1/4$. Nondegeneracy of the
torsion linking pairing on the closed oriented six-manifold
proves that the regular three-torus has exact order four.
The complete two-cycle and bounding-chain coefficient vectors
are retained in \path{records/manifolds_c4_regular_three_torus.json}.
This identifies its homology class; it does not by itself
specify a framed embedded three-sphere or a spin surgery.
