\begin{proposition}[The smooth-multiple assertion of Remark 7.2]\label{cdp:st:A.3.4}
Under the hypotheses of CDP20 Proposition~7.3, let $mS$, with $m\ge2$, be a full fibre whose reduced support $S$ is smooth. For every real irrational transgression parameter $r$ of \ref{cdp:st:D.1.9},
\[
 H^0\bigl(mS,(\Omega_Y^1\otimes L_r)|_{mS}\bigr)=0.
 \tag{A.15a}\label{cdp:eq:A.15a}
\]
The same simultaneous vanishing on all smooth multiple fibres $(m\ge2)$ holds on the complement of a proper complex analytic subset of $\Pic(Y)$. The assertion includes the entire scheme-theoretic fibre, with every nilpotent layer.
\end{proposition}
\Uses{the hypotheses, notation and definitions stated here; \cref{cdp:st:D.1.9}; \cref{cdp:st:A.4.9}; \cref{cdp:st:A.4.7}; \cref{cdp:st:A.3.1}}

\begin{proof}
Choose a disc $\Delta$ about the fibre value and write $U=g^{-1}(\Delta)$. Form the base change $t=s^m$ and normalize it, obtaining $p:U'\to U$ and $g':U'\to\Delta'$. This map $p$ is finite étale of degree $m$. Indeed, near a point of the smooth support write $g=u z^m$ with $u$ a holomorphic unit. The normalization is given by
\[
 w^m=u,\qquad s=wz.
\]
The derivative $m w^{m-1}$ is invertible, so these equations give an unramified cover of the original smooth chart. They cover the normalized base change, since they coincide away from $z=0$ and are normal. The local constructions glue by uniqueness of normalization. The divisor $S'=(g')^{-1}(0)$ is smooth and reduced, $g'$ is a submersion near it, and $p^{-1}(mS)=mS'$ as Cartier divisors.

Pull back the unitary local system (A.22) on $U$ to $U'$; its associated holomorphic line is $A'=p^*L_r$. Every nearby smooth fibre of $g'$ maps isomorphically onto the corresponding nearby fibre of $g$. Its character is therefore the irrational exponential of a nonzero integral class. The smooth-fibre calculation in \ref{cdp:st:A.4.9} gives zero cohomology in degrees zero and one for that local system. Proper smooth local triviality at $S'$ transports the same cohomology statement to $S'$. If a component presentation of the cover is used, the argument applies on each component and the groups add.

A holomorphic section of a unitary-flat line on a compact smooth complex manifold is parallel: its squared norm is plurisubharmonic and hence constant by the maximum principle. Thus $H^0(S',A')=0$ follows from the zero degree-zero local-system cohomology. \ref{cdp:st:A.4.7} gives $H^0(S',\Omega_{S'}^1\otimes A')=0$ from degree-one vanishing. The conormal of the reduced full fibre $S'$ is trivial, with global generator $dg'$. Its conormal sequence, tensored by $A'$, now gives
$H^0(S',(\Omega_{U'}^1\otimes A')|_{S'})=0$.

All the graded factors for $mS'$ in \ref{cdp:st:A.3.1} equal this same restricted bundle, because the conormal is trivial. Induction in (A.10) proves $H^0(mS',(\Omega_{U'}^1\otimes A')|_{mS'})=0$. Since $p$ is étale, $p^*\Omega_U^1=\Omega_{U'}^1$, and finite faithfully flat pullback injects sections on $mS$ into sections on $mS'$. One can verify that injection locally by tensoring the inclusion of a module into its faithfully flat scalar extension, or by the trace divided by $m$. This proves \eqref{cdp:eq:A.15a}.

There are finitely many non-submersion values. For each of these fixed full fibres the coherent ambient bundle varies in the holomorphic Picard family constructed in \ref{cdp:st:A.4.9}, and proper semicontinuity makes its nonvanishing locus analytic. Each locus is proper because it excludes every $L_r$ with $r$ real irrational. Their finite union is proper, proving the simultaneous generic assertion. This proves the smooth-multiple conclusion of Remark~7.2. For a singular support the identical root cover retains its singularities and the kernel $N^*\otimes\mathcal J^{-1}$; the present proof does not erase that term in Remark~7.4.
\end{proof}
