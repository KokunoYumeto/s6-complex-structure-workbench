# Superseded endpoint-coordinate sign

2026-11-17T14:35:08.525990+00:00

The earlier SQ.4c used +epsilon(r-1)/(r+1) while SQ.4a had already defined gamma as the coefficient of the contracting B. The positive F9 moment direction runs from the v branch to the u branch; B contracts u by q times a unit, so its coefficient increases oppositely. The correct gamma_B is 1/2-epsilon(r-1)/(r+1). Both endpoint indices and the noninteger separation remain exactly retained; the zero Pontryagin result is unchanged, but the two oriented coordinates must not be identified implicitly. No theorem or mapping was promoted before this correction.
