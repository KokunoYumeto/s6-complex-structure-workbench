## Version-specific point

The arXiv record for 2610.00250 lists v1 (23 September 2026) and v2 (23 October 2026). The historical title includes deformations, and v1 Theorems1.3–1.4 state miniversality, the 2Z equivalence, and the full automorphism group. The current title is *Oka Geometry of Complex Six-Spheres* and its contents omit those deformation/classification sections. We infer no reason for the revision.

After observing this, we read the actual v1 proof chain. A.5.2 of the delivered paper gives its full reduction to this family's explicit charts, with additional coarse root-character calculations, an independently executed weight check, the cusp cup product, and the normal-derivative map. The mathematical result is supported by that written proof. The version history is not used as evidence for or against the proof.
