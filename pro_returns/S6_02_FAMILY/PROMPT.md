## What is to be settled

[AC26] constructs X_c from period functions τ, μ, β = β^part + c on the upper half-plane, for a constant c with Im c sufficiently negative, and writes (Remark 6.4, T l. 1890): "Whether X_{c₀} ≅ X_{c₀′} for admissible c₀ ≠ c₀′ is left open." [E26] constructs X(u, a₀, a₁) for u in a small punctured disc and discrete data (a₀, a₁), and remarks (8.1) that the complex structure "is indeed deforming", with a sketch; its Conjecture 8.2 proposes a flat proper filling at u = 0; its Remark 8.4 records Klingler's question whether the monodromy group acts properly discontinuously on the period domain. Viaclovsky (arXiv:2610.39102, §1.1) asks: (2) "Does varying u, with the logarithmic transformation data fixed, give all sufficiently small deformations?" (3) "For fixed multiplicities and fixed u, do different choices of the admissible translation vectors produce even more biholomorphically distinct complex structures on S6?" X. Yang (arXiv:2609.29666) proves, for related families with multiplicities (4,6) and (3,6) that are not S^6, that two members are biholomorphic exactly when their parameters differ by an element of (6/r)Z.

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

- **Lead 1 (centralizer and norm obstruction).** The rational centralizer of A₁, A₂ in End(Λ ⊗ Q) is {aI + bE}, E = δ̂ ⊗ γ. A biholomorphism X_c → X_{c′} covers the identity of B, is affine on fibres with L Π_c = Π_{c′}(aI + bE), a = ±1, so L = aI and c′ − c = −b/a ∈ Z. Extending over the multiple fibres requires l_j ∈ Λ with N_j l_j = −(1 − a)v_j − nε_jδ̂, N_j = 1 + A_j + … + A_j^{m_j−1}; N₁Λ = 3Zv₁ + Zδ̂, N₂Λ = 4Z(1,3,−3,0) + 2Zδ̂; Λ^{A₁}/N₁Λ = Z/3, Λ^{A₂}/N₂Λ = Z/4 ⊕ Z/2; a = −1 is impossible and for a = 1 the obstruction is the class of nδ̂ at the order-four fibre, of order two.
- **Lead 2 (even shifts).** For even n there is an isomorphism X_c → X_{c+n} with linear part I and lattice map I − nE, built from r₁ = −(n/3)(û + ŵ), r₂ = (n/4)(û + ŵ), the toric map (x, t) ↦ (t^{q_n}x, t) with q_n = (−3n/2, n/2) at the cusp, and a correction from H¹(B, V) = 0 for the rank-two bundle V, 0 → O_B → V → O_B(−p₀) → 0.
- **Lead 3 (Albanese cross-check).** Alb(S₁) = C/(Z + κ₁Z) and Alb(S₂) has modulus κ₂/2, with κ_j(c) = κ_j^part + c; so the nome of Alb(S₂) is a constant times w and that of Alb(S₁) a constant times w². This would prove the "only if" for generic c without the lattice argument.
- **Lead 4 (all discrete data).** For twist vectors w_j, w′_j ∈ Λ^{A_j}, a map X(c; w) → X(c′; w′) with linear part I exists iff there are l_j with N_j l_j = w_j − (I + nE)w′_j and a compatible cusp value; this holds iff p = p′ and, writing w₂ = r₂ε′ + k₂δ̂, c′ − c ≡ k₂ − k₂′ mod 2. Consequence: every member diffeomorphic to S^6 of the family with multiplicities (3,4) is biholomorphic to exactly one X_w; in Engel's notation X_{c+1} = X(u; a₀ + δ/3, a₁ − δ/4) up to conventions, and for fixed u the threefolds X(u, a₀, a₁) with |12ψ(a₀ + a₁)| = 1 form exactly two isomorphism classes, exchanged by the monodromy of the marking around u = 0.
- **Lead 5 (μ and the cusp fibre).** e^{2πiμ(p₀)} = −(5 + 2√6)^{±1}; μ is the Eichler-Zagier zero of ℘ (formula in `notes/G1a_notes.md`, G1a-3; Duke-Imamoglu attached). The three gluing multipliers of W are e^{−2πiμ₀}, e^{12πiμ₀}, e^{−2πi(7μ₀ + b₀)} with b₀ = (β + τ)(p₀); two are units of Z[√6] and the third moves with c, so the cusp fibres of X_c and X_{c+1} are isomorphic.
- **Lead 6 (special fibres).** The fibre over t has a surjection onto its elliptic curve E_t with elliptic kernel iff 6μ ∈ Zτ + Z, i.e. 6P(t) = O(t); on y² = x³ − 3t³(t−1)x + 2t⁴(t−1)² this holds exactly at t = 64(47 ± 27√3)/11; these two fibres, and the fibres over the dense countable set where P(t) is torsion, are abelian surfaces; the number of t with 6kP = O is 3k² − 1 with multiplicity.
- **Lead 7 (automorphisms).** Aut(X_c) = C^* (connected): cocycles with l_j ∈ (1 − A_j)Λ modulo coboundaries give no extra component.

If it does not come back, write the work out in the chat as text: every deliverable file in full, each in its own code block headed by its file name, taken from the code that saved it; then continue the mathematics in the chat as far as possible. An answer that only reports the failure or asks for the files again is not acceptable.
