3. By (Cornulier, 2011, Theorem 3.1, p. 2017), the map from closed subgroups of a finite-

it to the dual graph gives exactly (19.2). This theorem’s hypotheses are met by the real
vector space underlying C2 and by the closed full lattices Γn; no nonclosed group is used as a
Chabauty point.
