Proposition 22.17 (A restricted non-Kähler cap obstruction). [tag 0316]
Let D be a compact reduced normal-crossings complex space. Suppose its irreducible compo-
nents have been partitioned into closed unions D0, . . . , Ds, with their reduced induced structures.
Assume that
H1(Di, C) −→H1(Di, O)
is surjective for every i,
(22.1)
and that the analogous map is injective for every pairwise intersection Di ∩Dj. Then
H1(D, C) −→H1(D, OD)
is surjective.
(22.2)
In particular these hypotheses hold for the following specified cap assembly. The horizontal
block D0 is a two-section pinching of a ruled bundle over an analytic locally trivial torsor V →C
of 22.12, with conductor map A covering σ and A12 = t6P . The normalization may undergo the
modifications of 22.11, or any smooth bimeromorphic modifications for which the two conductor
restriction maps on H1 are the same pullbacks as before. The other blocks are smooth hyperbolic
toric Kato threefolds or compact smooth spaces satisfying (22.1), for example class-C spaces.
Assume every pairwise overlap is either a reduced union of invariant divisors of a hyperbolic
toric Kato threefold, or a compact normal-crossings surface whose normalized components and
normalized double curves are rational. No such assembly can be a semistable central fibre of the
S6 family.
Proof. 1. Write D[p] for the disjoint union of intersections of p+1 distinct blocks. The augmented
closed-cover complexes resolve both CD and OD. For the coherent assertion this can be checked
in a normal-crossings coordinate chart. The local equation of D is a product of distinct
coordinates, and each block groups a disjoint subset of those factors. The corresponding ideals
are generated by products in disjoint sets of variables. The ordinary alternating intersection
complex is exact, as one sees on each monomial, where it is the augmented complex of a
simplex. The same contraction acts coefficientwise on convergent power series. For constant
sheaves, small intersections are contractible unions of coordinate subspaces, and the same
stalkwise simplex complex is exact. This proves both resolutions also when the global blocks
are self-crossing.
