8. At a central node, the smooth Kato total space has coordinates (u, v, s) with divisor h =
uv −s = 0. In coordinates (u, v, h) its map to the base is

(22.20)
It is a submersion. The total space of the normal line, with the inherited map from D, has

10. There is also a concrete obstruction to the most direct global use of this plumbing. For fixed

near strict-axis points. The other axis Ds is v = 0, x ̸= 0. The axes are disjoint, and hence
every exceptional rational curve misses Ds. A plumbing that leaves the complement of a small
tubular neighbourhood of Ds unchanged retains these compact curves in its r ̸= 0 fibres over
the same s. A complex torus has no rational curve: a holomorphic map from P1 lifts to its
