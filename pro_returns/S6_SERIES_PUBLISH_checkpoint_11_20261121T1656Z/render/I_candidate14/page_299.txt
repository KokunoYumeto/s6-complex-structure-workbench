Remark 26.7 (The theta and metric normalizations). [tag IA28] The multiplication law in

For αΛ ⊂Λ, the exponential multipliers of θ(αz)/θ(z)d cancel because α¯α = d. Its remaining

quotient. The action of α on this differential is algebraic, so α2 is an allowed normalization.
2. Choose a nonreal α ∈O. Since the normalized sigma series is σ(z) = z + O(z5) with algebraic

the chosen field. At nontorsion u, none of the arguments αu is a lattice point; evaluating the
identity proves the algebraicity implication. The injective map O →C, α 7→αu, proves the
rank assertion.
3. The qualification about rationality is necessary. For a square lattice, s2 = 0 and α = 1 + i give
F1+i = 2i℘. The unsquared quotient has a simple zero at the nonzero point in ker(1 + i) and
a simple pole at O, which is not a principal divisor on E; its square is the rational function
just displayed.
