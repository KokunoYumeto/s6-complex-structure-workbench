Proposition 17.3 (Two conjugacy classes of real structures). [tag 02PE]
For a real member the antiholomorphic involutions have exactly two conjugacy classes under
holomorphic automorphisms. For any fixed involutive splitting σ they are represented by σ and
ρ(1/2)σ. Every nonempty fixed set is a closed connected orientable totally real three-manifold
with the mod-two homology of S3. The circle ρ preserves each such fixed set and acts semifreely
on it.
