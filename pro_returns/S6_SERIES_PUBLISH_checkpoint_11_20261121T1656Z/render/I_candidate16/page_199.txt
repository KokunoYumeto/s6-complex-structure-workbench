7. For completeness, the two scalar Hopf surfaces used above are not biholomorphic.
An
isomorphism lifts to an automorphism of C2 \ {0} that intertwines the two cyclic generators
or their inverses. Hartogs extension applies to the lift and its inverse; both fix zero and have
invertible derivative there. Differentiating the intertwining identity gives e−2π = (−e−π)±1,
which is false. They have no rational curves, since a map P1 →Hα lifts to C2 \ {0} and is
constant. The usual resolution of a bimeromorphic map of smooth compact surfaces therefore
also shows that these two Hopf surfaces are not bimeromorphic. This agrees with the explicit
scalar case of Abasheva and Déev (2024, arXiv version 1, Section 2.2, pp. 4–5).
8. The fundamental groups also exclude a meromorphic extension of the punctured product
comparison. A sufficiently small closed semistable neighbourhood in H retracts onto Y ,
