8. Why global continuation gives a finite orbit. Choose a base point q∗∈B◦. Let S be the finite
set of angular classes furnished at q∗by all the connected double-curve fibres, their ordered
branches, their monomial-fibre components, and both orientations. Take generators of the
finitely generated group π1(B◦, q∗). A generator can be lifted to a path in the appropriate
family of double curves; it ends in one of the finitely many connected fibre components. Avoid
the triple points during this lift. Properness over the compact generator paths and finitely
many product charts give one sufficiently small r∗for which all their annular circles can be
transported in the smooth fibres Xq,r∗.
