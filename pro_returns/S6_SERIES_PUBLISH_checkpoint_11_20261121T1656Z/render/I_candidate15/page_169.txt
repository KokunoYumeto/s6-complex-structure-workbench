Proposition 21.9 (An Euler-zero smoothing of the pinching). [tag PT02] The pinching Q admits
a proper flat smoothing with smooth fibres of Euler number zero, obtained by choosing the zero of
its smoothing section on a smooth elliptic fibre of S.
Proof. Replace ϕ in the global construction of 20.6 by a section of J vanishing on a smooth
elliptic fibre of S. The compact collar construction still applies. At its zero divisor the fixed
nonzero-parameter equation is xy = uz with dz ̸= 0, so every nearby threefold is smooth. The
double-locus nearby Euler function is one on that elliptic curve and zero off it; its integral is zero.
Thus this proper flat smoothing has Euler number zero, whereas the fibres of Engel’s family have
Euler number two by Theorem 21.8.
