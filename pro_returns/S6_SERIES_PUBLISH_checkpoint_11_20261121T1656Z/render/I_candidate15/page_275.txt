Remark 24.15 (Ordinary CM leading-term theorems). [tag IA05]
The applicable ordinary CM results concern a prime p at which E has good ordinary reduction,
equivalently p splits in the CM field. One formulation of Rubin’s rank-one formula is Bertolini
et al. (2012, Theorem 1, p. 262): for a curve over Q with CM by the maximal order, an ordinary
prime prime to the conductor, and a simple central zero, an appropriate Katz p-adic L-value
is, up to a nonzero algebraic factor, the square of a formal logarithm of a nontorsion rational
point divided by a p-adic period. The analytic-rank-one input holds by proposition 24.11. The
formal logarithm, Katz value and period in this formula are the p-adic quantities specified in
that theorem.
For the p-part of the real leading-term formula, a modern precise version is Castella (2025,
Theorem A, p. 2 of the author version). In addition to analytic rank one it requires full CM,
abelian torsion field over the CM field, p ∤6hK, splitting of p, conductor prime to p, and that
the different of K divide the Hecke conductor exactly. For the maximal odd-discriminant cases
here, the latter follows from the exponent two of the ramified prime in the rational conductor.
The nonmaximal cases of discriminant −12 and −28 can be passed through a 2-isogeny at
p > 3. The discriminant −16 case requires care: the direct application of this version has a
different-conductor hypothesis at 2 that fails, so this formulation does not apply directly to that
case.
