Lemma 22.32 (The quotient of indexed cores). [tag PT15] Let h be an automorphism of H, and
let modifications pj : Yj →H, indexed by j ∈Z, have isomorphisms ehj : Yj →Yj+1 satisfying
pj+1ehj = hpj. On an h-invariant open set H◦over which every pj is an isomorphism, the shift
quotient of the disjoint union of their cores is biholomorphic to H◦by
[(j, y)] 7−→h−jpj(y).
Under this isomorphism, its natural map to the orbit space H◦/⟨h⟩is the ordinary quotient map.
Moreover, two embedded compact subspaces of a Hausdorff space that share a subset open and
dense in both have the same image.
Proof. The displayed map is invariant under (j, y) 7→(j + 1, ehjy) because h−j−1pj+1ehj = h−jpj.
Every shift orbit meets the zeroth core exactly once, so the inverse is x 7→[(0, p−1
0 x)]. These maps
are holomorphic on the component charts. Composing with H◦→H◦/⟨h⟩gives the asserted
orbit-space map. For the last assertion, compact images in a Hausdorff space are closed; each of
the two images is consequently the closure of their shared dense subset.
