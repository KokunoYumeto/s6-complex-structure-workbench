one-forms are
ω1 = dZ/Z,

invariant, so this accounts for all relative holomorphic one- and two-forms on the ordinary
fibres. The relative log forms extend in the Tate model. Ordinary coherent relative forms also
give finite-order extensions: for example tω1, tω2, and t2ω1 ∧ω2 extend as ordinary relative
forms. In the first resolution chart the first two are explicitly
tω1 = Av dx,
tω2 = Av dx + xv dA;
the other chart gives the analogous regular expressions. At an isolated Tate node the same
identity is t dx/x = y dx.
6. More generally, take the torsion-free image of each coherent direct image of relative forms in
its bundle on the punctured disc. Under the two punctured identifications id and Th, these
are identical coherent lattices, since the induced map on the entire ordinary relative-form
bundle is the identity. Coherence therefore gives no bound on h in this example.
7. Gauss–Manin and an absolute form. Every fibre translation is homotopic to the identity
and acts identically on integral cohomology. Consequently Th induces the identity on the
flat Gauss–Manin bundle, its connection, and its Hodge subbundle. The logarithmic period
growth and regular singularity of that connection are unchanged.
