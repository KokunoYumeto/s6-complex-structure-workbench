Remark 26.4 (The motivic dimension formula). [tag IA11] Bertolin et al. (2022, Construction 3.4,
p. 12, and Lemma 3.5, p. 13, arXiv v2) compute the motivic group from its abelian point and
extension data and the central Weil-pairing bracket. For the 1-motives used here, let B be the
smallest abelian subvariety generated by these data, and let Z(1) be the central torus obtained
from that bracket and the remaining algebraic lift. Their dimension formula is
dim Gmot(M) = dim Gmot(A) + 2 dim B + dim Z(1).
