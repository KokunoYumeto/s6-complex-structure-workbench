4. For the fundamental group, first choose the reference model obtained by base-changing X +
by u = rn and resolving the surface equation xy = rn along its smooth conductor S. Its
normalized central components are R+ and n −1 projective-line bundles over S. All are
simply connected, and their double surfaces are copies of the simply connected S. The gluing
graph is an n-cycle, interpreted as the self-gluing circle when n = 1. Van Kampen therefore
gives fundamental group Z for this reference centre.
