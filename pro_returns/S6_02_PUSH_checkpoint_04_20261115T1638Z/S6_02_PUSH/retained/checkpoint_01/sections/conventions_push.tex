\section*{Conventions and scope}
We use $B=\PP^1_t$, with $p_1=0$, $p_2=1$, and $p_0=\infty$.
For the standard twisted family $f:X_c\to B$ we write
$f^*p_1=3S_1$, $f^*p_2=4S_2$, and $W=f^{-1}(p_0)$;
$S_1,S_2$ denote the reductions. The symbol $W_8$ will denote an
eight-dimensional coboundary, to distinguish it from the surface $W$.
The lattice $\Lambda=\Z^4$ has ordered basis
$(\widehat\gamma,\widehat u,\widehat w,\widehat\delta)$,
and all lattice matrices act on columns. We use
\[
 A_1=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0&0\\6&0&1&0\\-6&-1&-1&0\\-2&1&0&1\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 A_2=\begin{pmatrix}1&0&0&0\\0&0&-1&0\\-6&1&0&0\\3&0&1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
We put $A_0=(A_1A_2)^{-1}$,
$\epsilon=(1,2,-4,0)^t$, $\epsilon'=(1,3,-3,0)^t$,
$v_1=\epsilon$, $v_2=-\epsilon'$, and $l_0=0$.
Thus $p=12l_0-4l_1-3l_2=-1$.
The period matrix and parameters are
\[
 \Pi_c=\begin{pmatrix}6\mu&\tau&1&0\\\beta^{\rm part}+c&\mu&0&1\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad w=e^{-\pi\ii c},\qquad u=w^2.
\]
We normalize $\beta^{\rm part}+\tau\to0$ at $p_0$ and use
$D_c=\Im(\beta^{\rm part}+c)-6(\Im\mu)^2/\Im\tau$.
The numerical maximum is denoted by $M$; the line bundle on Engel's
rational elliptic surface is denoted by $M_S=\OO_S(P-O)$.
We put $T=t_{6P}$, $R=\PP_S(\OO\oplus M_S)$, and use $Q$ for the
specified quotient identifying its zero and infinity sections by $T$.
The untwisted family is $X(u)$; the sphere family is $X_w$.
Neither a local toric chart nor a candidate central fibre is called a
global filling without the required quotient and gluing proof.
All homology groups have integral coefficients unless otherwise stated.
The six-sphere has its complex orientation, and a positive parameter
circle is counterclockwise. A smoothing and a resolution are distinct
operations throughout.

This return is incomplete. Every proved assertion is restricted to the
hypotheses stated with it; an unresolved construction or invariant is not
assigned a value. The supplied return and review are treated as
mathematical source material requiring verification, rather than as
independent authorities. We claim no priority.
