# The global marked-source loop route: retained maps and open endpoints

Working route proposed by root after K49; no global return relation is assigned.

The literal source is
\[
 E_v:\quad Y^2=X^3+(1-2v)X^2+TX+v^2,\qquad G=(0,v),
 \qquad T_0(v)=v^2-2v.
\]
The old normalized source uses \(v=u^3\), with coefficient
\(a=u^{-2}-2u\). Its exact discriminant-factor identity is
\[
 a^3-27=-\frac{(v+1)^2(8v-1)}{v^2}.
\]
The selected nodal curve at \(T=T_0\) factors as
\[
 Y^2=(X-v)^2(X+1).
\]
Its normalization has \(s^2=X+1\), \(Y=s(X-v)\), and its two
node preimages are \(s=\pm\sqrt{1+v}\). Thus the global cover
\(\rho^2=1+v\) labels the branches. Positive loops about \(-1\)
and infinity exchange them; the exact Tate/Cartier reflection is
F54's map and has linear fan transition J. On the rho cover the
two lifts of v=0 are rho=+1 and rho=-1. The reflection compares the
plus lift in fan T with the minus lift in fan JT, so a global loop
relation cannot silently replace those two marked returns by twice
one unknown.

The other critical-value curves are
\[
 T_\pm=\frac{1+4v\pm(1-8v)^{3/2}}8.
\]
At v=1/8 they coincide at 3/16, while the selected value is
-15/64. The selected cusp remains disjoint from that collision.
The exact source/root/Cartier extension around this point is the
next local comparison to verify; no loop value is assigned here.

For infinity, put v=w^{-2} and use the literal source map
\[
 x=w^2X,\quad y=w^3Y,\quad t=w^4T.
\]
It gives
\[
 y^2=x^3+(w^2-2)x^2+tx+w^2,\qquad G=(0,w),
 \qquad t_0=1-2w^2.
\]
The endpoint of the w-to--w sheet exchange is the actual source
map (x,y,t,w) to (x,-y,t,-w). Its extension to the marked Cartier
line, cap roots, scalar and fan has still to be tracked. The literal
scalar is lambda=v^N lambda0=w^{-2N}lambda0. It must remain in that
comparison; replacing it by a constant or assigning an infinity
return from a scalar formula alone is not done here.

At w=0 the source equation is y^2=x(x^2-2x+t). The selected node
is t=1 and the other singular fibre is t=0. This exact limit does
not by itself supply a family of the compact suspensions across
w=0. No endpoint or numerical source-zero class follows yet.
