## What is to be settled

[E26] Remark 8.5: the construction of the complex structures on S^6 "works in significantly greater generality" (other elliptic surfaces π: S → C with a section, a line bundle of fibre degree zero and a translation); "it is unclear what class of 6-manifolds can arise from such a construction"; and moving the zero of the linearization away from the fibre over ∞ "has the effect, it appears, of contracting the (−1,−1)-curve in the Mumford fiber and then smoothing the resulting threefold ordinary double point", a conifold transition. Viaclovsky (arXiv:2610.39102, Theorem 1.1) obtains S^6 for logarithmic transformations of order 3k and 4l with gcd(3k, 4l) = 1 and asks (Question 4): "Other starting rational elliptic surfaces have been found to produce complex structures on S6 …, corresponding to many cases on Oguiso-Shioda's list with Mordell-Weil group Z. Which other starting configurations admit infinitely many allowable multiplicities also yielding S6?" Wang (arXiv:2609.11541) obtains a simply connected rational homology sphere with H₂ = H₃ = Z/2 from the configuration IV*, I₂, I₁, I₁; Yang (arXiv:2609.29666) obtains families with π₁ = Z/2 and Z/3 and Euler number 2r; Liu and Rollenske (arXiv:2609.33826) another family on S^6.

Where the matter stands (survey F11, F25, F26, F30):

1. [AC26] Theorems 7.17 and 7.22 give, for every admissible triple (l₀, l₁, l₂) with p = 12l₀ − 4l₁ − 3l₂, π₁ = Z/|p| and H_* = (Z, Z/p, Z/p, Z/p, Z/p, 0, Z). The diffeomorphism types of these threefolds and of their universal covers are identified nowhere.
2. The workbench studies a rank-three "higher torus wrap": a local Mumford degeneration of 3-tori with a holomorphic map onto the cusp neighbourhood of X, and records the global gluing as open (`workbench/higher_rung_toroidal_elliptic_bridge.tex` l. 1057-1105, (TEB.54); `workbench/higher_rung_fourfold_toric_degeneration.tex`; `workbench/higher_rung_s8_constraints.tex`, which proves only that the result could not be S^8). A reader of the survey observes that the gluing is the fibre product of X with Engel's rational elliptic surface (`notes/E_notes.md`, E-2): a sketch, checked by no one.
3. The same reader isolates a question (`notes/E_notes.md`, E-3): the Euler number 3! = 6 of the rank-three cusp is the smallest that Hirzebruch's congruence allows for an almost complex 8-manifold with c₁c₃ = 0, a 3-connected such manifold has the invariants of 3HP² # (−HP²), and that manifold is almost complex (Mandal, arXiv:2609.27929). Is there a compact complex fourfold of this kind, fibred in 3-tori over P¹?
4. The conifold transition and the systematic answer to "what 6-manifolds arise" are not taken up anywhere in the workspace.

This prompt asks for the manifolds: identified up to diffeomorphism where they exist, constructed where they are only sketched, and for dimension eight either a construction or a proof that the search space is empty.

C.1. Prove the facts of Lead 3 (the Euler number 6d; the congruences; the invariants of a 3-connected almost complex 8-manifold with χ = 6; read Mandal's theorem and quote it exactly).
