# S6_5_MANIFOLDS: the other manifolds the S^6 construction gives. Covers, other elliptic surfaces, the conifold transition, the fibre-product fourfold, and a 3-connected analogue in dimension eight

Development prompt, 12 November 2026. File prefix for your deliverables: `S6_5_MANIFOLDS`.

## What is to be settled

[E26] Remark 8.5: the construction of the complex structures on S^6 "works in significantly greater generality" (other elliptic surfaces π: S → C with a section, a line bundle of fibre degree zero and a translation); "it is unclear what class of 6-manifolds can arise from such a construction"; and moving the zero of the linearization away from the fibre over ∞ "has the effect, it appears, of contracting the (−1,−1)-curve in the Mumford fiber and then smoothing the resulting threefold ordinary double point", a conifold transition. Viaclovsky (arXiv:2610.39102, Theorem 1.1) obtains S^6 for logarithmic transformations of order 3k and 4l with gcd(3k, 4l) = 1 and asks (Question 4): "Other starting rational elliptic surfaces have been found to produce complex structures on S6 …, corresponding to many cases on Oguiso-Shioda's list with Mordell-Weil group Z. Which other starting configurations admit infinitely many allowable multiplicities also yielding S6?" Wang (arXiv:2609.11541) obtains a simply connected rational homology sphere with H₂ = H₃ = Z/2 from the configuration IV*, I₂, I₁, I₁; Yang (arXiv:2609.29666) obtains families with π₁ = Z/2 and Z/3 and Euler number 2r; Liu and Rollenske (arXiv:2609.33826) another family on S^6.

Where the matter stands (survey F11, F25, F26, F30):

1. [AC26] Theorems 7.17 and 7.22 give, for every admissible triple (l₀, l₁, l₂) with p = 12l₀ − 4l₁ − 3l₂, π₁ = Z/|p| and H_* = (Z, Z/p, Z/p, Z/p, Z/p, 0, Z). The diffeomorphism types of these threefolds and of their universal covers are identified nowhere.
2. The workbench studies a rank-three "higher torus wrap": a local Mumford degeneration of 3-tori with a holomorphic map onto the cusp neighbourhood of X, and records the global gluing as open (`workbench/higher_rung_toroidal_elliptic_bridge.tex` l. 1057-1105, (TEB.54); `workbench/higher_rung_fourfold_toric_degeneration.tex`; `workbench/higher_rung_s8_constraints.tex`, which proves only that the result could not be S^8). A reader of the survey observes that the gluing is the fibre product of X with Engel's rational elliptic surface (`notes/E_notes.md`, E-2): a sketch, checked by no one.
3. The same reader isolates a question (`notes/E_notes.md`, E-3): the Euler number 3! = 6 of the rank-three cusp is the smallest that Hirzebruch's congruence allows for an almost complex 8-manifold with c₁c₃ = 0, a 3-connected such manifold has the invariants of 3HP² # (−HP²), and that manifold is almost complex (Mandal, arXiv:2609.27929). Is there a compact complex fourfold of this kind, fibred in 3-tori over P¹?
4. The conifold transition and the systematic answer to "what 6-manifolds arise" are not taken up anywhere in the workspace.

This prompt asks for the manifolds: identified up to diffeomorphism where they exist, constructed where they are only sketched, and for dimension eight either a construction or a proof that the search space is empty.

## Leads (unverified; prove or refute each)

From the survey and the readers' notes `notes/E_notes.md` (E-1 to E-5, with the scripts `notes/scripts/E_leray_betti.py`, `notes/scripts/E_h1_bielliptic.py`) and `notes/G1a_notes.md` (G1a-9, G1a-13(e)).

- **Lead 1 (the members with |p| > 1).** The universal cover X̃_p of a member with π₁ = Z/|p| is a simply connected compact complex threefold with e = 2|p| and a cusp fibre with |p| components; if H₂(X̃_p; Z) = 0 and H₃ is free it is diffeomorphic to the connected sum of |p| − 1 copies of S³ × S³.
- **Lead 2 (the fourfold).** The matrices C₁ = [[−1,−1],[1,0]], C₂′ = [[0,1],[−1,0]], C₀ = [[1,1],[0,1]] of the workbench's rank-three extension are Kodaira's local monodromies of fibres IV*, III, I₁, so the extension is the fibre product with Engel's surface S: Z = X ×_{P¹} S, normalized at p₁, p₂ as (F ×_{Δ̃} E)/μ_{m_j} and resolved at the cusp by the workbench's toric model N₃^C, is a compact complex fourfold with a holomorphic map to P¹ with general fibre F_z × E_z and an elliptic fibration Π: Z → X with a section; χ(Z) = 6, π₁(Z) = 1, b₂(Z) ≥ 2, Z is not Kähler; heuristic Betti numbers (1,0,2,1,2,1,2,0,1).
- **Lead 3 (dimension eight).** A proper map g: M⁸ → S² that is a T⁶-bundle off finitely many points, with one maximally degenerate cusp fibre of index d and all other special fibres free finite quotients of T⁶, has χ(M) = 6d (`workbench/higher_rung_fourfold_toric_degeneration.tex`, `hrftd:cor:euler-shear-determinant`). A closed almost complex 8-manifold with c₁ = c₃ = 0 rationally has 6 | χ, 3c₂² = 45σ − 14χ and Td = (3σ − χ)/48; a 3-connected one with χ = 6 has b₄ = 4, σ = 2, form 3⟨1⟩ ⊕ ⟨−1⟩, c₂² = 2, Td = 0. 3HP² # (−HP²) is almost complex (Mandal, arXiv:2609.27929, attached). The diagonal monodromy I₃ ⊗ (S, Q, T) of E_τ³ has no free affine lifts (`hrftd:prop:finite-fixed-points`).

## Parts

Every Part asks for mathematics: a manifold identified with proof, a construction carried out, a search run with its code and its bounds. Where something resists, prove the strongest partial statement and state the obstruction exactly with its smallest undecided instance. Carry each Part to where it stops.

**A. The other manifolds.**
A.1. Prove or refute Lead 1: for each p compute H_*(X̃_p; Z), the cup products, w₂ and p₁, and identify X̃_p and X_p up to diffeomorphism by the classification of simply connected 6-manifolds (Wall, Jupp, Zhubr; cite the theorems by number) and of their free quotients as far as it goes. State the resulting theorem on complex structures (on connected sums of copies of S³ × S³ with a free Z/|p| action, if that is what comes out), and compare with the complex structures already known on these manifolds.
A.2. Viaclovsky's Question 4 and Engel's Remark 8.5. Take the rational elliptic surfaces with Mordell-Weil group Z from the Oguiso-Shioda list (cited in [E26] and in arXiv:2610.39102). For each configuration of singular fibres, with all choices of which additive fibres are log-transformed (with every multiplicity k·ord compatible with the finite monodromy) and which multiplicative fibres are filled by Mumford degenerations, compute by program the fundamental group and integral homology of the resulting 6-manifold from the monodromy and the twist data, as in [E26] §§5-7 and [AC26] §7. Deliver the table: which configurations give S^6, which give other simply connected manifolds (identified by A.1's method), which give non-simply-connected ones. Reproduce the known cases ([AC26], Viaclovsky, Liu-Rollenske, Wang, Yang) as checks.
A.3. The conifold transition of [E26] Remark 8.5: construct the family in which the zero of the linearization moves away from the fibre over ∞, prove or refute that the limit contracts a (−1,−1)-curve of the Mumford fibre and that the nearby threefold is a smoothing of an ordinary double point; identify the diffeomorphism type on the other side of the transition (S³ × S³, if the Euler number drops by 2 and it stays simply connected with H₂ = 0: prove it) and its complex invariants (K, a, h^{0,q}).

**B. The fibre-product fourfold.**
B.1. Prove or refute Lead 2: construct Z completely (the three local models and the gluing), prove compactness and smoothness, and compute π₁(Z), H_*(Z; Z) with torsion, the signature and the intersection form on H⁴, by Mayer-Vietoris or Leray as in [AC26] §7 (program the linear algebra).
B.2. Compute the complex invariants of Z: K_Z, a(Z), h^{0,q}, and decide whether Z is in Fujiki's class C. Then the general statement: for a compact complex manifold Y with a holomorphic map to P¹ that is a submersion over 0, 1, ∞, X ×_{P¹} Y is a compact complex manifold; identify the diffeomorphism types for Y = P¹ × N and for Y a second copy of X after a Möbius transformation moving the critical values (a fibre product X ×_{P¹} φ*X with general fibre T⁴ × T⁴): compute χ, π₁, H_*.

**C. A 3-connected complex fourfold fibred in 3-tori?**
C.1. Prove the facts of Lead 3 (the Euler number 6d; the congruences; the invariants of a 3-connected almost complex 8-manifold with χ = 6; read Mandal's theorem and quote it exactly).
C.2. Classify by program, up to conjugacy in GL₆(Z), the tuples (M₁, …, M_k, M₀) with M₁ ⋯ M_k M₀ = 1, each M_i (i ≥ 1) of finite order with a free affine lift x ↦ M_i x + v_i/m_i, and M₀ unipotent with (M₀ − 1)² = 0, rank(M₀ − 1) = 3 and M₀ − 1 inducing an isomorphism of the quotient lattice onto the image lattice (index 1), for k = 2 and k = 3; for each, with each choice of twists, compute π₁ and H_*(·; Z) of the glued 8-manifold by van Kampen and Mayer-Vietoris, and list the data with π₁ = 1 and H₂ = H₃ = 0. If the search space must be bounded, say how and why the bound loses nothing, or what it loses.
C.3. For each surviving datum decide whether a holomorphic period family with that monodromy exists over the punctured sphere, with non-degenerate imaginary part and bounded correction at the cusp. The natural source is Engel's method with S replaced by a family of abelian surfaces A → P¹ with three singular fibres (one of toric rank 2, two with finite monodromy; for instance the Jacobian fibration of a pencil of genus-2 curves), a fibrewise degree-0 line bundle and a section of infinite order: construct it, or prove it cannot have the required monodromy.
C.4. Conclude with one of: an explicit compact complex fourfold that is 3-connected with χ = 6, identified in Wall's classification of 3-connected 8-manifolds and compared with 3HP² # (−HP²); or a theorem that no datum of type C.2 gives H₂ = H₃ = 0; or the exact list of undecided data.
