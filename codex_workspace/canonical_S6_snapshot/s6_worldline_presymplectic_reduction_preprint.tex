\begin{abstract}
Retaining \(\alpha>0\), \(c>0\), \(m_0\), all orbit types, and the sign
\(\Omega=-d\langle p,dx\rangle_L\), this paper computes the complete
presymplectic reduction of
\[
 S[x,Z,e]=\int_I\left(\langle\alpha Z^*Z,\dot x\rangle_L
 -\frac e2\bigl(\det(\alpha Z^*Z)-m_0^2c^2\bigr)\right)d\tau
\]
by arbitrary \(\tau\)-dependent left \(SU(3)\) transformations.  For
\(m_0>0\), the colour action is free, the colour quotient is
\(\mathcal J_2\times\mathcal H^+_{m_0,c}\), and quotienting its
characteristic flow \((x,p)\mapsto(x+sp,p)\) gives the canonical
symplectic space \(T^*\mathcal H^+_{m_0,c}\); the system is exactly the
future-timelike spinless relativistic particle.

For \(m_0=0\), the intrinsic nonzero rank-one quotient and its
characteristic reduction are exactly
\(T^*\mathcal N_+^\times\), but the written multiplier action is not the
standard massless variational system.  The identity
\(\det(\alpha Z^*Z)=\alpha^2\sum_{a<b}|\Delta_{ab}(Z)|^2\) makes its
differential vanish on every rank-one matrix; hence
\(\dot x=\lambda p\) with no equation \(\lambda=e\), four transverse
rank-opening directions are first-order null without being colour gauge,
and the apex \(Z=0\) admits arbitrary \(x(\tau)\).

For the separately constrained \((\lambda_0,\omega_0)\) packet with
\(\omega_0\ne0\), the carrier remains rank two, but its momentum
projection has rank four for \(w\ne0\) and rank three at \(w=0\).
Direct variation therefore forces \(\dot x=0\) strictly above the sharp
threshold and gives the ordinary characteristic
\(\dot x\in\mathbb Rp\) only at the threshold.  Its determinant bound
and multiplication/Fock spectra are exact in their algebraic and free
operator scope; they are not a dynamically generated interacting mass
gap, and the multiplication operator is not the Hamiltonian generating
the displayed worldline action.
\end{abstract}
