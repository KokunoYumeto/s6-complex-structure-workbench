\begin{abstract}
We transport the actual de Bruijn--Newman initial function through the
retained cover $s=-r^2/2$. We give its connection, square, branch
commutators, analytic heat realization, and the exact evolution of its
arithmetic relation. We also compute the full Euclidean spatial
diffusion of its actual scalar observable and its transported covector.
The original inverse-fibre coordinates and all
analytic-unit terms remain present. Complete proofs specify the
relations between branch escape, arithmetic zeros, and the transported
operators. The inverse-fibre construction alone does not exhibit a counterexample
to the Riemann hypothesis or to the classical Euclidean Navier--Stokes problem.
The source's exact fourth-order interacting electric-correlation
coefficient is now propagated into this arithmetic heat flow through
an explicitly invertible Fourier operator. Its full source proof,
spectral derivative term, coupling scales, and spatial pullbacks are
included. The complete arithmetic quotient continuation proves exact
heat transport, closure and multiplicity-jet results in its stated
topologies, and Mellin, principal-part and compact-test morphisms.
The common-representative map identifies its heat dynamics with
the original Fourier dynamics.
The complete second Euclidean diffusion has an explicit jet residue.
We prove that its divergence along the original escaping trajectory
survives in the actual fourth coefficient at sufficiently negative
Newman times, with a full error bound.
The original three special points of the supplied period construction
also give an exact real three-torus quotient. Their retained twists and
cusp direction generate a three-dimensional forced fluid with all mixed
interactions and localization terms calculated explicitly.
For a supplied incompressible flow we also construct three profiles in
the original polynomial target and recover its velocity from their
arithmetic temporal defect by an explicit inverse integral and norm
identity. The complete force-dependent acceleration, material diffusion
and translated zero divisors are calculated.

The actual corrected Navier--Stokes source is also retained as an
explicit construction input. Its exact axis vorticity gives an arithmetic
endpoint derivative and a growing Mellin residue. An invertible radial
map carries the complete corrected central angular momentum and all its
fluxes into order-one entire functions with an equivalent entire
evolution equation. Their exact positive value at the fixed spectral
point \(s=-5i/2\) grows at the source rate. The full solenoidal
correspondence isolates the orthogonal swirl component carrying this
signal. The original quotient map and every kernel remain explicit;
its actual topology and relation-space invariance are not assumed.
\end{abstract}

\section{Provenance and reproducibility}
The Newman convention and its public source are Rodgers--Tao,
\cite[Section 1, equations (1)--(4)]{rodgersTao2020}.
The polynomial is Tao's displayed map
\cite[equation (1)]{tao2026jacobian}; its coordinate identities used here are
reproved above, so no hidden local reference is required to check them.
The actual topological Connes quotient is described in
\cite[Section 3.6, Proposition 3.6]{ccmProlate2024}.
Section~\ref{ccb:bridge} proves the coordinate-level Mellin/Fourier map,
the common-representative quotient correspondence with its complete
kernels, and its heat and multiplication intertwiners, retaining both
original topologies.

The readable proofs establish the analytic assertions. The scripts
\path{scripts/check_heat_connection.py} and
\path{scripts/zero_transport_check.py}, together with
\path{scripts/check_euclidean_observable.py}, replay exact algebraic
identities using rational polynomial arithmetic. Their JSON receipts
state precisely which identities were checked. They are not Lean
certificates or certificates of an off-critical zero. No Lean, Lake, or
Elan run was used. The work was prepared with Codex and independent
agent checks; the cited human literature remains the scholarly source.
The fourth-order transfer is also checked by
\path{scripts/check_xi4_transport.py}. The explicit physical, temporal,
spatial and zero-jet morphisms are also checked by
\path{scripts/check_xi4_typed_maps.py}. Appendix~\ref{app:xi4-source}
contains the complete supplied spectral-coefficient proof; the original
Markdown and its independent verification are preserved under
\path{sources/xi4_source/}. The coupling notation there is $\xi^4$.
The full arithmetic quotient continuation is preserved byte-for-byte
under \path{sources/connes_continuation/}; its full proof is included
in Appendices~\ref{sec:cq-source}--\ref{sec:cq-tests}.
Its \path{checks/verify_exact.py} supplies 206 supplementary exact
checks; the replay receipt is \path{checks/connes_continuation_replay.json}.
Section~\ref{ccb:continuation} proves the common-space identification
and its simultaneous moving quotient intertwiners.
The complete incoming Cartesian bilaplacian proof is preserved in
\path{sources/ns_bilaplacian/} and included in
Section~\ref{sec:ab-bilaplacian}. Its fourth-coefficient continuation
in Sections~\ref{x4b:material}--\ref{x4b:negative} proves the full
material intertwiner, residue map and negative-time asymptotic;
\path{scripts/check_xi4_bilaplacian.py} verifies the exact algebra.
Section~\ref{sec:s6q-three-points} retains the three actual marked
special points of the supplied period construction and proves its
real circle quotient, including the full logarithmic overlaps.
Sections~\ref{s6f:field}--\ref{s6f:localization} give the resulting
three-dimensional force, pressure, all mixed interactions, the exact
finite-mode classification and every spatial localization term.
The source provenance is retained under
\path{sources/s6_three_points/}; the independent algebra replays are
\path{scripts/check_s6_three_point_forcing.py} and
\path{scripts/check_s6_three_point_fields.py}.
The exact supplied-flow arithmetic correspondence is proved in
Section~\ref{sec:naf}; its source versions are identified in
\path{sources/ns_public/provenance.json}, and its independent algebra
checks are in \path{scripts/check_ns_arithmetic_flow.py}.
