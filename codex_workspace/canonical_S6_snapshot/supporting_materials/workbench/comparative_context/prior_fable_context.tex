\section{The prior Fable certificate: relevance and boundary}

The separate Erd\H{o}s--Strauss workspace contains a pinned copy of Jon
Poplett's JGPTech package for the polynomial map publicly attributed there to
Fable.  This is relevant as a standard of verification, not as evidence for
any step of the present $S^6$ construction.

Define $F=(F_1,F_2,F_3)\colon\mathbb C^3\to\mathbb C^3$ by
\begin{align*}
F_1(x,y,t)&=(1+xy)^3t+y^2(1+xy)(4+3xy),\\
F_2(x,y,t)&=y+3x(1+xy)^2t+3xy^2(4+3xy),\\
F_3(x,y,t)&=2x-3x^2y-x^3t.
\end{align*}

\begin{proposition}[replayed finite certificate]
\Verified\ Exact rational-polynomial arithmetic gives
\[
\det DF=-2.
\]
Moreover, the three distinct points
\[
\left(0,0,-\tfrac14\right),\qquad
\left(1,-\tfrac32,\tfrac{13}{2}\right),\qquad
\left(-1,\tfrac32,\tfrac{13}{2}\right)
\]
all map to $(-\tfrac14,0,0)$.  The local pinned checker
\Source{Jacobian/aceg.py} was replayed successfully on 25 August 2026.
Thus the displayed map itself has the advertised constant nonzero Jacobian
and noninjective fibre.
\end{proposition}

The transferable lesson is procedural.  The prior result terminates in a
small certificate---one determinant identity and one explicit collision---that
can be checked independently of its discovery narrative.  The present paper

\subsection{What transfers mathematically---and what does not}

No theorem or typed map from either the Jacobian counterexample or the
Erd\H{o}s--Strauss Jacobi-symbol calculations implies an $S^6$ lemma.  The two
earlier subjects must also be kept distinct: the former is a polynomial map
with constant Jacobian and an explicit collision; the latter is quadratic
character arithmetic.  Numerical ``three-fold'' similarities are not a
mathematical bridge.

What transfers is a precise audit architecture.  The Jacobian proof retains a
marked factorization before applying a forgetful map; after the continuous
gauge is removed, a discrete three-point fibre remains.  The $S^6$ analogue is
to retain a marked certificate containing the actual cellular or singular
comparison maps, primitive oriented bases, basepoint, whiskers, meridian
lifts, and translation data.  Forgetting this marking may retain the rank and
Smith form while losing exactly the sign or index that identifies the
geometric map.

Likewise, the earlier polynomial argument checks its degenerate boundary
separately rather than dividing across it.  Here the corresponding boundary
closure test is the non-simple-normal-crossings central fibre: the lifted cusp
retraction must satisfy its stated stratum conditions, phase spreading must be
continuous at stabilizers, and straightening must preserve the lattice
marking.  A rank computation after collapse cannot replace those checks.

This comparison sharpens the reviewer objections.  The relevant constructions
are not simply absent from the manuscript: Proposition~7.2 and
Lemmas~7.5--7.10 are the candidate proof of C1; Proposition~7.12 and
Appendix~B address C2--C3; Lemmas~7.4 and~7.16 address V1;
Lemmas~7.18--7.21 and Theorem~7.22 address MV1; and Proposition~7.27
addresses L1.  The subsequent source audit verifies all six bridges.  In
particular, it distinguishes the finite-band maps
$\alpha_q=(\pi_{1*},-\pi_{2*})$ from the second cusp attachment, constructs
both in primitive bases, and proves that their dual obstruction squares
commute with Leray.  No replacement such as a separate finite CW model is
needed.

The same ``lossy quotient has no converse'' warning is literal in Section~10:
the map $\Omega_W^1\to\widetilde\Omega_W^1=\Omega_W^1/\mathrm{torsion}$
forgets the conductor-supported class under dispute.  Therefore a vanishing
statement for the torsion-free quotient cannot be promoted to the ambient
restricted sheaf without a separate argument.

Concrete falsification certificates are correspondingly small: one boundary
stratum where equivariance or continuity fails; one primitive generator whose
actual image differs from the printed matrix; one clockwise meridian with a
hidden nonzero drift; or one specialization/base-change map with an omitted
index.  Conversely, checking each marked seam validates the corresponding
manuscript step and may refute the ChatGPT objection.
