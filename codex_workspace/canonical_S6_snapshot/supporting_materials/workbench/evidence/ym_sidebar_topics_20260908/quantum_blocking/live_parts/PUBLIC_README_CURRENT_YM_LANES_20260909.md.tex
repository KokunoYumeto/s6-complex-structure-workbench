\section{Yang--Mills current research lanes --- public reading
guide}\label{public-readme-current-ym-lanes-20260909-md-yangmills-current-research-lanes-public-reading-guide}

This release is the additive continuation of the Yang--Mills workbench.
It is intended to be read together with the complete frozen Overleaf
workbench at Zenodo DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22667605}{10.5281/zenodo.22667605}.
The frozen archive preserves the historical source tree and provenance
through commit \texttt{c501b11ff6caf6db195e5dc06b2b22724e886955}; the
present packet records later authored calculations and their exact
checkers.

The main reader is \texttt{quantum\_coarse\_graining\_79p.pdf}, with
cumulative source in
\texttt{sources/quantum/extensive\_quantum\_blocking.tex} and
\texttt{.md}. It retains the original SU(2) Hamiltonian, actual positive
vacuum, edge and face sums, coupling parameters, gauge constraints,
Schur reductions, memory terms, and all coordinate maps. Sections 19--23
connect the stipulated smooth positive-viscosity Navier--Stokes profile
to exact gauge curvature, the full sourced Yang--Mills current,
gauge-invariant curvature measures, and finite-regulator quantum state
maps. The quantum reader also states where weak-coupling diagonal
estimates stop and why they do not imply a fixed-renormalized-coupling
theorem.

The interacting tensor-band reader gives the complete finite-box
positive-coupling tensor inverse, raw state and energy corrections,
three symmetry channels, and the smooth forced-Navier--Stokes right
inverse. The non-Abelian vertex reader records the finite vertex
calculation. The root, volume-uniform and spatial readers contain the
complementary source proofs and regulator estimates. Every claimed
identity is accompanied by its source text, exact checker or certificate
where applicable, and explicit hypotheses.

The package includes TeX and Markdown sources, readable PDFs, retained
authored input proofs, exact checker programs and JSON results,
correction notes, source-lineage notes, and a machine-readable release
manifest. The ZIP itself is a current YM-lane packet; it is not a
replacement for the complete Overleaf archive and does not silently
include private logs, credentials, copied literature corpora or
unrelated Navier--Stokes operational material.

The mathematical endpoint remains open. The packet contains exact
finite-regulator maps and obstructions, including the fixed-box spatial
centered-mass expansion, but it does not claim a source-free interacting
four-dimensional Yang--Mills mass-gap contradiction. The formulas
preserve units, signs, domains, coupling and regulator dependence so
that later work can test the missing interaction-survival and
simultaneous-continuum steps directly.

For reproducibility, use the checker scripts under
\texttt{sources/quantum}, then inspect \texttt{BUILD\_RECEIPT.json} and
\texttt{PDF\_QA\_RECEIPT.json} for the cumulative quantum reader. Before
public deposition, regenerate the full current Overleaf archive, add
this guide and \texttt{PUBLIC\_PROVENANCE\_AND\_SCOPE\_20260909.md},
update all page-count/file-name entries, and record archive hashes and
the explicit frozen/current boundary.
