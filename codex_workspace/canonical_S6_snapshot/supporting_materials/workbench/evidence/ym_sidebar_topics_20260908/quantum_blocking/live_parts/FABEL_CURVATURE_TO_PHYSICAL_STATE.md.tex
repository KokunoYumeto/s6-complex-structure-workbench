\section{Fabel curvature to an actual physical
observable}\label{fabel-curvature-to-physical-state-md-fabel-curvature-to-an-actual-physical-observable}

The path-ordered link map gives a concrete observable map, so the
remaining question can be tested in the unchanged physical Hilbert space
rather than at the level of coordinates.

\subsection{Wilson-loop state map}\label{fabel-curvature-to-physical-state-md-wilson-loop-state-map}

For a fixed oriented lattice plaquette (p), let \[
U_p=U_{e_1}U_{e_2}U_{e_3}^{-1}U_{e_4}^{-1},\qquad W_p=\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}U_p.
\] Under (U\_e\mapsto h\_\{s(e)\}\^{}\{-1\}U\_eh\_\{t(e)\}), the
internal factors cancel and (U\_p\mapsto h\_\{v\}\^{}\{-1\}U\_ph\_v).
Cyclicity of the trace proves (W\_p) is gauge invariant, so
(P\_\{\mathcal G\}W\_p=W\_p) exactly.

Let (mu=psi\^{}2\lambda) be the actual positive vacuum measure and
define the unnormalized centered observable \[
v_p=W_p-\int W_p\,d\mu.
\] Then (v\_p\perp1) in (L\^{}2(\mu)), and the physical state in the
Schrödinger representation is (u\_p=\psi v\_p), with \[
\langle\psi,u_p\rangle_{L^2(\lambda)}=0,qquad
\|u_p\|_{L^2(\lambda)}^2=\int v_p^2,d\mu.
\] No state normalization or replacement of (mu) by product Haar is
made.

The exact conditional lower bound in the retained finite-box proof
implies (v\_p\ne0): vary one edge of (p) while holding all other links
fixed. Haar integration gives nonzero conditional variance of (W\_p),
and the actual positive density is bounded below by the proved
(alpha\textgreater0). Consequently (int v\_p\^{}2dmu\textgreater0) for
every positive coupling in the finite box.

\subsection{Relation to the Fabel
curvature}\label{fabel-curvature-to-physical-state-md-relation-to-the-fabel-curvature}

For the exact continuum link map
(U\_e{[}\mathcal A{]}=\mathcal P\exp(-\int\_e\mathcal A)), the
small-cell expansion is \[
U_p=I-a^2F_{ij}+O(a^3),
\] so the centered Wilson observable records the gauge-invariant
curvature through \[
2-W_p=a^4\,c(F_{ij},F_{ij})+O(a^5)
\] under the retained anti-Hermitian inner product
(c(X,Y)=-\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(XY)). On the Cartan Fabel encoding
this is the same (lambda\^{}2(\partial\_i u\_j-\partial\_j u\_i)\^{}2)
curvature component used in the current-to-lattice audit. The expansion
is local; no uniform remainder is asserted along a concentrating
sequence.

\subsection{Exact excitation lower bound in the proved
regime}\label{fabel-curvature-to-physical-state-md-exact-excitation-lower-bound-in-the-proved-regime}

Retain \[
H=\kappa\sum_eE_e+b\sum_p(2-W_p),\qquad
\kappa=\frac{2g^2}{a},\quad b=\frac1{2g^2a},\quad \xi=\frac1{4g^4}.
\] The physical-complement theorem already proved in this project gives,
for every physical mean-zero form-domain vector, in the explicit range
\[
0<\xi\le\frac1{49152}quad(g^4\ge12288),
\] \[
q_{H-E_0}[u]\ge\Delta_{\rm sc}\|u\|^2,
\qquad
\Delta_{\rm sc}=\kappa(3-512\xi)\ge\frac{287\kappa}{96}>0.
\] Applying this to (u\_p=\psi v\_p) gives the exact raw quotient bound
\[
\frac{q_{H-E_0}[u_p]}{\|u_p\|^2}\ge\Delta_{\rm sc}>0.
\] The same bound applies to every finite linear combination of centered
Wilson loops that remains nonzero and physical. Thus the direct
gauge-invariant observable induced by Fabel curvature cannot yield a
zero-gap sequence in this established coupling range.

\subsection{What remains outside this
result}\label{fabel-curvature-to-physical-state-md-what-remains-outside-this-result}

The inequality is finite-box and its coupling range is explicit. It does
not settle the simultaneous (a\downarrow0), volume-infinite, fixed-(g)
limit. Conversely, failure of this Wilson-loop map to soften does not
exclude another state map. A genuine counterexample still needs a
different explicitly constructed physical sequence whose unchanged raw
quotient tends to zero, together with its volume, spacing,
source-current and continuum-domain limits.
