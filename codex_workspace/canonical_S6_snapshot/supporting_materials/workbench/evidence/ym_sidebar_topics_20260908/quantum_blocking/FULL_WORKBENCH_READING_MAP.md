# How the mathematical workbench fits together

This guide describes the canonical Overleaf project and the source relationships that a complete archival edition should preserve. A version manifest identifies the exact files and revisions actually deposited. The first dedicated Yang–Mills checkpoint, [version DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22666541](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22666541), contains selected research manuscripts and their inputs. Its 84-file source ZIP is not a snapshot of the entire workbench. The [concept DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22666540) identifies the continuing record across versions.

The project began by reconstructing and testing a claimed complex structure on the six-dimensional sphere. It developed explicit coordinate, geometric, arithmetic and spectral constructions from that material. The Yang–Mills programme investigates whether those constructions, together with specified fluid profiles, can be carried through the original gauge-theory Hamiltonian into physical quantum states and a four-dimensional continuum theory. The archive should let a reader follow each claimed connection through its actual formulas and proofs, including the routes that failed or were corrected.

## Start with the original manuscript and the cumulative reconstruction

`main.tex` and `original_clean_transcription.tex` retain the original-manuscript side of the project. `workbench.tex` is the cumulative validate/repair/disprove reconstruction. `workbench_and_manuscript_overview.tex` gives the mathematical reading map and identifies the standalone companion manuscripts. Their supplied PDFs are readable entry points; their source trees are necessary to inspect or rebuild the work. A PDF's presence does not establish that it was rebuilt after every later source change, so the edition should state the source and build dates explicitly.

The directory `supporting_materials/workbench/research/` contains the shared mathematics used by the cumulative workbench and its companion manuscripts. `supporting_materials/workbench/evidence/` contains the authored calculations, corrections, proof inputs, and diagnostic material on which those manuscripts rely. The root bibliography and all referenced TeX, BibTeX, style, figure and other source inputs belong with this hierarchy. Moving only the final manuscripts into a ZIP breaks that source history and can break reproduction.

## Follow the Yang–Mills calculations in their mathematical order

The finite-box work starts with every original edge and face, the SU(2) gauge action, the full electric-plus-magnetic Wilson Hamiltonian, and its actual positive vacuum. The weak-coupling companion derives the original finite-box eigenvalue, state and derivative limits before calculating the radial spectral and observable maps. The volume-uniform estimates, temporal transfer, Wilson variation and spatial-continuum companions address their respective bounds and coordinate limits. Their full sources identify the parameter ranges in which each conclusion is proved.

The quantum-blocking manuscript then carries the full Fabel tensor into the original local-energy operator and physical states. Its later sections retain the complete tensor, the exact first-band image, local-energy and regular-time Navier–Stokes curvature maps, and all terms leaving the compressed state space. Section16 evaluates the complete positive-time vacuum two-creation spectrum, with an explicit original-lattice-to-momentum map and an actual positive-coupling sequence. Its common state representation is a free transverse-field representation.

Section17 now calculates the nonlinear terms of the original finite-box Hamiltonian in the same coordinates. Both the kinetic and magnetic cubic terms contribute to an explicitly certified nonzero physical three-creation channel. The actual vacuum changes at first order in the coupling, while parity makes the first-order local-energy covariance vanish. The complete second-order covariance retains the vacuum response, its centering and Gram factors, the quartic term and both cubic insertions through all reached intermediate modes. These finite-box coefficients make the next calculation concrete: determine how they behave when the physical volume, lattice spacing and coupling change together. A nonzero finite-box vertex and the earlier continuous low-energy tail do not themselves establish that interacting continuum limit.

The spatial manuscript relates the cusp, refinement and amplitude constructions to actual quantum states. Its newer local-band/radial correspondence specifies the maps between the different state coordinates and retains the original raw amplitudes. The supplied source versions should be read together: an older frozen spatial reader can cite an earlier quantum section even when both authors have since extended their manuscripts. An archive must identify those historical copies and supply the current cumulative versions, rather than silently treating all copies as the same edition.

Section18 now proves that the original full tensor weights reach the entire actual six-dimensional first physical band at sufficiently small positive coupling in each fixed box. It gives the exact inverse, carries the full interaction matrix into those tensor coordinates with every raw Gram and state correction retained, and computes the Fabel endpoint's nonzero contribution to all three symmetry channels. It also constructs a smooth incompressible velocity and its positive-viscosity forcing whose sampled curvature represents any prescribed real band state. This right inverse changes the force with the selected state and regulators; it does not identify that constructed regular flow with a stipulated singular Navier–Stokes profile. The interaction coefficients' signs and simultaneous-regulator behavior remain the next spectral calculation.

## Read provenance beside the claim it supports

Every imported mathematical source should have its title or original filename, author attribution when established, public source link or parent deposit, retained revision or edition date, and content hash. The archive should say which proof was actually read or replayed. A source announcement, an exported theorem statement, an exact algebra check and an independent full proof verification are different records of work performed; each should be described precisely.

The earlier [S6 publication family](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22235523) records the parent reconstruction and its disclosed source lineage. The dedicated Yang–Mills record should preserve that relationship alongside the original gauge-theory and analytical literature. Human research suggestions, AI-assisted derivations, subsequent corrections and supplied external feedback should be attributed as such. Discovery priority is not established merely by a local filename or the date of a later archive capture.

Correction notices are part of the mathematical record. In particular, the Fabel correction history retains changes to the weighted energy operator, transport convention, background-to-state dependence and scaling claims. Exact obstructions and unsuccessful constructions remain useful because they identify what was calculated and what a subsequent construction must change. They should travel with the corrected cumulative proof.

## What a complete frozen workbench edition must contain

The complete project snapshot should preserve the original relative hierarchy, all mathematical source inputs, supplied pertinent readers, reproducibility scripts and their recorded results, bibliography and public source relationships, correction history, and a manifest of every included file. It should identify the captured Overleaf revision and distinguish the sources from generated readers. The manifest and a successful ZIP round-trip establish file integrity; a separate comparison against the complete captured project inventory establishes completeness.

The original selected checkpoint remains a historical version. Updating the continuing record should add a complete current snapshot and revise this reading map to its actual contents. The pertinent current mathematical PDF should be the readable preview. Private account data, credentials, operational instructions and unrelated downloaded publication collections do not supply missing mathematical provenance; the archive should instead retain the complete authored proof and the exact public source references on which it relies.
