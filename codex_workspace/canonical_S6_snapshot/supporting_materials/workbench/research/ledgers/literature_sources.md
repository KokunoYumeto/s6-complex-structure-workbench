## 5 September ES–Niemeier source integration

- Cheng, Duncan and Harvey, *Umbral Moonshine and the Niemeier Lattices*,
  Research in the Mathematical Sciences 1 (2014), Article 3, equation (2.30)
  and Table 3 (arXiv p. 21), <https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.5793>.
  The abstract quotient `Aut(N)/W(A5^4 D4)=GL2(3)` is existing literature.
  The shared module proves its exact signed-code isomorphism and lattice
  section, exhausts all isometries as `w L_g`, and proves that the residue
  six-cycle belongs to the Weyl group and centralizes that chosen section.
- Mare and Willems, *Topology of the octonionic flag manifold*, Münster
  Journal of Mathematics 6 (2013), 483–523, Definition 2.2, Propositions
  2.4 and 2.7, equations (5.4)–(5.6),
  <https://www.uni-muenster.de/FB10/mjm/vol_6/mjm_vol_6_14.pdf>.
  Their flag and tangent modules are the primary representation source;
  the module proves the exact shortest-vector correspondence in the
  retained `v,s,c` marking. It does not equate the full lattice and tangent
  representation.
- The maintained residual note and Foundation transporter are cited as
  `esn:Residual2026` and `esn:ESMain2026`. Exact excerpts, original labels,
  snapshot hashes and the raw/complement-quotient correction are retained in
  `supporting_materials/workbench/research/ES_NIEMEIER_PROVENANCE.md`.
  The shared proof keeps actual prime, shell and sample data; its count
  counterexample is `A_3(2521)=0`, `A_3(3361)=6`, although both prime
  residues are one modulo 840. The full proof is
  `supporting_materials/workbench/research/es_niemeier_shared.tex`, including
  `esn:fa:thm:full-quotient` and `esn:fa:thm:residue-weyl`.
