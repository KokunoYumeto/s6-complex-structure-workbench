# Peer-verification monograph

`s6_peer_verification_monograph.tex` is the publication-focused primary
source for the Zenodo edition. It combines, in order:

1. a title and attribution page;
2. the complete mathematical orientation from the canonical root
   overview; and
3. the complete validate/repair/disprove paper, including its status ledger,
   proofs, literature audit, exact certificates, and bibliography.

The wrapper uses `docmute` to import the two maintained canonical documents;
it does not duplicate or fork their mathematical content. Compile it from the
canonical project root with pdfLaTeX. The four principal root TeX entry points
remain the public Overleaf navigation surface; the narrower root companion
drafts are indexed separately in `../project_documentation/PROJECT_README.md`.

The cumulative source also includes the [5 September two-cross exclusion
and residual certificate](../workbench/evidence/two_cross_exceptional_20260905/README.md).
It excludes the eight-dimensional spatial class whose two matrices exchange
the matter line and its orthogonal complement. The quantitative spatial
residual bound applies on that class with both scalar equations and Gauss
imposed, original coupling positive, and frequency nonzero; its uniform
version requires bounded spatial norm and coupling bounded away from zero.
The constrained domain is not asserted nonempty, existence outside the
class remains open, and no quantum mass-gap conclusion is made. Its initial
integration checkpoint precedes later typography corrections and final
artifact verification; the following PDF identities remain historical.
