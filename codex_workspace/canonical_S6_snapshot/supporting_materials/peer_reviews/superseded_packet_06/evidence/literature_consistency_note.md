# Literature consistency note: Campana-Demailly-Peternell obstruction

## Manuscript propositions used

The accessible manuscript states:

1. `Corollary 7.29`: the constructed compact complex threefold has the integral homology of `S^6` and top Chern number/euler number 2.
2. `Theorem 8.1`: the underlying smooth manifold is diffeomorphic to `S^6`.
3. `Theorem 9.1(i)`: `a(X)=1` and the holomorphic map `f:X->P^1` is the algebraic reduction.

The homology-sphere assertion implies `H^1(X;Z)=H^2(X;Z)=0`.

## Published result

Campana, Demailly, and Peternell (2020), Theorem 2.2, proves that a compact complex threefold with `H^1(X;Z)=H^2(X;Z)=0`, algebraic dimension one, and holomorphic algebraic reduction satisfies `c_3(X)<=0`. Their Corollary 2.3 concludes that a compact complex threefold homeomorphic to `S^6` has algebraic dimension zero.

APA reference:

Campana, F., Demailly, J.-P., & Peternell, T. (2020). The algebraic dimension of compact complex threefolds with vanishing second Betti number. *Compositio Mathematica, 156*(4), 679-696. https://doi.org/10.1112/S0010437X19007802

## Formal conflict

The manuscript's hypotheses instantiate the published theorem and yield `c_3(X)<=0`, while the manuscript also gives `c_3(X)=2`. Equivalently, CDP Corollary 2.3 gives `a(X)=0`, while the manuscript gives `a(X)=1`.

This is not resolved by stating that the manuscript's proof does not use CDP20. Logical independence of proofs does not remove incompatibility of conclusions. A valid response must identify an exact failed hypothesis or a precise error in the published proof.

## Scope limitation

The manuscript announces a discussion in Remark 9.9, but packet 07 and Remark 9.9 were not supplied. This review therefore cannot assess any attempted rebuttal. Until such a rebuttal is supplied and verified, the published theorem is a fatal objection.
