## Principal correction to the first audit

The first audit made equality of the intrinsic nearby-cycle specialization map with the explicit collapse pullback appear necessary for every topology proof. The canonical source distinguishes them:

- `sp^q` is the stalk map from central-fibre cohomology to local monodromy invariants and is proved by nearby cycles in Appendix B.
- `c^*` comes from the explicit collapse map of Section 7.2.
- Their equality and degree-two normalizations belong to the first collapse/Mayer-Vietoris route.
- Section 7.7 uses `sp^q`, not `c^*`.

This correction is the reason the old R1-R4 block was replaced by route-specific risks C1, C2, B1, V1, L1, and MV1.
