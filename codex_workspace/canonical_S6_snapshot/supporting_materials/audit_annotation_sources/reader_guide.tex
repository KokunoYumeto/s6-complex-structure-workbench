The manuscript claims a compact complex threefold $X$ with $f:X\to\mathbb P^1$, general fibres complex two-tori, special fibres $W,3S_1,4S_2$, and
$\pi_1(X)=1$, $H_*(X;\mathbb Z)\cong H_*(S^6;\mathbb Z)$, $X\cong_{\rm diff}S^6$; it further claims $a(X)=1$ and $c_3(X)=2$.
\textbf{Both Pro audits recommend major revision: neither found a fatal internal contradiction in the complete manuscript, but neither certifies the main theorem.}
\end{minipage}}

\vspace{3pt}
{\fontsize{7.45pt}{8.45pt}\selectfont
\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{0.492\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\GuideHead{Pro review A: the CDP obstruction and Section 10}
Campana--Demailly--Peternell print $c_3(X)\leq0$ under $H^1(X;\mathbb Z)=H^2(X;\mathbb Z)=0$ with a holomorphic algebraic reduction, and $a(X)=0$ for a compact complex threefold homeomorphic to $S^6$. The manuscript has $c_3(X)=2$ and $a(X)=1$, so both sets of conclusions cannot simultaneously be correct.

Section~10 gives two concrete proposed failure mechanisms. At the nonnormal fibre $W$, the conductor construction produces a nonzero ambient cotangent section
\[
s\in H^0\!\left(W,\Omega_X^1|_W\otimes A\right)
\]
whose image is conductor-supported torsion and hence zero in $\widetilde\Omega_W^1$. Therefore
\[
H^0(W,\widetilde\Omega_W^1\otimes A)=0
\quad\not\!\Longrightarrow\quad
H^0(W,\Omega_X^1|_W\otimes A)=0.
\]
Separately, for $1\to K\to G\to Q\to1$, the relevant abelianization is
\[
G^{\rm ab}\cong Q^{\rm ab}\oplus(K^{\rm ab})_Q;
\]
in this fibration the monodromy coinvariant rank is one, not the fibre value $b_1=4$.

Review A found no local-algebra error in either mechanism, withdrew the packet-only CDP rejection, and judged Section~10 coherent but not externally adjudicated. Exact analytic duality and top-degree base-change hypotheses at $W$ and the multiple fibres remain to be stated and independently checked. In particular, the proof must distinguish fibrewise, ambient-restricted and reflexive differentials from the relative dualizing sheaf and show that the conductor class survives the required duality/base-change maps.

\GuideHead{Definite local errors}
Remark~7.28 is false for a fixed triple: $(100,1,-1)$ has the listed residues but
$p=12(100)-4(1)-3(-1)=1199\ne\pm1$; the correct assertion is existential in $\ell_0$. Proposition~4.7(iv) cannot justify closedness by saying that the section is ``proper over the open disc''; it needs a closed-graph/Hausdorff argument. These defects are repairable and do not decide the main theorem.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.478\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\GuideHead{Pro review B: exact algebra versus geometric topology}
Exact integer arithmetic verifies the printed monodromy and lattice algebra, including
\[
T_1^3=T_2^4=I,\quad T_0=I+N,\quad N^2=0,
\quad\operatorname{rk}N=2,\quad A_1A_2M_0=I,
\]
the invariant lattices and rank-one alternating form, the Smith forms, the selected twists $(\ell_0,\ell_1,\ell_2)=(0,1,-1)$, and
\[
p=12\ell_0-4\ell_1-3\ell_2=-1.
\]
Thus the \emph{displayed} group presentation is trivial and the printed homology matrices have the claimed Smith forms. What is not certified is that these are the maps induced by the actual geometry.

A complete proof must establish all four of the following.
\GuideTightList
\begin{itemize}
\item Continuity and $\bar\Lambda$-equivariance of the cusp collapse across every double and triple toric stratum, with quotient and chain-level compatibility.
\item Surjectivity and the integral identity
\[
\ker\!\left(c_*:H_q(F;\mathbb Z)\to H_q(W;\mathbb Z)\right)
=(\bigwedge^qM_0-I)\bigwedge^q\Lambda,
\]
including torsion, saturation, orientations, bases, and equality of the nearby-cycle edge map with the geometric collapse map.
\item That the actual based overlap inclusions induce exactly $A_1,A_2,M_0$ and drifts $-v_1,-v_2,\ell_0$, with the printed meridian signs and primitive classes. A sign or primitivity error changes $p$.
\item That the displayed Mayer--Vietoris and Leray matrices are the actual geometric boundary maps. Their agreement is not independent validation because both reuse the decisive specialization and monodromy inputs.
\end{itemize}

Hence the implication
\[
X\stackrel{?}{\Longrightarrow}
\pi_1(X)=0,\qquad H_*(X;\mathbb Z)\cong H_*(S^6;\mathbb Z)
\]
remains open in this audit. Once those inputs hold, the final recognition
\[
\text{simply connected integral homology }6\text{-sphere}
\Longrightarrow X\cong_{\rm diff}S^6
\]
is sound. The downstream identities $c_1=c_2=0$, $\int_Xc_3=2$, $\chi(\mathcal O_X)=0$, and $\chi(T_X)=1$ are mutually consistent under that topology.
\end{minipage}

Review A's Section~10 analytic mechanism and Review B's integral-topology obligations are distinct and both must be settled. Review A's 28 and Review B's 38 passing checks overlap substantially and are not 66 independent checks.
