Status and logical scope. All statements involving the global S 6 fibration use the unpublished construction in Alpöge (2026). Its topology, singular-fibre maps, and direct-image
argument have been reconstructed and separately verified in the accompanying proof-verification
workbench; this standalone note imports that geometric package as stated input instead of
reproducing those proofs. A contemporaneous downstream paper treats that construction as
an assumption rather than supplying an independent proof (Liang et al., 2026). The linear
algebra, Fourier spectra, cusp estimates, and direct-integral argument below are independent
deductions from the displayed period and cusp formulae. The regular-locus quotient theorem
additionally uses the asserted integral monodromy matrices, which are restated and checked
below; its nonextension statement additionally uses the asserted toric cusp chart, and is only
a nonextension result for the literal fixed product-circle target with the stated regular-fibre
map. The quotient map is real. Its radial circumference and pointed-limit statements use
the separately chosen Euclidean metrics on marked cusp fibres; no deck-invariant global fibre
metric is asserted. It is neither the holomorphic first-coordinate quotient nor the separate
degree-M 2 cover construction. The counting theorem and the arbitrary-flux magnetic theorem
are likewise statements along the marked cusp. A general alternating form E is fixed only in the
marked lattice; a global magnetic line bundle over the regular family would require additional
descent data. Separately, the displayed class η = u ∧ w + 6γ ∧ δ is proved invariant under the
asserted monodromy generators and is realized by an explicit factor of automorphy on every
marked fibre. The radial direct-integral theorem uses Lebesgue measure in logarithmic height
and a multiplication operator with the exact maximal domain; it includes no radial derivative.
The local toric Kummer theorem included below is proved for a completed cusp disc. It uses
the modified source factor exp(2πiC(v N )λ̄/N ) and distinguishes the degree-N 3 map to the
original cusp family from the generic degree-N 2 relative map to the base-changed target. It
supplies no full-base cover. The separate cone targets, simultaneous-root construction, and
Seifert filling proofs in the canonical workbench are not imported or claimed here. This paper
does prove statements about scalar and magnetic fibre Laplacians, their specified decomposable
radial multiplication operators, classical Yang–Mills Hessians at the product and explicit
reducible split backgrounds, an honest compact-link flux configuration, and explicitly defined
finite cochain and Gaussian models. It constructs neither nonlinear quantum Yang–Mills
theory nor a four-dimensional physical Hamiltonian, and makes no physical mass-gap claim.

integration over a noncompact degenerating modulus without a uniform coercive estimate.
Conversely, a gapless decomposable vertical operator or classical Hessian cannot be identified
with a field-theoretic Hamiltonian without the explicit operator-level bridge. Likewise, the
native T2 × R2 limit, finite-cover R4 unspooling, fixed-target quotient limits, and cone-target
constructions are four different operations. The fixed-target obstruction concerns only the
displayed character map to (R/Z)4 with its stated fibre restriction; the cone constructions
change the codomain and distinguish intrinsic expanding slice metrics from collapse in a fixed
compact ambient metric.
