# Public reading map — 9 September 2026

This Overleaf is the evolving mathematical workspace. The original reconstruction and historical annotations remain here; the topic collections below provide readable, dated papers with their matching editable sources and check records.

## Find the relevant work

- **Yang–Mills:** [five readers and complete selected sources](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench/tree/main/yang-mills). Start with the 79-page quantum coarse-graining reader; companions cover interacting tensor bands, non-Abelian vertices, volume-uniform vacuum estimates and spatial-continuum maps. [Archived edition 22678364](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22678364).
- **S6 topology and related constructions:** [five-page key advances and complete frozen project guide](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench/tree/main/s6). The full archive preserves the project frozen on 6 September, including the original transcription, historical reviews, later workbench and topic drafts. [Archived edition 22678442](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22678442).
- **Navier–Stokes:** [corrected 208-page reconstruction, standalone LaTeX and records](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench/tree/main/navier-stokes). [Corrected edition 22678406](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22678406); the earlier 162-page edition remains historical.

The [workbench overview](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench/blob/main/WORKBENCH.md) explains what each programme tried, why, what its released documents contain and what remains unfinished. The papers carry the full definitions, calculations, hypotheses and proofs. Navigation and file hashes do not add mathematical verification marks.

## Read or contribute from another workbench

The [PolyClank documentation](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench/tree/main/docs/polyclank) describes independently maintained workbenches sharing complete research and version-specific evidence. A contribution can be one calculation or an entire programme. A handoff describes what happened; it does not dictate the next researcher's mathematical direction.

Use the [contribution guide](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) and [scoped review guide](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench/blob/main/REVIEW_GUIDE.md) to identify the particular statement, source revision, checking method, evidence and exclusions. Original coordinates, constants, signs, parameters, hypotheses, domains and codomains remain explicit.

The [machine-readable catalogue](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench/blob/main/workbench.json) supplies artifact identities, byte counts and SHA-256 fingerprints. It is an experimental static catalogue, not a deployed peer network or a theorem-verification service.

## Which version am I reading?

A version-specific DOI identifies a dated archive. This Overleaf may contain later work and does not automatically revise that archive. Git history preserves source revisions; the [release correspondence](https://github.com/KokunoYumeto/yang-mills-interacting-workbench/tree/main/releases/2026-09-09) links the topic editions. Older annotations are retained as provenance rather than silently rewritten as new conclusions.

This reading-map addition changes navigation only. It does not replace mathematical text, alter sharing, or claim completion of the open research targets.
