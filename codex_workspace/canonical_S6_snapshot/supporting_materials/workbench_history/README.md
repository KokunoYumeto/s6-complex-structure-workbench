# Workbench and project history

This folder preserves unique, non-authoritative material from the earlier S6
Overleaf and workbench trees. It exists so consolidation does not erase the
development record. The live mathematical authority is
`workbench.tex` with its `supporting_materials/workbench/research/` inputs; the public reading
authority is root `main.tex`.

- `18_page_branch_2026-08-26/` preserves the shorter conservative fork and
  its latest 18-page PDF. Its topology labels are superseded by the complete
  60-page workbench, but its independent Pic0 argument was integrated.
- `former_workbench_checkpoint_56_pages/` preserves the former standalone
  workbench at migration time and its compiled migration notice.
- `early_workbench_2026-08-25/` preserves unique source/ledger drafts from the
  first repair notebook.
- `public_121_page_checkpoint_2026-08-24/` and
  `workflow_record_2026-08-24/` preserve unique files from the earlier public
  release workflow.
- `preconsolidation_conflicts/` preserves source files whose old paths
  collided with newer files but whose bytes differed.

Generated auxiliary files, bulk page-render PNGs, and byte-identical copies
were not duplicated. Their treatment is recorded in `PROJECT_MERGE_MAP.md`.
Machine-specific paths were removed from this public history without altering
the mathematical content.
