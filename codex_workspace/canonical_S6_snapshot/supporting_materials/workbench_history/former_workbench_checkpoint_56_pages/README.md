This Overleaf project is a publication-facing mathematical workbench for a
reconstructed manuscript claiming a compact complex threefold diffeomorphic
to S6. It tests both the manuscript and the objections raised in independent
audits; neither side is treated as authority.

## Read in this order

1. main.tex — the compiled validate/repair/disprove paper.
2. research/mathematical_summary.tex — a two-page mathematical result map.
3. research/candidate_geometry.tex — period family, cusp quotient, finite
   fillings, and compact global gluing.
4. research/exact_algebra.tex — complete integral matrix, lattice,
   finite-quotient, presentation, and saturation proofs.
5. research/integral_leray.tex — nearby cycles, Leray transgressions,
   fundamental group, and sphere recognition.
6. research/algebraic_dimension.tex — Neron--Severi, Appell--Humbert, and
   meromorphic-descent proof of a(X)=1.
7. research/analytic_picard.tex — structure-sheaf direct images, degree-one
   base change, Pic(X)=C, exact finite normal characters, and the verified
   Proposition 10.8 uniform non-torsion theorem.
8. research/topology_reduction.tex — remaining obligations in the independent
   collapse/Mayer--Vietoris route.
9. research/cdp_program.tex — the conductor construction, explicit proper
   counterexample to the CDP p. 692 inference, Cartier duality, flatness,
   analytic base change, the CDP98 monodromy defect, and theorem-level
   disposition.
10. research/references.tex — conventional, publicly resolvable bibliography.
11. audit_baseline/annotated_audit.tex — the original annotated audit, with a
    corrective addendum but no silent alteration of the original body.
12. audit_baseline/clean_transcription.tex — frozen 108-page transcription.

VERIFIED means that the immediately following assertion has a proof or an
explicit hand-checkable certificate. CONDITIONAL means a proved implication
whose unverified hypotheses are named. OPEN means no complete proof or
counterexample is recorded. REFUTED means the exact assertion has a complete
contradiction or counterexample.

## Build

Select root main.tex and pdfLaTeX. Build verification and the current page
count are recorded after every mathematical revision; no release is made
with fatal errors, undefined citations or references, or box warnings.

The clean transcription under audit_baseline/ is frozen. The annotated audit
preserves its original body and may receive dated correction pages as the
mathematics changes. Active proofs belong under research/. Third-party
literature is cited, not bundled. All compiled references are resolvable by a
reader without access to the editor's computer.
