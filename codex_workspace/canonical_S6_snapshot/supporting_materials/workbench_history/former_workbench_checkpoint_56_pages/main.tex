\begin{abstract}
This paper validates, repairs, or attempts to disprove a reconstructed
manuscript claiming a compact complex threefold diffeomorphic to $S^6$ and
of algebraic dimension one.  In its present form the workbench gives
complete proofs of the period-family descent, toric and finite fillings,
global analytic gluing, integral nearby-cycle specialization, the global
Leray page and three primitive transgressions, the based van Kampen
presentation, sphere recognition, and the very-general-fibre
N\'eron--Severi and meromorphic-descent calculation.  It therefore verifies
for the constructed threefold
\[
X\cong_{\mathrm{diff}}S^6,\qquad
\langle c_3(T_X),[X]\rangle=2,\qquad
\mathcal M(X)=f^*\mathbb C(t),\qquad a(X)=1.
\]
The same verified object satisfies the stated hypotheses of
Campana--Demailly--Peternell Theorem~2.2 and contradicts its conclusions, so
the workbench records a counterexample to that theorem as stated as well as
two exact defects in its printed dependency chain.  The separate
cellular-collapse/Mayer--Vietoris comparison remains open as an alternate
certificate.  The candidate's Proposition~10.8 is now also verified: the
structure-sheaf direct images, degree-one base change, Picard line, exact
finite normal characters, and uniform period/countability argument are
reconstructed in full without using that open collapse route.
Reviewer~B independently reviewed the reconstruction existing up to its
evidentiary cutoff and provisionally agreed on the non-collapse route; the
later Codex proofs here are separate.  Throughout, \Verified\ means that a
complete auditable proof is written in this document, not that specialist
replication or community acceptance has already occurred.
\end{abstract}
