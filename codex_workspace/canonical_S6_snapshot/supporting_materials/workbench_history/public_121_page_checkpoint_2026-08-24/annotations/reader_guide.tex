\textbf{Integrated determination: major revision; the central theorem is not presently certifiable.}
The completed manuscript has not been decisively refuted by these audits, but its claimed complex structure on S6 is not established. The present status is \textbf{unconfirmed}: neither accepted as proved nor classified as disproved.
\end{minipage}}

\vspace{4pt}
{\fontsize{8.15pt}{9.55pt}\selectfont
The earlier packet-only rejection based on CDP20 is superseded. Section~10 gives a substantive proposed answer to that obstruction, but it has not received external specialist adjudication. Finding a plausible gap in a published proof is not by itself proof that the published theorem is false.

\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\GuideHead{Pro review A - full-manuscript referee report}
Recommends major revision and specialist replication. Its 17-page report records 28 passing exact checks, withdraws the packet-only rejection, and treats the conductor-supported Section~10 mechanism as technically credible but unresolved externally. It emphasizes cusp collapse, integral specialization, analytic base change and duality at singular fibres, and the literature challenge.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.485\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\GuideHead{Pro review B - independent proof audit}
Also concludes \textit{major revision - main theorem not certified}. Its 6-page report and 92-row theorem ledger accompany 38 passing finite checks. The downstream matrix algebra works after the printed geometric maps are granted; the audit does not certify that those matrices are the maps induced by the actual construction.
\end{minipage}

\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{0.405\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\GuideHead{What the exact evidence supports}
\GuideTightList
\begin{itemize}
\item monodromy orders, products, invariant and coinvariant lattices;
\item determinants, Smith forms, exterior-power ranks and saturation claims;
\item conditional fundamental-group and homology matrix reductions;
\item printed period identities, intersection formulae, and Gram determinant.
\end{itemize}
The A and B harnesses overlap substantially: 28 plus 38 must \textbf{not} be reported as 66 independent checks.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.565\textwidth}
\vspace{0pt}
\GuideHead{What still blocks certification}
The proof needs an independent, integral geometric derivation of all four load-bearing inputs:
\GuideTightList
\begin{itemize}
\item the global equivariant cusp-collapse model;
\item the specialization map, including torsion, saturation, bases, signs, and orientations;
\item the based van Kampen inclusions, translation drifts, primitivity, and meridian conventions;
\item the identification of the displayed Mayer--Vietoris/Leray matrices with the actual geometric boundary maps.
\end{itemize}
\end{minipage}

\GuideHead{Reviewer C / Codex integration and confirmed local defects}
Codex reconciled both Pro reviews, checked the TeX and exact harnesses, and retained a separate issue ledger. Confirmed repairable findings are: p.~30, Proposition~4.7(iv), an incorrect justification of closedness; p.~63, Remark~7.28, a false fixed-triple biconditional needing an existential quantifier; source p.~71, a modular-build off-page text defect; p.~98, Lemma~A.3, cohomology mislabeled as homology with reversed variance; and p.~100, Lambda where bar-Lambda is intended. None alone resolves the central theorem.
