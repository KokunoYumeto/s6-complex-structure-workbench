\title{The Marked Quaternionic Continuation\\
\large Exact reconciliation, positive fibres, and a dynamical completion}
\author{Codex verification and continuation of two supplied Pro-mode constructions}
\date{5 September 2026 --- research draft}

\begin{abstract}
We identify two proposed $4+4+4+1$ continuations as the same embedded
subspace of $\operatorname{Herm}_3(\mathbb H)$, with explicit mutually inverse
coordinates preserving the original observation matrix. Their common
positive cone contains the old complex-Hermitian cone and yields the sphere
pair $(S^{12},S^8)$. We compute the Jordan product with the retained
nonunitary marking and prove its fifteen-dimensional closure. In the
full positive carrier a split observation to $\operatorname{Herm}_2(\mathbb C)$
gives the exact bound $\det G\geq|\rho|^2$, with every permitted pair
realized. An explicit spectral-floor map transfers this observation to
trace-free scalar configurations without identifying them with positive matrices.
In the
supplied scalar dynamics, however, the omitted complex coordinate is
generically sourced: explicit initial data give $\ddot\rho=-2\kappa$.
We also construct global nonzero invariant sectors of the unrestricted
equations: for fixed complex $W\subset u^\perp$, retain $b\in W$ and
$\Phi W\subset W$, with tangent velocities. Their configuration dimensions
are six and eight; no reaction force is needed. A fourth-order escape
example shows why low-order tangency tests alone do not prove invariance.
An exact periodic moving-kernel family then proves that these fixed-subspace
sectors are not exhaustive. Its conserved colour moment identifies the
nonzero Gauss residual of the zero-connection gauge lift. We prove that
no homogeneous spatial connection in temporal gauge repairs this literal
orbit, and derive the exact Gauss-inertia equation for arbitrary temporal
connection. A separate two-variable interacting sector solves every
gauge and scalar equation with zero connection. We also verify
the supplied scalar vacuum transition and its full metric Hessian, with
one radial, eight tangent, and five normal directions. Averaging the
marked Lorentz determinant over the colour group gives
$\operatorname{tr}\operatorname{adj}(A)$; on the positive rank-one
boundary this is $\|b\|^2$, a variable which ranges continuously along
gauge-inequivalent scalar minima. Finally, the retained rank-six period
map has complete positive metric fibres of dimensions twelve over $H$
and seventeen over $G$. An explicit monodromy-compatible metric produces
rank loss, whereas bounded relative metric condition number excludes it.
We derive the exact constrained dynamics as a separate model, and correct
the right-action convention and the anti-Hermitian gauge kinetic sign.
This is a finite-dimensional geometric and dynamical audit, not a
continuum quantum-field-theory mass-gap theorem.
We further prove all-time real-connection and static-frame complex
obstructions to the original rotating orbit. A different three-coordinate
sector supplies exact current-free null waves and periodic full-gauge
solutions with frequency tending to zero and energy tending to the vacuum
value at fixed spatial volume. Their amplitude does not tend to zero.
The complete eight-branch transverse gauge spectrum includes
$\mu_{\rm d}^2=(2g^2/3)\|b\|^2$, detected by an explicitly conserved
gauge-invariant vector current. Thus this branch is proportional to the
colour-averaged Lorentz determinant. Finally, $W$ is geometrically a
lattice cometric: Hermitian compatibility gives $h_3=2H_3^{-1}$.
The unchanged native Euclidean periods give constant $H_3=2I_3$ and
$G=2PP^*$ while their source and induced quotient Laplace gaps are
asymptotic to $4\pi^2/y^2$ and $8\pi^2/y^2$, respectively.
For the original rotating scalar orbit we also exclude a genuinely
complex rotating-pair gauge class, including its constant spatial
rotations. In the unrestricted rotating-pair equations, Gauss gives the
positive inverse $T=4(g^2\mathcal I+2\mathcal K)^{-1}[Z_1,Z_2]$,
$C=\omega T$, and the scalar equation fixes
$\omega^2=(1/4+\sum_j\|Z_jq\|^2)/\|Tq\|^2$.
We prove a two-sign bijection with the common zero set of four full
matrix/vector residuals in all sixteen spatial coordinates and $g^2$.
Existence or exclusion of that zero set remains open.
The unrestricted scalar equations also force the stabilizer coefficients
$s_X=-i\operatorname{tr}(HX)$ to satisfy
$s_C^2<s_{Z_1}^2+s_{Z_2}^2$ unless all vanish. Both spatial equations
give a positive two-by-two stress matrix with an explicit sum-of-squares
discriminant; these exact tests retain all colour coordinates.

For each fixed unrestricted spatial pair, we now prove an explicit
bounded coupling interval and a nonnegative residual polynomial of
degree exactly twenty-eight whenever its domain polynomial is nonzero.
Its admissible positive roots are precisely the allowed original $g^2$:
there are at most fourteen, with both frequency signs reconstructed.
Three vector tests decide the identically empty Gauss domain.
A nonzero, explicitly defined $24\times24$ Sylvester determinant
polynomial further confines the entire spatial solution projection to
a closed, nowhere-dense, measure-zero subset of the sixteen-coordinate
carrier. An exact unrestricted witness and rational and finite-field
Bezout certificates prove that polynomial is nonzero. The eight-dimensional linear class in which both spatial matrices
exchange the matter line with its orthogonal complement lies inside this
exceptional set, but a full-equation positivity identity excludes every
positive coupling and both frequency signs there. A denominator-free
residual identity also gives a strictly positive spatial-error bound on
its scalar--Gauss zero set, uniform for bounded spatial norm and coupling
bounded away from zero. This scalar--Gauss zero set is now completely
parameterized: the classified domain is
$\mathcal D\times\{1,-1\}^2\times U(1)^2$, and the literal domain
retains the frame phase subject only to
$(\zeta,p_+,p_-)\sim(-\zeta,-p_+,-p_-)$.
An explicit family attains every original $t=g^2>0$, with all scalar
and Gauss entries zero. The two spatial residuals cannot vanish simultaneously, and
existence on the remaining exceptional set is open.

The literal inverse charts further give the diffeomorphism
$\mathcal S_{\rm sg}\cong\{1,-1\}^2\times(0,\infty)
\times\mathbb R^2\times\mathbb T^3$ with $t=g^2$ unchanged.
Its four connected components are embedded and six-dimensional.
At every fixed positive coupling, each of the four components is
$\mathbb R^2\times\mathbb T^3$, of dimension five
(Corollary~\ref{sgd:smooth-addon:levels}). This smooth classification
imposes no spatial equation and gives no quantum spectral conclusion.

\end{abstract}
