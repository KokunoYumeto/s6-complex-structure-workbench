\begin{abstract}
We verify the complete oriented divisor-channel map, the diagonal-ray
certificate count, and its classical density-one argument. The integral
B\'ezout opening is retained with its precise positivity hypothesis and
explicit integral presentation maps. The supplied orthogonal map to the
defect-one Koide surface has no rational image point. We give a different
rational map, its inverse on specified open sets, and its exact denominator
formula. This extends the arithmetic comparison without asserting a global
threefold realization, a particle model, or a proof of Erd\H{o}s--Straus.
For $p=1201$, the complete fifteen-state selector census maps to fifteen
distinct projective rays in the upper-left real symmetric corner of
$\operatorname{Herm}_3(\mathbb H)$. Their linear span and Jordan closure
both have dimension three. Exact counts $15,54,36,29$ at four primes in
the same residue class show that fifteen is not a residue-class invariant.
\end{abstract}
